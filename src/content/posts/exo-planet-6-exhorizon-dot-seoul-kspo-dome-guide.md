EXO is back at **KSPO DOME in Olympic Park** this weekend for **EXO PLANET #6 – EXhOrizon [dot]**, three shows from **November 6 to 8, 2026**. This guide covers what a visitor needs on the day: the official show details, ticket pickup rules, seat warnings from the ticket page, and a return plan for the late subway.

Show details, prices and ticket rules come from the official Melon Ticket listing, read on November 5, 2026. The organizers are SM Entertainment and Dream Maker Entertainment Limited. Rules can change, so reread the listing before you go.

## Show Details From Melon Ticket

| | Day 1 | Day 2 | Day 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Date | Fri, Nov 6 | Sat, Nov 7 | Sun, Nov 8 |
| Start (KST) | 19:00 | 18:00 | 17:00 |

- **Venue:** KSPO DOME, Olympic Park, Songpa-gu, Seoul.
- **Prices:** VIP (standing or seated) KRW 198,000; general (standing or seated) KRW 165,000.
- **Age:** 8 and up. The listing does not state a running time.
- **Questions:** Melon Ticket customer center, 1899-0042 (09:00 to 18:00, per the notice).

## Ticket Pickup: The Part That Trips People Up

The listing is strict about this, and it matters more than anything else on the day.

- **Batch delivery** was for early orders. Orders completed by **October 12 at 18:00** were shipped from October 16. Anything booked after that is **on-site pickup only**.
- **Bring both:** a **physical ID** that proves you are the booker (copies and photos are not accepted) and the **Melon Ticket booking confirmation**. The details on the two must match.
- **Only the booker can collect.** A friend cannot pick up for you. One person can collect up to **2 tickets**, family-name bookings included.
- **Per-person limit:** the notice sets a 2-ticket limit per person per show, and says bookings over the limit under the same name can be cancelled without notice, with no pickup or entry.
- **A ticket cannot be reissued** if it is lost or damaged, so treat it carefully.

If you booked through the global page as a visitor, the notice says the domestic and global pages have separate schedules and procedures. Check the page you booked on, and confirm with Melon Ticket support which ID they accept for a foreign-name booking, since the notice only says "physical ID".

## Seat Notes From the Notice

Before you decide how early to arrive, check your section against these warnings on the ticket page:

- **Sections 1, 15, 24 and 43** and some other seats can have view limits from structures, equipment, cameras, stage production and the heights of people nearby.
- **Rows 14 and 15 in sections 26, 27, 40 and 41** have tight spacing and narrow aisles.
- **First-floor rows 1 to 11** are not sold because the floor stage takes up that space.
- None of these are grounds for a refund or change on show day.

## Payment, Cancellation and Refunds

- **Cancellation deadline:** 17:00 the previous weekday for Friday and Saturday shows; 11:00 on Saturday for the Sunday show.
- **Fees** (per Melon Ticket): none within 7 days of booking; up to KRW 4,000 per ticket (10% cap) from day 8 until 10 days before; then 10% (9 to 7 days), 20% (6 to 3 days) and 30% (2 to 1 days) before the show. The booking fee is refundable only if you cancel before midnight on the booking day.
- **Booking window:** open until 8 hours before the show, but late bookings cannot be cancelled.
- **Cancelled-seat reopening:** the organizer cancelled bookings it said were made by abnormal means and reopened those seats on September 16 for domestic card holders only. That sale has ended, and the notice's card restriction applied to it.

## Getting to KSPO DOME and Back

The venue transit facts are the same as for every KSPO show, and our other guides already cover them from official operator sources.

- **Arrival.** The operator directs subway passengers to **Olympic Park Station (Lines 5 and 9), Exit 3**. Follow the promoter's gate map inside the park, because the station is an arrival point, not proof you are at the right entrance. Details are in the [concert-day venue and transport guide](/posts/kpop-concert-day-venue-transport-guide-2026/).
- **Return.** Friday starts at 19:00 and ends late. Screenshot the route home for your hotel before you enter, add time for merchandise and restroom queues, and use the [KSPO Dome concert guide](/posts/kspo-dome-concert-survival-guide/) for lockers, food timing and meeting points.
- **Driving.** Do not plan on parking. If you must drive, the operator's parking facts are in the venue guide, and an organizer can impose different controls on show days.
- **Backup.** Keep a taxi app and your hotel name in Korean ready in case you miss the train you planned.
