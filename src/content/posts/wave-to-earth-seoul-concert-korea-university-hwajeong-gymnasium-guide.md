wave to earth, the Korean indie-pop trio, closes the Korea leg of its world tour with three nights at **Korea University's Hwajeong Gymnasium** in Seongbuk-gu, Seoul, **December 18 to 20, 2026**. Tickets sold out quickly, so this guide is most useful if you already have one, are watching for released seats, or are planning a December trip around the dates.

The show details, prices and booking rules below come from the official Melon Ticket listing, read on November 5, 2026. The university's own site could not be opened from our research tools, so directions stay at station level with "check this" steps rather than exact walking times.

## Show Details From Melon Ticket

| | Day 1 | Day 2 | Day 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Date | Fri, Dec 18 | Sat, Dec 19 | Sun, Dec 20 |
| Start (KST) | 20:00 | 18:00 | 17:00 |

- **Venue:** Korea University Hwajeong Gymnasium (고려대학교 화정체육관).
- **Prices:** standing KRW 132,000; reserved seating KRW 143,000.
- **Running time:** 120 minutes. **Age:** 11 and up.
- **Organizer and seller:** Wavy International and NOL Universe; tickets via Melon Ticket (also NOL).
- **Ticket support:** Melon Ticket lists 1899-0042 for booking questions.

Because the first night starts at 20:00 on a Friday, you will leave late. Plan the return before you plan dinner.

## Booking, Payment and Cancellation

- **Availability.** Korean media reported the original Dec 19–20 nights sold out at the first on-sale and the added Dec 18 night sold out within minutes. Treat any seat you see now as returned inventory and check the live page.
- **Sales window.** Melon Ticket says sales stay open until **11:00 on the show day**. Late bookings cannot be cancelled, changed or refunded after the cancellation deadline.
- **Cancellation deadline.** For Tuesday–Saturday shows it is 17:00 on the previous weekday; for Sunday shows it is 11:00 on Saturday.
- **Cancellation fees** (per Melon Ticket): none within 7 days of booking; up to KRW 4,000 per ticket (10% cap) from day 8 until 10 days before the show; then 10% (9–7 days before), 20% (6–3 days) and 30% (2–1 days). The booking fee is refundable only if you cancel before midnight on the booking day.
- **Foreign visitors.** Melon Ticket's header includes a "Foreigner" pre-booking status check. Run it before sale time and read the show's own notice, since identity rules vary by event.
- **Ticket collection.** Melon's general policy is on-site pickup with the booking confirmation and a physical ID (no copies or photos), or delivery within 4–5 business days if you chose it. The policy says specifics vary by show, so check this show's detail page rather than assuming.

For the wider routine of Korean ticket sites for visitors (accounts, queues, payment), see our [K-pop concert ticketing guide](/posts/kpop-concert-ticketing-seoul-2026/).

## Getting to Hwajeong Gymnasium and Back

- **Location.** Melon Ticket lists the hall at **145 Anam-ro, Seongbuk-gu** (Anam-dong 5-ga, Korea University Anam campus, science side). It sits inside a university campus, not on a street front.
- **Subway.** The campus is served by **Seoul Subway Line 6**; **Korea University Station** (the cover photo shows its Exit 2) is the station name most visitors associate with it. We could not confirm an official walking route, so open the venue name in a map app and check which exit and gate you should use.
- **Return.** Everyone leaves at once after the show, and the Friday show ends late. Look up the last Line 6 train for your direction on the day, note the time you must leave by, and keep a taxi app ready as a backup.
- **Driving.** Campus parking rules for events were not confirmed here. Do not plan on parking; choose public transit.

## Where to Stay and What to Do Nearby

- **Stay on a line with few transfers.** Line 6 passes through Itaewon and Hapjeong, so a hotel on that line gives a simpler return. Confirm the last train for the full route before booking.
- **Before the show.** Eat near your hotel or on the Line 6 route before heading to the campus, since the area fills at doors-open.
- **The next day.** The Saturday and Sunday shows leave your mornings free.
