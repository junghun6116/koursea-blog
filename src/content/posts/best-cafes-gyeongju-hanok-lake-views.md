## Best Cafes in Gyeongju: Verified Hanok Spots & Scenic Lake Views

Gyeongju's cafe scene is uniquely defined by its geography. Around Hwangridan-gil, decades-old traditional Korean houses (hanok) and vintage bathhouses have been converted into specialty coffee bars. Further east, toward the Bomun Lake resort and Bulguksa Temple, expansive multi-story bakeries open directly onto reservoirs and seasonal flower fields.

Here are 11 verified cafes in Gyeongju, categorized by location and distinct specialty.

## Lake Views & Countryside Retreats (Bomun & Bulguksa Area)

### 1. [Cafe Aden Bomun Lake (아덴 보문호수점)](https://www.koursea.com/place/2408/)

**Location:** 424-34 Bomun-ro, Sinpyeong-dong (Bomun Tourist Complex)

**Ratings:** 4.2 / 5.0 (2,219 reviews)

**The Space:** A massive, two-story modern hanok perched right on the edge of Bomun Lake next to the swan pedal boat dock. Outdoor terraces on both floors look directly out across the water.

**What to Order:** Daily fresh-baked pastries and the signature Tomorrow Mocha (a rich cold mocha topped with thick sweet cream).

### 2. [LLOW (카페 엘로우)](https://www.koursea.com/place/2369/)

**Location:** 375-16 Gyeonggam-ro, Cheongun-dong

**Ratings:** 4.2 / 5.0 (1,498 reviews)

**The Space:** An expansive multi-level complex facing the quiet waters of Bomun Lake. The upper street-level entrance is styled as a refined wooden hanok, which opens downward into a modern lower gallery filled with floor-to-ceiling glass windows and lakeside seating.

**Note:** Functions primarily as a peaceful view lounge and enforces a no-kids zone in designated quiet sections.

### 3. [Cafe Arae Heon (아래헌)](https://www.koursea.com/place/2375/)

**Location:** 181 Bobul-ro, Hadong

**Ratings:** 4.9 / 5.0 (1,487 reviews)

**The Space:** A culture-and-coffee complex overlooking Hadong Reservoir between Bomun and Bulguksa Temple. Features an open-air garden, a traditional detached pavilion (numaru), and an underground art gallery hosting rotating exhibitions.

**What to Order:** The Arae Cream Latte (Einspänner-style espresso with sweet milk cream) and house fruit teas.

### 4. [Basilla (바실라)](https://www.koursea.com/place/2422/)

**Location:** 88 Hadongmotan-gil, Hadong

**Ratings:** 4.4 / 5.0 (793 reviews)

**The Space:** Famous across southern Korea for its summer sunflower fields surrounding Hadong Reservoir. The sprawling outdoor courtyard offers clear mountain views and lake breezes.

**What to Order:** Basil-langtte (a signature latte layered with homemade sweet red bean cream) and traditional shaved ice (patbingsu) loaded with slow-cooked red beans and roasted grain powder.

### 5. [Road 100 (카페 로드100)](https://www.koursea.com/place/2359/)

**Location:** 100 Bobul-ro, Hadong

**Ratings:** 4.3 / 5.0 (958 reviews)

**The Space:** A large-scale brunch cafe complex featuring both a Western-style main hall and a quiet hanok annex. Famous for its massive square outdoor window cutout that frames seasonal gardens (sunflowers in summer) like an architectural picture frame.

**What to Order:** All-day Western brunch platters, hot bulgogi paninis, and pasta salads.

## Hwangridan-gil & Historic Downtown (황리단길 & 도심)

### 6. [Skunkworks Roastery (스컹크웍스 황리단길점)](https://www.koursea.com/place/2406/)

**Location:** 1058-3 Poseok-ro, Hwangnam-dong

**Ratings:** 4.3 / 5.0 (678 reviews)

**The Space:** One of Hwangridan-gil's anchor specialty coffee spots, built within a classic wooden hanok courtyard with outdoor courtyard seating.

**What to Order:** In-house roasted single-origin brews, the signature Dongdong Latte (topped with ice cream), and craft beer on tap.

### 7. [Cafe 1894 (1894 사랑채)](https://www.koursea.com/place/2380/)

**Location:** 23 Poseok-ro 1068beon-gil, Hwangnam-dong

**Ratings:** 4.3 / 5.0 (610 reviews)

**The Space:** A meticulously restored historic estate originally constructed in 1894. Customers can sit in traditional floor-seated ondol rooms, the central courtyard garden, or outdoor tables overlooking a small lily pond.

**What to Order:** Patmul Jjinppang (steamed red bean buns drenched in rich, sweet red bean porridge sauce) and milk snow patbingsu.

### 8. [Coffee Place (커피플레이스 본점)](https://www.koursea.com/place/2400/)

**Location:** 18 Jungang-ro, Nodong-dong

**Ratings:** 4.7 / 5.0 (389 reviews)

**The Space:** A purist, community-oriented coffee roaster located right across the avenue from the Bonghwangdae ancient burial mound. Unlike aesthetic dessert spots, this shop focuses strictly on specialty bean sourcing and expert hand-drips.

**What to Order:** Single-origin filter coffees roasted at their local lab, seasonal fresh fruit juices, and house whole bean bags.

### 9. [Olivee (올리브)](https://www.koursea.com/place/2425/)

**Location:** 7-6 Sajeong-ro 57beon-gil, Sajeong-dong

**Ratings:** 4.8 / 5.0 (381 reviews)

**The Space:** Tucked into a quiet alleyway off Hwangridan-gil, Olivee centers around an enclosed gravel courtyard anchored by a large mature tree and natural wood facades.

**What to Order:** House-baked daily pastries, signature ice cream coffee, and vanilla cream lattes.

### 10. [Cafe Neung (카페 능)](https://www.koursea.com/place/2419/)

**Location:** 3 Poseok-ro 1068beon-gil, Hwangnam-dong

**Ratings:** 4.1 / 5.0 (266 reviews)

**The Space:** Housed inside the former Hwangnam-tang, an old neighborhood public bathhouse. The venue preserves the original vintage bathhouse signage outside while offering exposed-rafter ceilings on the 2nd floor and an open-air rooftop overlooking the tiled roofs of Hwangnam-dong.

**What to Order:** Espresso beverages paired with house-baked loaf pound cakes and fudgy brownies.

### 11. [Sol Cafe (카페 솔)](https://www.koursea.com/place/2384/)

**Location:** 62-8 Poseok-ro 1092beon-gil, Hwangnam-dong

**Ratings:** 4.3 / 5.0 (217 reviews)

**The Space:** Positioned right next to Sunghyejeon Shrine along the peaceful outer edge of Hwangridan-gil. The property features a stone stepping-stone path, a manicured lawn garden, and backdrops of dense pine groves.

**What to Order:** Classic cold brew, Einspänner, and seasonal iced teas enjoyed at the outdoor wooden terrace tables.
