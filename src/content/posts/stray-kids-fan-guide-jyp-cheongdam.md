# Stray Kids Fan Guide to Seoul: JYP Center, STAY Route & Concert Prep

Stray Kids officially debuted in March 2018 under JYP Entertainment. The group consists of Bang Chan, Lee Know, Changbin, Hyunjin, HAN, Felix, Seungmin and I.N, and the official fandom is **STAY**. For a Seoul trip, the practical agency stop is the current JYP Center in Seongnae-dong, Gangdong-gu. Despite old search results and the requested legacy filename, the headquarters is not in Cheongdam.

## Key Takeaways for STAY

- Navigate to **205 Gangdong-daero, Gangdong-gu, Seoul** (`서울특별시 강동구 강동대로 205`).
- Use Gangdong-gu Office Station on Line 8 for the shortest straightforward walk, checking the live route in a Korean map app.
- JYP Center is an office. Exterior photos from public sidewalks are appropriate; waiting at entrances or tracking vehicles is not.
- Pair the visit with Olympic Park and, when relevant, an officially announced KSPO DOME event rather than rumored private locations.
- Prepare ticket accounts, Korean SMS capability and backup payment before a high-demand sale.

## JYP Center for Stray Kids Fans

JYP Entertainment's official contact page lists the current headquarters at `서울특별시 강동구 강동대로 205 (성내동, JYP Center)`. Seoul's tourism guide places it about 500 meters from **Gangdong-gu Office Station, Line 8, Exit 2**. Exit numbers and pedestrian works can change, so use Naver Map or KakaoMap for the final block.

The clean glass façade gives STAY a recognizable exterior photo, especially from an angle that includes the JYP Center signage without framing employees or visitors. Use a wide shot from legal public space, keep the entrance clear and move on after taking your photos. The building does not operate as a general Stray Kids exhibition, and an exterior visit does not include access to practice rooms, offices or artist facilities.

Do not organize a wait based on a vehicle, staff movement or an unverified social post. Besides respecting privacy, this prevents a trip from being wasted on false information. Official JYP and Stray Kids notices are the only reliable source for public schedules.

## A STAY-Friendly Gangdong Route

### JYP exterior to Olympic Park

Start at Gangdong-gu Office Station, visit the exterior, then walk or take a short transit connection toward Olympic Park. This route keeps the fan stop grounded in public attractions and can fill two to four hours depending on your pace. Olympic Park's open grounds and sculptures are useful for group photos, banner shots and a relaxed break.

### Concert-venue orientation

Olympic Park contains KSPO DOME and other venues frequently used for K-pop performances. Even if no Stray Kids event is scheduled, seeing the station layout in daylight helps you understand crowd flow. On an actual concert day, follow the event map because temporary merchandise booths, ticket pickup and entry gates vary.

Line 9 can be valuable after a concert, but express and all-stop trains differ. Save your hotel route and the final feasible departure before entering the venue. Mobile service may slow when thousands of fans leave together, so choose a fixed meeting landmark that does not depend on messaging.

### Official merchandise and fan projects

Buy merchandise from official stores, concert booths or announced partners when authenticity matters. Fan-run cafés and cup-sleeve events can be enjoyable but are temporary. Check organizer dates, purchase requirements and closing updates on the same day. Respect café staff and ordinary customers, and never treat an unofficial fan event as proof of an artist appearance.

## Stray Kids Concert and Fan-Meeting Ticketing

High-demand ticketing is a preparation exercise. The current promoter notice determines the platform, fan-club presale eligibility, identity matching and ticket pickup. Do not reuse instructions from a different tour simply because the venue is the same.

If the platform requests Korean SMS, confirm your travel plan can receive it. Many low-cost eSIM products are data-only. The [Korean eSIM Guide](/posts/guide-korea-esim-sim-card-setup-2026/) explains what to check before purchase and redirects to Koursea's current connectivity guide.

Before ticketing opens:

1. Match your account and fan-club names to your passport.
2. Complete identity verification and save a working payment method.
3. Keep a second credit card ready, with overseas and online payments enabled.
4. Log in early, avoid switching devices mid-queue and follow the organizer's browser rules.
5. Avoid unofficial resale when the venue may enforce purchaser-name or ID checks.

## STAY Travel Checklist

- Use the current Seongnae-dong headquarters address, not an old Cheongdam result.
- Stay in public space and keep the office entrance unobstructed.
- Combine the stop with Olympic Park for a useful, respectful route.
- Save venue and hotel addresses in Korean for congested concert exits.
- Follow only official JYP, Stray Kids and promoter notices for schedules and sales.
