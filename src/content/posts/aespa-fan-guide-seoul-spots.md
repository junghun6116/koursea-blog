# aespa Fan Guide to Seoul: MY Spots, SM Seongsu & Concert Planning

aespa debuted on November 17, 2020, under SM Entertainment, and the official fandom name is MY. Seongsu is a useful base because the Seoul Forest area combines SM's working-office district with parks, design stores, cafes, and rotating public pop-ups.

## Key takeaways

- The SM office area is a workplace, not guaranteed public fan access.
- Seongsu pop-ups may require advance booking or same-day queue registration.
- Confirm official store and event locations through current SM notices.
- Prepare ticket identity, payment, phone verification, and return transport early.

## A MY-friendly Seongsu route

### Seoul Forest and the SM area

Arrive through Seoul Forest Station and remain in public plazas and streets. Do not enter restricted floors, gather near employee access, or photograph staff. Any public exhibition or retail operation should be confirmed with a current notice because branding and opening arrangements change.

The route still works without an event: Seoul Forest offers a quiet break, while Seongsu's cafes, fashion flagships, and exhibition spaces are walkable. Group nearby stops rather than repeatedly crossing congested streets.

### Pop-ups, albums, and official merchandise

Check whether a pop-up uses advance booking, remote queueing, or walk-in admission. Screenshot the Korean address, operating dates, booking name, and final-entry time. Follow purchase and photography limits, and never create an unofficial queue outside neighboring businesses.

Buy albums from an established retailer or a partner named in an official notice. Confirm the version and lucky-draw benefit before paying. For concert lightsticks, verify the current model and battery rules; counterfeit products may not connect to venue control.

## Concert preparation for overseas MYs

Use the seller named in the official announcement, not an unsolicited resale link. Our [K-Pop Concert Ticketing in Seoul guide](/posts/kpop-concert-ticketing-seoul-2026/) explains NOL World, account-name matching, overseas payment, and pickup rules.

If Korean SMS appears in the flow, confirm your telecom product first. A data-only eSIM cannot normally receive identity messages. See the [Korea eSIM and SIM setup guide](/posts/guide-korea-esim-sim-card-setup-2026/) for the difference between internet access and a usable `010` number.

Download the venue layout and last-train route. Large crowds can slow data and station access, so choose a fixed meeting point and save your accommodation address in Korean.

## Fan etiquette and Korean show card

Do not follow sightings or visit residences, hotels, vehicles, or private schedules. Official public appearances and authorized events are appropriate; inferred movements are not.

> 에스파 공식 팝업이나 굿즈 매장이 오늘 운영 중인가요?  
> Is the official aespa pop-up or merchandise store open today?

> 서울숲역에서 이 주소로 가는 가장 가까운 출구가 어디인가요?
