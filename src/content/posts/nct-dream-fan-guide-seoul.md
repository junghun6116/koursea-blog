# NCT DREAM Fan Guide to Seoul: NCTzen Stops & Gocheok Sky Dome History

NCT DREAM is an SM Entertainment group and shares the collective's official fandom name, **NCTzen**, with NCT's other units. There is no separately adopted official fandom name just for DREAM, whatever informal nicknames circulate among fans. For a Seoul visit, the useful official public stop is the same [KWANGYA@SEOUL](https://www.koursea.com/place/sm-kwangya-seoul-forest/) already covered on Koursea's [NCT 127](/posts/nct127-fan-guide-sm-seongsu/) and [RIIZE](/posts/riize-fan-guide-sm-seongsu/) guides — not a separate DREAM-only shop, and not the old SMTOWN Coex Artium.

## Key Takeaways

- NCT DREAM's fandom name is NCTzen, shared across NCT and its units; there is no confirmed DREAM-specific official fandom name.
- The group's shared official retail stop is KWANGYA@SEOUL in Seongsu — see our [NCT 127 guide](/posts/nct127-fan-guide-sm-seongsu/) and [SM KWANGYA Seongsu tour route](/posts/sm-kwangya-seongsu-tour-guide/) for the actual visit directions; this guide does not repeat them.
- Do not travel to the former SMTOWN Coex Artium. It closed completely in 2020 and is not a current SM destination.
- NCT DREAM's most recent Seoul concerts were three Gocheok Sky Dome dates in July 2025, and that tour has since finished. No NCT DREAM-specific Seoul venue or date is confirmed for late 2026 as of this writing.
- Prepare with the [Foreigner's Guide to K-Pop Concert Ticketing in Seoul](/posts/kpop-concert-ticketing-seoul-2026/) before any future sale.

## The Shared Official Stop, Not a New One

KWANGYA@SEOUL, at B1, D Tower, Acro Seoul Forest, 83-21 Wangsimni-ro, Seongdong-gu, is SM Entertainment's shared official retail space and carries merchandise for the label's artists, including NCT DREAM. Because our NCT 127 and RIIZE guides already document the walking directions, store layout, and access notes for this exact address, this guide links to them instead of publishing a second description of the same venue. A duplicate listing would only make directions less reliable, not more useful.

SMTOWN Coex Artium is a separate point worth repeating on its own: it closed completely in 2020 and is not an operating SM visitor destination. Any post, video, or map pin that still treats Coex as a current SMTOWN stop is out of date.

## NCT DREAM's Gocheok Sky Dome History

NCT DREAM's own official account confirmed three Seoul dates for **The Dream Show 4: Dream the Future** at **Gocheok Sky Dome** — July 10, 11, and 12, 2025. Roughly 60,000 fans attended across the three nights, per contemporaneous entertainment-press reporting. The tour continued through other Asian cities into late 2025 and early 2026, and by September 2026 it had wrapped.

This history is useful context for what a future NCT DREAM show in Seoul might look like, but it is not a current booking. If DREAM announces a new Seoul date, Koursea's [K-Pop concert day venue and transport guide](/posts/kpop-concert-day-venue-transport-guide-2026/) already covers Gocheok Sky Dome's Line 1 Guil Station access, parking limits, and post-show crowd flow in detail — read that guide once a real date and venue are announced, rather than assuming the 2025 run will repeat at the same venue.

## What's Confirmed for Late 2026 — and What Isn't

SM's wider NCT collective announced a 10th-anniversary project in 2026 covering an album release, world tours, and exhibition plans, reported by entertainment press. That confirms NCT-wide activity is planned, but it does not confirm an NCT DREAM-specific Seoul concert date, venue, or ticketing window.
