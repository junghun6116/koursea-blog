# Seomyeon vs Centum Skin Clinics in Busan (2026)

Busan's two most practical clinic areas serve different itineraries. Seomyeon has the city's largest officially designated medical cluster and the easiest connection from Busan Station. Centum City has a smaller, more premium-leaning clinic market near BEXCO, Shinsegae Centum City, and the Haeundae hotel zone.

> **Price-method note:** The figures below come directly from Koursea's existing Busan Beauty pSEO records. They are planning ranges compiled from public non-insured promotional listings, not government-set prices or guaranteed clinic quotes. Promotions and VAT inclusion can differ by clinic. Match the product, dose, shot count, device tip, physician involvement, anesthesia, and aftercare before comparing two prices.

## Seomyeon: Busan's Largest Medical Cluster

Busanjin-gu identifies Seomyeon Medical Street as one of Busan's three principal medical districts. Roughly 160 clinics and related medical providers sit within about a one-kilometer radius spanning Lotte Department Store and Seomyeon Station. That density supports broad treatment choice, large network clinics, and frequent promotional offers.

Seomyeon is the simpler base for a short KTX itinerary because Subway Line 1 runs directly from Busan Station in about 10–12 minutes. It may suit travelers who already know the procedure and specification they want, but clinic density does not guarantee English consultation, an identical aftercare standard, or a stable promotional price.

## Centum City: Premium-Leaning Care Near Haeundae

Centum's clinic cluster sits around BEXCO, Shinsegae Centum City, and the surrounding office and residential district. Public clinic pages in the area emphasize lifting and anti-aging devices, combined care, private rooms, and appointment-led service more often than Seomyeon's high-volume promotional model.

The location is convenient for travelers staying around Haeundae: the beach area is roughly 5–8 minutes away by Subway Line 2. From Busan Station, however, allow about 40–45 minutes and a transfer. Confirm English coordination and the person performing the procedure before booking; premium positioning alone does not establish either.

## How to Choose Between Seomyeon and Centum

- Choose **Seomyeon** when clinic variety, promotion comparison, and direct access from Busan Station matter most.
- Choose **Centum City** when you are staying near Haeundae or BEXCO and want to compare lifting or anti-aging consultations in a quieter, appointment-led setting.
- Choose neither district based on branding alone. Ask the clinic to identify the product, dose, device, shot count, practitioner, aftercare, VAT, and final total in writing.

For the service-model decision after choosing a district, read [How to Choose a Clinic in Korea](/posts/how-to-choose-a-clinic-korea/).

## Before You Pay Anywhere

1. Ask whether the written total includes VAT: **이 가격에 부가세 포함인가요?**
2. Match the product, dose, syringe volume, device model, shot count, treatment area, and included aftercare.
3. Confirm whether an English-speaking medical coordinator will be present for both consultation and aftercare—not only booking.
4. Ask who conducts the consultation and who performs the procedure.
5. Use the clinic's official channel and request an itemized final total before paying a deposit.
6. Do not assume a medical-procedure tax refund is available. The nine Busan pSEO records used here mark `taxRefundEligible: false`.
