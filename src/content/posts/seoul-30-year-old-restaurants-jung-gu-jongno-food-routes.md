Most "old restaurant" lists are written from memory. This one starts from a public dataset of Korean restaurants that have operated for 30 years or more. For Seoul the file has 50 rows, and 46 remain once duplicate listings are removed. We grouped them by where they stand, because the useful question for a visitor is "which of these can I eat at on one walk?"

The file records a name, address, coordinates, a representative menu item and, for some entries, a price. It does not give opening hours, closing days, reservation rules or founding years, so this guide does not either. Names below are in Korean first, with our own romanization; use the Korean name when you search a map app.

## Where the 46 restaurants are

| Jung-gu | 17 |

| Dongdaemun-gu, Seongbuk-gu, Mapo-gu | 3 each |
| Yeongdeungpo-gu | 2 |

Jung-gu and Jongno-gu together hold 26 of the 46, and 24 of the 46 are within about 2 km of Seoul City Hall (straight-line, from the file's coordinates). That is why the four routes below all sit in central Seoul.

## Route 1: Seosomun lunch row (soup and cold noodles)

Four listings stand in one cluster in Seosomun-dong, Jung-gu. Straight-line distances from Jinjuhoegwan are about 60 to 160 m.

| Restaurant | Representative menu in the file | Price in the file |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 진주회관 (Jinjuhoegwan) | Kongguksu (cold soybean noodle soup) | not stated |
| 잼배옥 (Jaembaeok) | Seolleongtang (ox-bone soup) | not stated |
| 강서면옥 (Gangseo Myeonok) | Pyeongyang naengmyeon (cold buckwheat noodles) | not stated |
| 고려삼계탕 (Goryeo Samgyetang) | Samgyetang (ginseng chicken soup) | not stated |

Pick one dish for lunch and keep the rest of the row for another day, or pair a cold noodle bowl with a hot soup if you travel as two.

## Route 2: Euljiro to Ojang-dong (naengmyeon and a late beer)

- **우래옥 (Uraeok)**, Pyeongyang naengmyeon, and **문화옥 (Munhwaok)**, seolleongtang, are about 25 m apart in Jugyo-dong.
- **오장동 함흥냉면 (Ojang-dong Hamheung Naengmyeon)** is about 450 m from Uraeok in a straight line.
- **함흥 곰보냉면 (Hamheung Gombo Naengmyeon)** is listed at Sewoon Square, unit 401, about 400 m from Uraeok.
- **을지OB베어 (Euljiro OB Bear)** lists a nogari (dried pollock snack) priced at 1,000 won per fish, about 600 m from Uraeok.
- **명동닭한마리 본점 (Myeongdong Dak Hanmari, main branch)** is listed at 종로40가길 14, Jongno 5-ga, about 700 m from Uraeok. Despite the name, the address in the file is in Jongno-gu, not Myeongdong. Its listed price is 25,000 won.

These are straight-line distances. Walking routes are longer, so check a map app.

## Route 3: Insadong and Jongno (set meals and bindaetteok)

- **선천집 (Seoncheonjip)**, listed simply as Korean food (한식) at 12,000 won, and **하나로회관 (Hanaro Hoegwan)**, a hanjeongsik (full course) at 25,000 won, are about 40 m apart in Insadong.
- **열차집 (Yeolchajip)**, bindaetteok (mung bean pancake), is about 350 m from Seoncheonjip in Gongpyeong-dong.
- **청진옥 (Cheongjinok)**, haejangguk (hangover soup), is about 280 m from Yeolchajip in Jongno 1-ga.

Opening hours are not in the file, so check them before you plan a second meal on the same walk.

## Route 4: Myeongdong soup stops

- **하동관 (Hadonggwan)**, gomtang (beef soup), and **금강섞어찌개 명동본점 (Geumgang Seokkeojjigae, Myeongdong main branch)**, a mixed stew at 9,000 won, are about 40 m apart.
- **명동 할매낙지 (Myeongdong Halmae Nakji)**, an octopus set meal, is about 260 m from Hadonggwan.
- **명동칼국수 (Myeongdong Kalguksu)**, 7,000 won, is about 590 m from Hadonggwan on Euljiro.

## Where to stay for this

The file does not recommend hotels. What it does show is that 24 of the 46 listings are within about 2 km of Seoul City Hall, spread over Jung-gu and Jongno-gu. If eating at old restaurants is the point of your trip, a base in central Jung-gu or Jongno-gu puts the most of them within a short ride or walk. For arrival, transit and first-day planning, see our [first 24 hours in Seoul guide](/posts/first-24-hours-in-seoul-survival-guide/), and browse more places on [Koursea](https://www.koursea.com/).

## Getting there, getting back, and paying

- **Late return:** the file contains no opening hours, so we cannot say which places are open late. Check last-train times and each restaurant's hours in a map app on the day.
- **Booking and payment:** not stated in the file. Call ahead or check the restaurant's map listing if you want a table at a busy hour or need to know whether they take cards.
- **Prices:** shown only where the file has them, as of 2024-12-31. Treat them as a guide, not a menu.

## Checklist before you go

1. Search by the Korean name in a map app and confirm the restaurant is still open at that address. The data is dated 2024-12-31.
2. Confirm opening hours and closing days; the file gives neither.
3. Choose one cluster, not all four routes.
4. Keep the dish name (for example seolleongtang or naengmyeon) saved as Korean text to show staff.
