EVNNE, the Jellyfish Entertainment boy group, is holding a two-day fan-concert called **[ONE TAKE] IN SEOUL** on **November 21 and 22, 2026** at YES24 LIVE HALL in Gwangjin-gu, eastern Seoul. This guide covers the parts a visitor has to plan: the official show details, how booking and payment work, and how to get to the hall and back after dark.

One scope note up front. The show details and ticketing rules below come from the official notice on the Mnet Plus fan community. The venue's own website could not be read when this guide was written, so the directions are station-level only, with clear "confirm this" steps rather than exact walking minutes or train times.

## Show Details From the Official Notice

| | Day 1 | Day 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Date | Saturday, November 21, 2026 | Sunday, November 22, 2026 |
| Start time | 18:00 KST | 17:00 KST |
| Venue | YES24 LIVE HALL, Seoul | YES24 LIVE HALL, Seoul |

- **Prices:** VIP KRW 132,000; general KRW 110,000 (VAT included).
- **Running time:** about 120 minutes.
- **Age:** 7 and up.
- **Organizer:** Jellyfish Entertainment, with Spring ENT as host. Tickets are sold through YES24 Ticket.

Source: the "EVNNE FAN-CONCERT [ONE TAKE] IN SEOUL" opening notice on [Mnet Plus](https://artist.mnetplus.world), posted September 23, 2026 and read on November 5, 2026. Sale rules can change, so re-read the notice before you act.

## How Booking Worked, and What That Means Now

The official schedule had two stages:

1. **Fan-club presale.** Fan-club verification ran October 7 to October 13, and the presale itself ran on the evening of October 13. It was limited to 2 tickets per person per show.
2. **General sale** from October 15 at 20:00 KST, up to 4 tickets per person per show including anything bought in the presale. The notice says remaining seats only are offered after the presale.

Both windows have passed. If you do not already have a ticket, the realistic route is to watch the YES24 Ticket page for the show for returned or remaining seats, rather than assuming seats exist. This guide cannot see live availability, so treat any "sold out" or "available" claim as something to verify on the ticket page itself.

For the general mechanics of Korean ticketing for visitors (account setup, queue behavior, identity checks), see our [K-pop concert ticketing guide for foreigners](/posts/kpop-concert-ticketing-seoul-2026/).

### Payment and Ticket Delivery

- The notice lists **card or bank transfer**. Bank transfer must be completed by 23:29 on the booking day or the order auto-cancels, which is hard to do from abroad, so plan on card.
- The notice does not say whether foreign-issued cards are accepted. Foreign cards often work on Korean ticket sites but can fail, so add your card before the queue and keep a second method ready.
- Tickets paid by card and sent by mail were scheduled for bulk shipping on October 29. The notice gives no overseas shipping or on-site pickup information. If you are a visitor buying late, ask the organizer or YES24 Ticket support how a ticket reaches you, and do it before you fly.

## Getting to YES24 LIVE HALL and Back

Treat these as planning steps, not verified door-to-door directions.

- **Area.** The hall is in Gwangjin-gu on the Han River's northern side, east of central Seoul. Most travel guides place it near **Gwangnaru Station on Seoul Subway Line 5**, around **Exit 2**, a short walk away. The cover photo shows that exit.
- **Confirm the entrance.** Open the venue name in a map app and confirm the walking route from your exit. Do this on your phone before the day, because exit numbers and walking distance are the kind of detail that a quick map check settles in a minute.
- **Line 5 and return plan.** Gwangnaru is on Line 5, which also serves Gwanghwamun and Yeouido. The Saturday show starts at 18:00 and the Sunday show at 17:00, so expect different finishing times. Look up the last Line 5 train in your direction on the day in Seoul Metro or your map app, and write down the departure time you must not miss.
- **If you miss the last train.** Taxis are the fallback in Korea. Order through a taxi app if you have one, and keep a screenshot of your hotel name in Korean for the driver.
- **Driving.** Do not plan on parking at the hall. Guides mention limited parking, and the venue's own parking notice could not be read. Ask the venue or choose public transit.

For concert-day habits that apply to any Seoul venue (exit crowding, bags, lightsticks, getting back), the [K-pop concert venue and transport guide](/posts/kpop-concert-day-venue-transport-guide-2026/) walks through KSPO, Gocheok and Jamsil, which are different venues but share the same crowd-flow logic.

## Where to Stay for This Show

Choose your base by the return trip, not by the hall:

- **Line 5 corridor.** A hotel on Line 5 near Gwanghwamun, Dongdaemun History & Culture Park or Yeouido gives you a one-line ride back with no transfer. That is the safest choice after a 120-minute show with a crowd leaving at once.
- **Hongdae, Myeongdong or Jamsil.** These work too, but expect at least one transfer, so confirm the last-train times for the full route.

## Before and After the Show

You do not need a full itinerary for a two-hour fan-concert, but a simple pattern helps:

1. **Eat before you go.** Venue areas fill up around start time, so have dinner near your hotel or on the Line 5 route before the doors open.
2. **Arrive early for entry checks.** Fan-concerts commonly check tickets and IDs at the door. Bring the ID or passport your booking requires, and read the notice for bag and recording rules.
3. **Leave the pre-show area early after the encore.** Everyone walks to the same station at once.
