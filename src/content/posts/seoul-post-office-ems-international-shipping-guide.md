Shipping purchases home can cost less than an airline overweight charge, but only when the contents are accepted by Korea Post and your destination customs. Cosmetics, food, batteries, aerosols, and merchandise need more care than clothing. Pack only after checking destination-specific restrictions.

## Key takeaways

- Use Korea Post’s official country-conditions and price tools before visiting.
- Write customs descriptions in specific English, not “gift” or “stuff.”
- Keep liquids, batteries, food, and pressurized products separate until staff approve them.
- Bring the recipient’s full Roman-alphabet address, postal code, phone, and email.
- Keep the 13-character tracking number and photograph every declaration page.

## Which post office to use

Search **우체국** plus your district in Naver Map. The Seoul Central Post Office near Myeongdong is convenient for travelers because it is central and handles international services, but counter hours and service desks can change. Verify the official Korea Post branch listing on the day you go and arrive well before the final acceptance time.

Not every small postal counter provides the same packaging stock or international assistance. If sending many cosmetics or a valuable parcel, choose a larger branch and allow at least an hour. “Open” on a map does not guarantee the international counter is still accepting complex shipments.

## Step 1: sort before buying a box

Create four groups: ordinary clothing and paper goods; cosmetics and liquids; food or plant-derived goods; electronics and batteries. The first group is usually simplest. The others may face airline-security, postal, carrier, or destination-country restrictions.

Korea Post states that international mail is security-screened and prohibited air items can be returned to the accepting post office. Batteries inside electronics may require a different service, while loose power banks are especially problematic. Aerosols, perfume, nail products, sprays, and unknown liquids should never be hidden inside clothing.

Do not remove ingredient labels from cosmetics. Factory-sealed products are easier to identify than decanted liquids. Large Olive Young hauls can become heavy quickly; weigh them at the hotel before carrying everything across town.

## Step 2: confirm the destination accepts each item

Use the official [Korea Post EMS portal](https://ems.epost.go.kr/) and its country-specific conditions. Destination customs decides what may enter even if Korea Post accepts the parcel. Import tax, brokerage, inspection, and storage fees can be charged to the recipient.

Check maximum weight and dimensions, suspended services, and postcode requirements. Service disruptions change. Never promise yourself a delivery date based on an old blog: Korea Post’s estimate excludes posting day, holidays, and customs retention.

## Step 3: prepare the address and customs invoice

Bring the recipient’s name, street, apartment or unit, city, state or province, postal code, country, phone number with country code, and email in Roman characters. Confirm the address with the recipient rather than translating it yourself.

Korea Post’s smart-acceptance form requires customs information in English for non-document contents. Prepare:

- detailed English item description;
- quantity;
- total value and currency;
- net weight;
- country of origin;
- HS tariff code where required;
- classification such as gift or merchandise.

Write “facial moisturizer, 50 ml, 3 units,” not “cosmetics.” Write “cotton T-shirts, 4,” not “clothes.” Declare the honest purchase value. Under-declaring can cause delay, seizure, insurance problems, or penalties.

Korea Post notes that a label may print only a limited number of content lines even when the online form accepts more; staff can issue or request a separate invoice for additional items. Group genuinely identical products, but do not combine unrelated categories into a vague line.

## Step 4: pack at the branch or hotel

If the contents are straightforward, pre-pack at the hotel but leave the box unsealed so staff can inspect it. For uncertain cosmetics or electronics, bring items sorted in bags and buy the final box only after acceptance is confirmed.

Use a strong box with empty space filled so products do not move. Protect pump tops and caps in separate sealed bags. The postal service’s acceptance does not guarantee leakage coverage. Photograph contents, serial numbers, receipts, and the packed layers.

Remove old barcodes and hazardous-goods markings from reused boxes. Do not wrap the parcel in a way that hides seams staff may need to inspect.

## Step 5: choose EMS, parcel, or another solution

EMS is tracked express postal shipping, but it is not automatically best for every country or weight. International parcel services may be slower and cheaper. EMS Premium can follow different carrier rules. Ask staff to compare available products for the destination and contents.

The official calculator is a reference, not a guaranteed counter total. Volumetric weight can make a large light box cost more than its scale weight. Additional transport surcharges may apply. Compare the shipping total with airline extra-bag or overweight fees before paying.

For K-pop albums, remove unnecessary retail bags but preserve collectible packaging. Albums, books, and magazines become extremely heavy; use a smaller reinforced box. Declare merchandise accurately even if the items are fan gifts.

## Step 6: pay, document, and track

Check the printed recipient address character by character. Confirm declared value, weight, destination, service, and return contact before paying. Keep the original receipt until delivery and photograph it immediately.

EMS tracking uses a 13-character item number such as a code beginning with letters and ending in KR. Track first through Korea Post, then through the destination postal operator after handoff. A “customs” scan is not necessarily a seizure; it may indicate normal inspection.

If tracking stops, contact Korea Post at the official customer channel shown on the EMS site. Time limits apply to inquiries and claims, so do not wait months. The sender normally needs the receipt to open an investigation.

## Cosmetics, batteries, food, and tax realities

Skincare is not one universal category. Creams, sheet masks, powders, alcohol-heavy products, aerosols, and devices can receive different treatment. Ask staff with the product physically present. Do not assume a product accepted in cabin baggage is accepted in international post.

Lithium batteries create both aviation and carrier restrictions. Keep power banks and loose cells out until explicitly approved. Food, seeds, meat ingredients, and herbal products can trigger quarantine rules abroad even when commercially packaged.

Shipping does not erase customs obligations or automatically preserve a tourist tax-refund claim. Keep purchase receipts and follow the relevant export-verification procedure. The recipient may owe import tax based on local thresholds.

## What to say at the counter

Show: **이 물건들을 해외로 EMS로 보내고 싶습니다. 발송 가능한지 먼저 확인해 주세요.** This means, “I want to send these items overseas by EMS. Please check first whether they can be sent.”

For packaging: **상자와 완충재를 여기에서 살 수 있나요?** — “Can I buy a box and cushioning material here?”

Translation apps help, but the official English item list and destination address are more important than spoken fluency. Visit before the late-afternoon rush.

## If EMS is not suitable

Compare a checked bag, airline prepaid baggage, a licensed international courier, or leaving restricted items behind. Never hand valuables to an unverified “shipping helper.” A courier that accepts an item can still require destination customs documents.

## Address formatting that prevents delays

Write the recipient’s name exactly as used for deliveries in the destination country. Put the building number, street, apartment or unit, city, region, postal code, and country in a logical Roman-alphabet order. Add a reachable phone number with country code. Decorative handwriting and Korean transliteration do not help a foreign postal operator.

The return address should identify a real location that can receive a returned parcel after you leave Korea. Ask your hotel before using its address; checkout ends its obligation to store mail. If no reliable Korean return address exists, discuss the limitation with the counter rather than inventing one.

For customs, avoid vague descriptions such as “gift,” “goods,” “samples,” or “merch.” Use concrete English terms—cotton T-shirts, printed photo cards, facial cleanser, paperback books—with truthful quantities and values. Group identical low-risk items only when the declaration remains accurate. A low declared value does not guarantee lower tax and may weaken an insurance claim.

## Volumetric weight and packing strategy

International carriers may charge for space as well as scale weight. A large light box can therefore cost more than expected. Remove retail air pillows, oversized presentation cartons, and redundant packaging, but keep seals needed for hygiene or proof of purchase. Place dense objects low and central, fill movement gaps, and protect corners.

Do not create a box so heavy that the recipient cannot safely carry it. Two smaller parcels may be easier but can cost more because each has a base charge. Compare both configurations before sealing. Photograph the packed layers and final label without publishing personal details.

Tape should reinforce seams without covering barcodes or required declarations. If reusing a box, remove every old label, hazard mark, and tracking barcode. Postal staff can require repacking when the carton is damaged or misleading.

## Tracking, customs, and delivery after Korea

The EMS number proves acceptance, not guaranteed delivery by a particular date. Tracking may pause while the parcel is in flight, customs, or a partner network. Send the number to the recipient immediately and ask them to monitor requests for tax, identity confirmation, or collection.

Use only the destination postal service or Korea Post tracking links. Delivery-text scams often demand a small “redelivery” payment and harvest card details. Confirm the tracking number independently before opening any payment page.

If tracking stalls, note the last scan, date, contents, declared value, and recipient details before contacting Korea Post. Insurance and inquiry deadlines apply, so do not wait indefinitely. Keep the physical receipt until the recipient has inspected the parcel.

## Traveler FAQ

### Can the post office pack everything for me?

Some larger branches sell boxes and materials, but staff are not personal packing services and acceptance checks can require you to reopen the parcel. Arrive with items sorted and enough time to repack.

### Can I send purchases without receipts?

Acceptance and destination customs are separate questions. Receipts support accurate value declarations, tax-refund procedures, authenticity questions, and claims. Preserve them even if the counter does not request them.

### Is EMS always cheaper than airline baggage?

No. Weight, box dimensions, destination zone, surcharge, airline allowance, and possible import tax all matter. Compare the complete cost, not a headline per-kilogram rate.
