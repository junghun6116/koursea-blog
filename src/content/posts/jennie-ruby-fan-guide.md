# JENNIE Fan Guide: Ruby, Official Releases & Seoul Planning

JENNIE is a BLACKPINK member and solo artist. Her official store describes **Ruby** as her first full-length studio album, released with ODDATELIER and Columbia Records. This guide uses that primary source and avoids turning unconfirmed sightings, private spaces, or fan speculation into travel advice.

## Key takeaways

- Confirm music and merchandise through JENNIE's official store and official channels.
- Ruby is an album, not a permanent Seoul attraction.
- Do not visit offices, residences, hotels, or inferred private schedules.
- Follow the seller named in the official concert or event announcement.

## What is officially confirmed about Ruby?

The [official JENNIE store](https://shop.jenn.ie/collections/music) lists Ruby in its music collection. Its [official product description](https://shopasia.jenn.ie/products/ruby-digital-download) identifies Ruby as JENNIE's first full-length studio album, released in collaboration with ODDATELIER and Columbia Records, and lists a 15-song track sequence.

Use those official pages to verify formats and current availability. Product stock, regional shipping, inclusions, and prices can change, so confirm the exact edition at checkout rather than relying on an old screenshot or resale listing.

## Planning a JENNIE-themed Seoul day

Do not assume that an entertainment office or private workspace accepts visitors. A safer plan uses public, confirmed destinations: an established album retailer, an organizer-announced pop-up that is operating on your date, and nearby public neighborhoods that remain worthwhile if merchandise sells out.

For physical releases, compare the seller, edition name, inclusions, and sealed packaging before paying. Koursea's [guide to spotting fake K-pop merchandise in Seoul](/posts/how-to-spot-fake-k-pop-merch-seoul-guide/) provides a practical authenticity checklist.

When a temporary event is announced, use the original organizer notice for the address, operating dates, entry method, purchase limits, and photography rules. A past event page does not prove that the venue is still running the same activation.

## Concert and event preparation

Use only the ticket seller linked by the official promoter or artist notice. Our [K-Pop Concert Ticketing in Seoul guide](/posts/kpop-concert-ticketing-seoul-2026/) explains passport-name matching, overseas cards, presale steps, venue pickup, and return transport.

Save the venue's Korean address and entry conditions offline. Keep a payment backup, and distinguish a confirmed ticket announcement from album promotion or unofficial resale advertising.

## Fan etiquette

Attend official public schedules, ticketed events, and authorized retail activations. Do not follow vehicles, wait at private buildings, or use airport and hotel rumors as itinerary information. Respect queue, filming, and purchase rules at every venue.
