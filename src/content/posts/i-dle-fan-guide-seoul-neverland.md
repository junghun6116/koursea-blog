# i-dle Fan Guide to Seoul: NEVERLAND Concert & Official Planning

Cube Entertainment's official notices use **i-dle** as the current group styling and identify NEVERLAND as the official fandom name. This guide relies on dated Cube and official-store notices rather than fan-made schedules.

Company offices and studios are workplaces, not public attractions. Build a Seoul fan route around announced concerts, showcases, pop-ups, and authorized retail—not office access or inferred artist movements.

## Key takeaways

- Check Cube's current official notices before making a venue or merchandise plan.
- NEVERLAND membership service arrangements have changed, so do not rely on an old signup guide.
- A past concert booth confirms historical activity only; it is not a current event notice.
- Use the ticket seller and identity rules named for the exact show you plan to attend.

## What should NEVERLAND verify now?

Cube's [official 2025 notice](https://weverse.io/i-dle/notice/30903) announced the end of sales and service for the former i-dle GLOBAL OFFICIAL FANCLUB NEVERLAND MEMBERSHIP on Weverse. A second [official channel notice](https://weverse.io/i-dle/notice/30950) said the group's Weverse service would end and that i-dle would return through a new community.

That means older instructions for joining or displaying a Weverse membership card should not be treated as current. Find the latest community and fan-club route through Cube's present official channels before a presale.

## How should past Seoul notices be used?

Cube's [official 2024 NEVERLAND ZONE notice](https://weverse.io/gidle/notice/21204) confirms that the **iDOL** world tour's Seoul shows used KSPO DOME and operated a fan-club booth requiring a same-day ticket, identification, and a membership card. Those dates and booth rules have expired.

The notice is still a useful checklist for the kind of details a current announcement may contain: venue, operating hours, eligibility, accepted identification, and collection steps. Never carry its dates or entry rules into a future event without a new notice.

## Is the Cube office a visitor attraction?

No public access should be assumed. Do not enter staff areas, wait at entrances, photograph employees, follow vehicles, or share live locations from unannounced movements. If Cube announces a public fan event, use only the public venue and operating window stated in that notice.

## Concert preparation for overseas fans

Our [Foreigner's Guide to K-Pop Concert Ticketing in Seoul](/posts/kpop-concert-ticketing-seoul-2026/) explains passport-name matching, fan-club presales, foreign-card checks, pickup, and return transport.

Before booking:

- confirm the group styling, event title, city, venue, and date;
- use only the seller linked by Cube or the official organizer;
- verify the current fan-club platform instead of following an archived Weverse flow;
- read the identification, booth, recording, bag, and light-stick rules;
- save the venue address and final train or bus route offline.

## Official merchandise and respectful fan conduct

Cube's [official album notice](https://weverse.io/i-dle/notice/27602) identified CUBEE and named online music stores for the **We are i-dle** release. Apply the same rule to future purchases: use a current official notice, verify the exact product and benefit period, and do not treat copied promotional images as proof that a seller is authorized.

Public concerts and announced events are appropriate fan activities. Airports, hotels, residences, vehicles, and private schedules are not.
