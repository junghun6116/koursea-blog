# ATEEZ Fan Guide to Seoul: ATINY Concert & Official Planning

KQ Entertainment's [ATEEZ official platform](https://ateez.kqent.com/) is the primary place to check notices, schedules, public content, community updates, and official shop information. KQ's platform-launch notice identifies the service as a space for ATEEZ and ATINY, confirming the official fandom name.

This guide covers public activities only. A company office is a workplace, not a fan attraction, and its published business address does not imply lobby access, tours, or artist encounters.

## Key takeaways

- Start with the official ATEEZ platform rather than an undated fan itinerary.
- Treat every concert, fan meeting, pop-up, and merchandise booth as date-specific.
- Do not visit offices, studios, hotels, vehicles, or private schedules.
- Prepare ticket identity, payment, membership, and return transport before a sale opens.

## What should ATINY verify before visiting Seoul?

Open the [official platform notice board](https://ateez.kqent.com/) and confirm that a current announcement names the event, city, venue, date, organizer, and sales channel. A schedule screenshot or repost can become stale; return to the source before paying or traveling.

KQ's [official platform announcement](https://ateez.kqent.com/community/board/6409e36d8125892dac2820a7/post/6449e533d59af407458a5623) explains that ATEEZ photos, videos, schedules, public-broadcast guidance, chat, and live content were brought into the official service for ATINY. Use that platform rather than copying instructions from an older fan-cafe guide.

## Is KQ Entertainment a visitor attraction?

No public office access should be assumed. The official platform publishes KQ's business address for company and customer-service purposes, not as a sightseeing invitation. Stay away from employee entrances, do not wait for vehicles, and never infer artist movements from office activity.

If KQ announces a public exhibition, pop-up, or fan event, follow only the venue entrance, operating dates, and participation rules in the current notice. Build the rest of the day around public neighborhoods, cafes, parks, or established music retailers.

## Concert preparation for overseas ATINY

Use only the ticket seller linked by KQ or the official organizer. Our [Foreigner's Guide to K-Pop Concert Ticketing in Seoul](/posts/kpop-concert-ticketing-seoul-2026/) explains account-name matching, fan-club presales, overseas payment checks, ticket pickup, and post-show transport.

Before booking:

- confirm the city, venue, show date, and sale time on the official page;
- check whether ATINY membership verification is required before the ticket sale;
- match the account name to the identification rules;
- save the venue address and final train or bus route offline;
- recheck current bag, recording, light-stick, and merchandise rules.

## Official merchandise and respectful fan conduct

Use the shop or seller linked from the official platform. Verify the product edition, benefit period, collection deadline, and exchange policy. A copied logo or official photo does not prove that a third-party seller is authorized.

Attend announced concerts, broadcasts, fan meetings, and public pop-ups. Airports, accommodation, offices, private transport, and unannounced movements are outside a respectful fan itinerary.
