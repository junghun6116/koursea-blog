# Hongdae Station T-Locker Guide: Sizes, Location, and Backup Prices

Hongik University Station is one of Seoul's busiest transfer points, sitting on Line 2, the Airport Railroad (AREX), and the Gyeongui-Jungang Line. That makes it a convenient place to drop your bags — and also one of the first locker banks to fill up on a busy day.

## Finding the lockers

Look for signage reading "Locker" or "물품보관함" (mulpum-bogwanham) near the station's main transfer corridors. The largest concentration sits close to **Exit 9** and along the passage that connects to the AREX platforms, since that's the route most travelers with luggage are already walking.

## Size tiers and what actually fits

T-Locker banks are split into small, medium, and large compartments:

- **Small** — a day bag, backpack, or shopping bag.
- **Medium** — a standard carry-on suitcase.
- **Large** — a full-size checked suitcase (around 28 inches), though it's a snug fit. If you're carrying two large bags, plan on renting two lockers.

Payment runs through the kiosk screen, which has an English option, and accepts card payment — you don't need exact cash the way you sometimes do for subway top-ups.

## If the bank is full

Weekends and holiday afternoons are when Hongdae's lockers run out first. Before you make the trip specifically to store bags, check the **T-Locker app**, which shows live availability by station so you're not walking over on a gamble. If the whole bank is genuinely full, the fallback is one of the private luggage storage counters operating above ground near the station's main exits — slightly more expensive than a T-Locker, but they don't run out the same way.
