Yangyang's cafe culture is deeply intertwined with its dual geography: breaking East Sea swells along the coastal strip and quiet pine groves rooted toward the foothills of Gangwon Province. From laid-back roasteries serving the surfing community to modern pavilions hidden inside pine forests, each spot provides a distinct perspective on the region.

Here are 12 verified cafes across Yangyang, organized by geographic setting.

## Ocean Views & Beachfront Lookouts (해안선 & 오션뷰)

### 1. [Sea View Bakery (바다뷰제빵소)](https://www.koursea.com/place/4966/)

Ganghyeon-myeon, near Naksan Beach. 4.0/5.0 (1,409 reviews). A sprawling, multi-story bakery cafe sitting directly beside the coastal highway above Naksan Beach. An expansive wooden outdoor terrace extends right along the waterline, featuring open sea seating and panoramic horizon photo points. What to order: fresh daily fruit tarts, buttery croissants, and house iced brews served over the waves.

### 2. [P.E.I Coffee (P.E.I 커피)](https://www.koursea.com/place/4985/)

Ganghyeon-myeon, Mulchi Beach. 4.4/5.0 (468 reviews). Inspired by Prince Edward Island and the storytelling world of Anne of Green Gables, this modern glass-walled cafe offers full ocean views near Mulchi Port and Mulchi Beach from its upper floors and open rooftop. What to order: Pei Viennese Coffee (layered espresso under rich whipped cream) and sparkling seasonal fruit coolers.

### 3. [Cafe Dalpado (달파도)](https://www.koursea.com/place/4952/)

Hyeonnam-myeon, near Namae Port & Namae 3-ri Beach. 4.3/5.0 (68 reviews). A peaceful ocean-facing stop situated along the quiet, curved sandy shoreline of southern Yangyang. Large glass windows frame the breaking surf of Namae. What to order: chilled signature ades and balanced drip coffees in a tranquil beach setting away from dense surf hubs.

## Surfer Vibes & Restored Country Houses (서핑 로스터리 & 구옥)

### 4. [Hajodae Coffee (하조대커피)](https://www.koursea.com/place/4992/)

Hyeonbuk-myeon, near Hajodae Beach. 4.7/5.0 (135 reviews). Centered around a restored rustic countryside cottage with an open gravel courtyard, wooden benches beneath mature trees, and a casual rooftop deck. What to order: the signature Hajodae Coffee (a creamy, nutty espresso drink with sweet milk).

### 5. [Cafe Maem-Maem (카페 맴맴)](https://www.koursea.com/place/4994/)

Hyeonnam-myeon, Ingu Beach. 4.4/5.0 (92 reviews). Tucked into a narrow residential lane behind Ingu Beach, this cozy roastery repurposes an old village home with exposed wooden timber beams, retro furnishings, and surfboard racks. What to order: meticulously brewed single-origin filter coffees and iced lattes.

### 6. [Kinship (킨십 카페)](https://www.koursea.com/place/4980/)

Hyeonnam-myeon, near Jukdo Beach. 4.9/5.0 (79 reviews). A minimalist coffee bar serving travelers and local surfers around Jukdo Beach. Emphasizes clean timber finishes and careful extraction. What to order: hand-poured drip coffees using rotating beans, alongside balanced flat whites.

### 7. [Hey Cafe (헤이카페)](https://www.koursea.com/place/4961/)

Hyeonnam-myeon, Ingu / Jukdo corridor. 4.4/5.0 (160 reviews). A welcoming, compact neighborhood spot providing a quick coffee pause between beach walks and surf sessions. What to order: iced Americanos, matcha lattes, and daily baked sweet treats.

### 8. [Badajigi Atelier Cafe (바다지기 공방카페)](https://www.koursea.com/place/4988/)

Sonyang-myeon, near Dongho Beach. 4.4/5.0 (299 reviews). Combines a working craft workshop with a relaxing cafe area, displaying handmade ceramics and decorative pieces near the pine-fringed coastal road. What to order: handcrafted fruit teas, refreshing ades, and artisanal desserts.

## Pine Forests & Concept Retreats (솔숲, 온천 족욕 & 북카페)

### 9. [Cafe Log (카페로그)](https://www.koursea.com/place/5014/)

Hyeonbuk-myeon. 4.7/5.0 (312 reviews). Housed within a training retreat run by the Korean Textbook Association (한국교과서협회), set on a serene, pine-covered hillside called "Solhyanggi Hill." Spanning roughly 130 pyeong, the expansive floor-to-ceiling glass interior houses over 13,000 books looking directly out into the dense pine forest. Guests can read freely in quiet lounge chairs immersed in natural timber interiors and pine foliage vistas.

### 10. [Concentric Yangyang (컨센트릭 양양점)](https://www.koursea.com/place/5012/)

Sonyang-myeon, Songjeon-ri. 4.4/5.0 (127 reviews). A contemporary architectural building featuring clean concrete lines and wide panorama windows looking onto natural pine trees. What to order: in-house roasted specialty beans, cold brew varietals, and signature milk coffees.

### 11. [Seol-On (설온)](https://www.koursea.com/place/4959/)

Ganghyeon-myeon. 4.4/5.0 (51 reviews). A concept cafe created by revitalizing the historic Bokgol Hot Spring (복골온천) site, tucked inside peaceful mountain woods. Features a natural hot spring foot-bath lounge where visitors can soak their feet in warm mineral water. What to order: the signature Hot Spring Pudding (온천 푸딩) paired with hot specialty teas or iced coffee.

### 12. [Cafe Dunchi (카페 둔치)](https://www.koursea.com/place/5002/)

Yangyang-eup, Namdaecheon Stream. 4.9/5.0 (312 reviews). Set right along the wide reed beds and scenic walking paths of Namdaecheon Stream, offering open inland river views distinct from the beachfront spots. What to order: fresh fruit beverages, roasted coffees, and baked goods enjoyed in a bright, modern interior.
