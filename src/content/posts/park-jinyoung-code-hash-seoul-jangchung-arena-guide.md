GOT7's Park Jinyoung is playing his **first solo concert tour, [CODE#]**, in Seoul this weekend: **November 7 and 8, 2026** at **Jangchung Arena**, the old Jangchung Gymnasium in Jung-gu. This guide covers what a visitor needs: the official show details, the seat and pickup rules on the ticket page, and a plan for getting there and back.

Show details, prices and ticket rules come from the official Melon Ticket listing, read on November 5, 2026. The organizers are BH Entertainment and Shownote. Venue directions come from the arena operator, Seoul Facilities Corporation. Rules can change, so reread the listing before you go.

## Show Details From Melon Ticket

| | Day 1 | Day 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Date | Sat, Nov 7 | Sun, Nov 8 |
| Start (KST) | 18:00 | 16:00 |

- **Prices:** VIP KRW 176,000; general KRW 154,000; view-limited KRW 143,000 (VAT included).
- **Running time:** 120 minutes. **Age:** 7 and up.
- **Support:** Melon Ticket customer center, 1899-0042.

The Sunday show starts at 16:00, earlier than the Saturday show, so plan your Sunday transport accordingly.

## The View-Limited Seats

The listing carries a notice about seats added in the last week:

- **Opened:** November 2 at 20:00 KST.
- **Where:** parts of 2nd-floor sections **D2, E2, L2 and M2**, for every show.
- **What it means:** part of the stage, the screen or the artist may be hidden by stage structure or production. How much depends on the seat and on you.
- **No refund for the view:** cancellation, change or refund on the day because of the view is not possible for any reason.
- **Limit:** 2 tickets per person per show, counting tickets already bought.

If you already hold a ticket in another area, this does not change your seat.

## Booking and Pickup

- **Domestic page** sale opened September 18 at 20:00 KST; the **global page** on September 21 at 20:00 KST, with a separate wheelchair-seat sale on September 22. Each page has its own schedule.
- **Batch delivery:** for payments completed by **October 15 at 18:00**, shipped in order from October 21. After that, **on-site pickup only**.
- **Cancelling a shipped batch order** was possible only through the customer center and 1:1 inquiry until October 20 at 18:00, and the shipping fee is not refunded afterward.
- **Cancellation fees** (per Melon Ticket): none within 7 days of booking; up to KRW 4,000 per ticket (10% cap) from day 8 until 10 days before; then 10% (9 to 7 days), 20% (6 to 3 days) and 30% (2 to 1 days). The booking fee is refundable only if you cancel before midnight on the booking day.
- **If you pick up on site,** the standard Melon Ticket rule is a booking confirmation and a physical ID, but this show's own pickup details were not in the listing text we read, so open the full notice and check.

For the general routine of Korean ticket sites for visitors, see our [K-pop concert ticketing guide](/posts/kpop-concert-ticketing-seoul-2026/).

## Getting to Jangchung Arena and Back

Everything here comes from the operator's directions page.

- **Address:** 241 Dongho-ro, Jung-gu, Seoul (Jangchung-dong).
- **Subway:** **Line 3, Dongguk Univ. Station, Exit 5**, which the operator describes as directly connected to the arena.
- **Buses:** the operator lists services including N13, 144, 301 and 7212 at the stop in front of the arena, and 05, 02 and 03 at the Dongguk Univ. Station/Jangchung-dong stop. Check a map app for current routes.
- **Parking:** there is a car park on the north side, but the operator says there is **no general parking during events**. Use the subway.
- **Return:** exits fill at once after the show. The station is connected to the arena, so you can reach the platform without a street walk, but expect a queue. Note the last Line 3 train for your direction ahead of time; this guide did not confirm one from an official source. A taxi app is the backup.
