# Olive Young 2026 Best Sellers by Skin Type & Awards Winners

> For the complete tax refund walkthrough and flagship store map, read our [Complete Foreigner Guide to Olive Young](/posts/olive-young-guide-foreign-travelers/).

Olive Young’s best-seller wall can make almost every product look essential. A better approach is to start with your skin type, then use Olive Young Awards results and sales trends as a shortlist—not as a substitute for checking ingredients and texture.

This guide focuses only on what to buy in 2026: award-recognized products, fast-growing categories, and practical picks for dry, oily, sensitive, and blemish-prone skin.

## Quick Picks by Skin Type

| Skin type or concern | Start with | What to look for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dry or dehydrated | Moisturizing sunscreen, hydrating serum, sheet mask | Humectants plus barrier support; comfortable finish |
| Oily or combination | Lightweight sunscreen, thin toner pads, patches | Fast absorption and non-greasy layers |
| Sensitive or reactive | Fragrance-free soothing products | Shorter formulas, centella, panthenol, or ceramides |
| Blemish-prone | Hydrocolloid patches and gentle toner pads | Minimal irritation; avoid stacking strong actives |
| Dull or uneven-looking | PDRN cosmetic serum or hydrating ampoule | Full ingredient list and realistic cosmetic claims |

Patch test unfamiliar products and introduce one active product at a time, especially during travel.

## Olive Young Awards Winner to Know

### Round Lab Birch Juice Moisturizing Sun Cream

Round Lab’s Birch Juice Moisturizing Sun Cream placed first in the Olive Young Awards sun-care category for four consecutive years through the 2025 awards, according to the brand’s official announcement.

It is worth testing if you want:

- A moisturizing daily sunscreen
- A cream-like rather than dry-matte finish
- A widely stocked Korean sun-care product
- An easy gift or repeat-purchase option

Awards reflect strong sales and customer recognition, but they do not guarantee a match for every skin type. Test the finish in-store if you are very oily, highly reactive, or sensitive to chemical UV filters.

## 2026 Trending Category: PDRN Skincare

CJ Olive Young reported that searches for “PDRN” during a major sale period rose **695% compared with the corresponding previous-year sale period**. That measures shopping interest—not proof that every topical PDRN product produces a medical or regenerative result.

When comparing ampoules and serums:

- Confirm that PDRN appears in the ingredient list.
- Compare the complete formula instead of the headline ingredient alone.
- Do not assume a higher advertised concentration is automatically better.
- Distinguish topical cosmetics from injectable clinic treatments.
- Add only one new product at a time if your skin is reactive.

## Best Practical Buy for Blemishes: Hydrocolloid Patches

Shop this category by size, thickness, and intended use rather than chasing one viral brand.

- **Small circular patches:** Individual superficial blemishes
- **Thin daytime patches:** Less visible under makeup
- **Larger sheets:** Flexible sizing for broader areas
- **Thicker overnight patches:** More absorption when appropriate
- **Waterproof dressings:** Minor spots that need protection

Hydrocolloid products are not a substitute for medical care and should not cover signs of a serious infection. After a procedure, follow the clinic’s aftercare instructions before adding an unfamiliar dressing.

## Best Easy Gift: Sheet Masks and Soothing Masks

Choose by function rather than packaging:

- Fragrance-free hydration for reactive skin
- Panthenol or centella formulas for soothing
- Ceramide masks for barrier support
- Lightweight gel masks after sun exposure
- Simple moisturizing masks before a flight

Avoid combining unfamiliar exfoliating acids, retinoids, and other strong actives immediately after a procedure unless your clinician has approved them.

## How to Shop the Awards Wall Without Overbuying

### Before You Visit

- Build a list by category, not by brand hype.
- Check the regular price against the current promotion.
- Note your preferred texture and ingredients you avoid.
- Leave luggage space for bulky boxed sets.

### In the Store

- Test textures on a small area.
- Compare single products with promotional twin packs.
- Read the actual size and quantity on each package.
- Check expiration dates when buying multiples.
- Look beyond the overall winner to category winners that fit your skin.

### Before You Commit to a Viral Product

1. Does it solve a need already in my routine?
2. Can I identify the active and supporting ingredients?
3. Will I realistically finish this size before it expires?

## Final Shopping Checklist

- [ ] Choose products for your skin type and current routine.
- [ ] Use Awards rankings as a shortlist, not medical advice.
