Epik High will play three nights at **KSPO DOME in Olympic Park** from **January 22 to 24, 2027**. The shows are still months away, which is the right time to plan the parts that are hard to fix later: how the booking and cancellation rules work, what a winter evening at an Olympic Park arena involves, and how to get back to your hotel after dark.

Show details, prices and rules come from the official Melon Ticket listing, read on November 5, 2026. The organizers are Ours Co. and Shownote. Rules can change, so reread the listing before you pay or cancel.

## Show Details From Melon Ticket

| | Day 1 | Day 2 | Day 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Date | Fri, Jan 22, 2027 | Sat, Jan 23 | Sun, Jan 24 |
| Start (KST) | 20:00 | 18:00 | 16:00 |

- **Venue:** KSPO DOME, Olympic Park, Songpa-gu, Seoul.
- **Prices:** R seats KRW 176,000; S seats KRW 165,000; A seats KRW 154,000.
- **Running time:** 120 minutes. **Age:** 12 and up.
- **Support:** Melon Ticket, 1899-0042 (09:00 to 18:00 on weekdays and weekends, per the listing).

The Friday show starts at 20:00, so it ends late. The Sunday show starts at 16:00 and ends earlier, which matters if you have an early flight on Monday.

## The Sale Schedule and What It Means Now

The listing published this schedule:

- **Fan-club verification:** September 29 at 14:00 to October 3 at 23:59 (KST).
- **Fan-club presale:** October 2 at 20:00 to October 3 at 23:59.
- **General sale:** from October 7 at 20:00 (KST).
- **Overseas-resident Korean nationals:** if the passport verification shows an error, contact Melon Ticket support (1899-0042 or the 1:1 inquiry).

All of these dates have passed. Whether seats are left is something this guide cannot see. If you do not yet have a ticket, watch the Melon Ticket page for released seats, and treat any seat you can book as live inventory that may disappear.

For the general routine of Korean ticket sites (account setup, queues, identity checks), see our [K-pop concert ticketing guide](/posts/kpop-concert-ticketing-seoul-2026/).

## Cancellation Rules and Fees

These apply to this show per Melon Ticket:

- **Booking fee:** refundable only if you cancel before midnight on the booking day.
- **Cancellation fees:** none within 7 days of booking; up to KRW 4,000 per ticket (10% cap) from day 8 until 10 days before the show; then 10% (9 to 7 days before), 20% (6 to 3 days) and 30% (2 to 1 days).
- **Show-day booking:** cannot be cancelled, changed or refunded, nor can anything booked after the cancellation deadline.
- **Card refunds:** the refund method and timing depend on the card company. If you paid by bank transfer, the bank's late-night downtime can block a cancellation, so do not leave it to the last minute.

**Ticket delivery is not stated.** The listing text we read does not say whether this show uses paper delivery, on-site pickup or mobile tickets. Open your booking confirmation and the full show notice on Melon Ticket before January, and ask support at 1899-0042 if it is unclear, rather than finding out at the gate.

## Getting to KSPO DOME and Back

The venue facts below come from the operator-sourced details in our earlier guides.

- **Arrival.** The operator directs subway passengers to **Olympic Park Station (Lines 5 and 9), Exit 3**. The station is an arrival point, not proof you are at the right entrance, so follow the promoter's gate map inside the park. The [concert-day venue and transport guide](/posts/kpop-concert-day-venue-transport-guide-2026/) has the details.
- **Winter.** January evenings in Seoul are cold, and an arena queue means waiting outside. Bring layers, hand warmers and a charged phone, and plan where you will warm up before the doors open.
- **Return.** Everyone leaves at once after the show. Screenshot the route to your hotel before you go in, add time for merchandise and restroom queues, and note the last train you can still catch. The [KSPO Dome concert guide](/posts/kspo-dome-concert-survival-guide/) covers lockers, food timing and meeting points.
- **Driving.** Do not plan on parking. The operator's parking facts are in the venue guide, and an organizer can impose different controls on show days.
- **Backup.** Keep a taxi app and your hotel name in Korean ready in case you miss your train.
