# Can You Visit HYBE Yongsan? What's Actually There in 2026

A lot of fan itineraries still list HYBE's Yongsan headquarters as a stop with a museum and shop inside. That's out of date, and knowing it before you go saves a wasted trip.

## The building is office-only now

HYBE's Yongsan headquarters (Hangang-daero 42) is a working office facility. General public access — the lobby, any internal cafe, the basement level — is not permitted. There's no signage or process for walking in as a visitor.

## HYBE Insight closed here in 2023

The company's official museum-and-shop space, HYBE Insight, operated inside this building but closed there in January 2023. If your source is an older blog post or a fan itinerary from before that date, the museum it describes isn't there anymore.

## Where to actually go nearby

There's no official record store directly walkable from the HYBE building itself. The closest option is the music section on the **6th floor of Youngpoong Bookstore inside I'Park Mall at Yongsan Station**, roughly a 15-minute walk, which carries official albums.

For the full merchandise range — official lightsticks, photocards, and the widest store selection — Myeongdong and Hongdae's major retailers (Withmuu, Ktown4u) are the more complete stops rather than anything near the HYBE building itself.
