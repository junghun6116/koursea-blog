Chuncheon is Korea's dakgalbi capital, and its second signature dish, makguksu (cold buckwheat noodles), is just as essential. These 10 restaurants — sourced from verified local listings and cross-checked against Chuncheon's own tourism authority and Korean food media — cover the full range, from the restaurant that invented dakgalbi in 1961 to a modern burger shop that just placed in a national championship.

## Chuncheon's Essential Dakgalbi and Makguksu Restaurants

### 1. [Wonjo Suttbul Dak-bulgogi (원조숯불닭불고기)](https://www.koursea.com/place/2312/)

The birthplace of dakgalbi itself. Founded in 1961 by Kim Yeong-seok, this is documented by Korea's own cultural heritage archive (지역N문화) as the restaurant where dakgalbi was first developed in Chuncheon — originally gochujang-marinated chicken grilled over charcoal briquettes on a metal grate. Google rating: 4.2/5 (1,764 reviews).

### 2. [Tongnamujip Dakgalbi Main Store (통나무집닭갈비 본점)](https://www.koursea.com/place/1729/)

Chuncheon's best-known dakgalbi hall, in the Soyanggang/Sinbuk area near Soyang Dam — a regular stop for tour groups visiting the dam. Google rating: 4.2/5 (6,080 reviews, the most-reviewed restaurant in this list).

### 3. [Todam Charcoal Grilled Dakgalbi (토담숯불닭갈비)](https://www.koursea.com/place/2220/)

Repeatedly named by longtime Chuncheon residents as a go-to dakgalbi spot in local food-media roundups of native Chuncheon locals' personal picks, rather than tourist-guide lists. Google rating: 4.1/5 (3,727 reviews).

### 4. [1.5 Dakgalbi (1.5닭갈비)](https://www.koursea.com/place/1730/)

The highest Google rating of any restaurant on this list, in the same Soyanggang/Sinbuk cluster near the dam. Google rating: 4.4/5 (1,840 reviews).

### 5. [Sembat Makguksu (샘밭막국수 본점)](https://www.koursea.com/place/1733/)

Located in the Saembat area of Sinbuk-eup, this is one of Chuncheon's most established makguksu specialists, known for a clean, mild broth. Google rating: 4.1/5 (1,420 reviews).

### 6. [Yupori Makguksu (유포리막국수)](https://www.koursea.com/place/2295/)

Another Sinbuk-eup makguksu specialist with a loyal following distinct from Sembat's — Chuncheon locals typically pick a side between the two. Google rating: 4.2/5 (1,797 reviews).

### 7. [Wooseong Dakgalbi Main Store (우성닭갈비 본점)](https://www.koursea.com/place/2297/)

Located in the Mancheon-ri area outside the city center, drawing diners willing to travel for a quieter, more local dakgalbi experience away from the downtown alleys. Google rating: 4.2/5 (1,424 reviews).

### 8. [Namchon Makguksu (남촌막국수)](https://www.koursea.com/place/2326/)

In Geunhwa-dong, serving makguksu alongside gamjajeon (potato pancake) — a common pairing in this region. Google rating: 4.3/5 (732 reviews).

### 9. [Topgol Garden (탑골가든)](https://www.koursea.com/place/2346/)

A garden-style grilled-meat restaurant in Namsan-myeon, outside the main dakgalbi districts, popular for groups and outdoor seating. Google rating: 4.2/5 (1,193 reviews).

### 10. [Ramos Burger (라모스버거)](https://www.koursea.com/place/1741/)

A change of pace: this handmade-burger shop at 옛경춘로 835 placed 6th nationally at the 2026 Korea Burger Championship. Its signature Nagoya Burger is based on a recipe reported to have sold over 100,000 units in Nagoya, Japan, built on a fresh-baked daily bun, beef patty, egg, bacon, lettuce, and tomato. Google rating: 4.0/5 (908 reviews).
