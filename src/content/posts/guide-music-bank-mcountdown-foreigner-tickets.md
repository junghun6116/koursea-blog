## Music Bank and M Countdown Use Different Entry Systems

Music Bank and M Countdown are both weekly K-pop broadcasts, but their public audience routes are not the same. KBS maintains an official Music Bank audience-application page. Recent M Countdown participation notices are published for individual artists and episodes through official artist channels, including Mnet Plus.

For the separate Inkigayo, The Show and full fanclub pre-recording breakdown, read Koursea's [Inkigayo and The Show foreigner ticket guide](/posts/guide-kpop-music-show-foreigner-tickets-inkigayo-theshow/). For broader ticketing-platform mechanics (NOL World, Melon Ticket, payment issues foreign cards hit), see Koursea's [K-pop concert ticketing guide for Seoul](/posts/kpop-concert-ticketing-seoul-2026/).

## KBS Music Bank: Start With the Official KBS Page

### Venue and Schedule

- **Broadcaster:** KBS
- **Venue:** [KBS New Wing Open Hall](https://about.kbs.co.kr/index.html?sname=kbs&stype=location) (KBS 신관 공개홀), 13 Yeouigongwon-ro, Yeongdeungpo-gu, Seoul
- **Nearest station:** National Assembly Station, Seoul Subway Line 9, Exit 4
- **Current episode details:** Use the [official Music Bank audience list](https://kbsticket.kbs.co.kr/requestnew/list.do?m_seq=9); dates, times, venue and age rating are shown per episode

The live audience lottery and an artist's fanclub pre-recording are different routes. Check which session your notice covers before arranging transport.

### How to Check Whether You Can Apply

1. Open the [Music Bank audience list](https://kbsticket.kbs.co.kr/requestnew/list.do?m_seq=9) and select the episode you actually plan to attend.
2. Sign in with a KBS account. KBS's [general audience guide](https://kbsticket.kbs.co.kr/mobile/attend/totalGuide.do) says applications are limited to KBS members.
3. Read that episode's age, application, winner-announcement and identity-check instructions before submitting.
4. Enter the contact details requested by KBS and keep the confirmation available.
5. If selected, bring the exact identification required by the episode notice. Do not assume a passport alone will satisfy every notice.

The official pages checked on October 23, 2026 do **not** describe a separate, guaranteed foreigner-only route. International visitors should therefore test account eligibility early and rely on the live episode page rather than copied application schedules.

### Fanclub Pre-Recording Is a Separate Route

Music Bank can also have artist-specific pre-recordings. Those are separate from KBS's general audience page. Use the artist's current official notice and compare every required item with the [sa-nok preparation checklist](/posts/guide-kpop-music-show-foreigner-tickets-inkigayo-theshow/#mastering-the-weverse-sa-nok-requirements-zero-tolerance-rules).

### Is There a Paid Option?

Tour products can appear and disappear. Only treat one as usable when its live booking page names the program, date, inclusions, cancellation terms and identity requirements. An old search result or screenshot is not proof that seats remain available.

## Mnet M Countdown: Artist-Specific Notices

### Venue and Schedule

- **Broadcaster:** Mnet, operated by CJ ENM
- **Venue:** CJ ENM Center in Sangam-dong, Seoul
- **Broadcast:** Mnet Plus currently lists M Countdown on Thursday; confirm the episode in the app before traveling
- **Call and recording times:** Set by each week's notice

M Countdown is filmed at the **CJ ENM Center**, not KBS New Wing Open Hall. The KBS venue belongs to Music Bank. Do not use fan pages that mix the two locations.

A current official notice can set a check-in time hours apart from another week's notice. Always use the notice for the artist and episode you want to attend.

### How Foreign Fans Apply

M Countdown does not publish a KBS-style general audience page in the official sources checked for this update. Start with the performing artist's official notice. For example, an [October 8, 2026 Mnet Plus notice](https://artist.mnetplus.world/main/stg/flareu/surveys/6ac46b02e6af4f7feb43270d) required advance selection, official membership and release-specific purchase proof, and explicitly said there would be no on-site participation. That is evidence for that event only, not a permanent rule for every artist.

Mnet Plus is also used for M Countdown voting and related content, as described by [CJ ENM's newsroom](https://newsroom.cj.net/?p=9218), but that does not turn every Mnet Plus account into an audience ticket. Never travel to the studio expecting a passport-only walk-up unless the current official notice expressly permits it.

### Is There a Guaranteed Paid Package?

The official Mnet Plus and CJ ENM sources checked on October 23, 2026 do not present a general guaranteed-seat M Countdown product. A reseller offer should be treated as date-specific and unverified until its live page clearly identifies the episode and admission terms. For a separate program with different rules, see the [Inkigayo and The Show guide](/posts/guide-kpop-music-show-foreigner-tickets-inkigayo-theshow/).

## Studio Etiquette for Either Show

- Bring the exact original identification named in the current notice.
- Follow the week's official notice and staff instructions at the venue.
- Keep phones and cameras put away when recording is prohibited.
- Use the restroom and finish food or drinks before entering the studio.
- Do not expect re-entry after taping starts.

## Getting to the Correct Studio

Music Bank and M Countdown are not filmed in the same building. Before leaving, search the venue on [Koursea's Korea place lookup](https://www.koursea.com/) and use its Korean address card when showing the destination to a taxi driver. For Music Bank, use **KBS 신관 공개홀**. For M Countdown, use **CJ ENM Center in Sangam-dong**.

If you are taking the subway instead of a taxi, load your T-money balance before you go — see Koursea's [Seoul transit card guide](/posts/seoul-subway-transit-card-guide/) for T-money vs. Climate Card, since a stalled top-up is the last thing you want with a fixed application or recording time.
