# RIIZE Fan Guide to Seoul: BRIIZE Stops & KWANGYA@SEOUL

## Key Takeaways

- Visit KWANGYA@SEOUL at **Acro Seoul Forest D Tower B1, 83-21 Wangsimni-ro, Seongdong-gu**.
- Do not travel to the former SMTOWN Coex Artium; it closed in 2020.
- *RIIZING*, the fan-con tour era and the KSPO DOME finale are useful established milestones, not proof of a current event.
- Confirm pop-ups and purchase benefits through the newest official notice.

## The Official Seongsu Stop

KWANGYA@SEOUL is a public retail destination inside Acro Seoul Forest, while SM work areas are not fan attractions. Follow the mall's current signs to D Tower basement level 1. Inventory and event rules change quickly, so verify opening hours, eligible products and reservation requirements before making a special trip.

RIIZE and NCT 127 share this location because it is an SM retail hub. Creating a second map entity would imply two different venues and make directions less reliable, so Koursea links both guides to one verified place page.
