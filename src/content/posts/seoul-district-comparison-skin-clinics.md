# Gangnam vs Myeongdong vs Hongdae Skin Clinics (2026)

Seoul's three most practical clinic areas suit different itineraries. Gangnam has the deepest selection, Myeongdong fits easily into a central shopping day, and Hongdae now has enough public price listings to compare several common treatments—but not enough to treat every advertised number as a stable district average.

> **Price-method note:** The ranges below are planning benchmarks compiled from current, VAT-inclusive promotional listings and Koursea's existing district dataset. They are not guaranteed clinic quotes. Product, dose, shot count, device tip, physician involvement, anesthesia, and aftercare must match before two prices are comparable.

| Hongdae | Staying near Mapo and comparing youth-oriented, high-volume offers | Available at some clinics, but not safe to assume | Direct listings exist for six treatments; Ultherapy Prime remains an estimate |

## Gangnam: The Deepest Selection and Widest Price Range

Gangnam includes high-volume networks, private physician-led practices, and specialist clinics. That breadth creates more choice, but also makes headline prices difficult to compare. A low event price, a foreign-patient package, and a physician-designed plan may describe different inclusions even when the treatment name looks identical.

Choose Gangnam when treatment variety, doctor selection, or documented English coordination matters more than minimizing travel time.

## Myeongdong: Competitive and Easy to Add to a Shopping Day

Myeongdong remains competitive for standardized procedures such as forehead Botox, laser toning, and fixed-volume skin boosters. Its practical advantage is location: a short appointment can fit between shopping, dining, and central-Seoul sightseeing.

Tourist-facing service does not make every quote equivalent. Confirm whether the listed price includes VAT, the exact product or device, treatment quantity, anesthesia, and post-treatment care.

Hongdae can be a practical choice when you are already staying in Mapo. The main tradeoff is verification: do not infer English medical consultation from an English reservation page, and do not treat a first-visit promotion as the permanent price.

## How to Choose the District

- Choose **Gangnam** for the largest treatment range, physician choice, and the strongest chance of dedicated English coordination.
- Choose **Myeongdong** when convenience and a central shopping itinerary matter most.
- Choose **Hongdae** when you are staying nearby and can verify the exact product, quantity, device, English support, and aftercare before paying.

For the service-model decision after choosing a district, read [Factory-Style vs Private Clinic in Korea](/posts/how-to-choose-a-clinic-korea/).

## Before You Pay Anywhere

1. Ask whether the quoted total includes VAT: **이 가격에 부가세 포함인가요?**
2. Match the product, dose, device, shot count, treatment area, and aftercare before comparing two offers.
3. Confirm who conducts the consultation and who performs the procedure.
4. Use the clinic's official channel and request the final fixed total in writing.
5. Avoid price-quoting brokers or agencies that will not provide an itemized clinic-issued quote.

Korea's former tourist VAT refund for eligible cosmetic procedures ended in 2026. The Beauty pSEO records therefore keep `taxRefundEligible: false`; retail tax-refund rules for products are separate.
