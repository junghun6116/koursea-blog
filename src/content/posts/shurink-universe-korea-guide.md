# Shurink Universe in Korea 2026: HIFU Cost Guide

Shurink Universe is the Korean-market name associated with Classys's Ultraformer MPT focused-ultrasound platform. Korean clinics commonly position it as a lower-priced alternative to Ultherapy, but the two are different systems. “300 shots” alone does not make their treatment plans, energy delivery, imaging, indications, or outcomes equivalent.

This guide explains Shurink's Korean price tier, what to verify in a clinic, and how to interpret FDA language accurately instead of relying on “approved,” “registered,” or “certified” badges in an advertisement.

> **Medical disclaimer:** This article is for informational and travel-planning purposes only. Focused-ultrasound treatment is a medical procedure. Consult a licensed healthcare professional trained on the specific device for diagnosis, suitability, settings, expected results, risks, and aftercare.

## Key Takeaways

- Korean promotional pricing commonly falls around **₩150,000–₩297,000** for offers labeled as approximately 300 shots.
- Verify the exact Ultraformer MPT/Shurink Universe console, cartridge, treatment area, depth allocation, and tax-inclusive total.
- As of the FDA database update available on August 31, 2026, Koursea could not verify a U.S. 510(k) clearance specifically for a device named Shurink Universe or Ultraformer MPT.
- An IDE permits investigational use in a clinical study; it is not commercial clearance or approval.
- Ultherapy Prime's U.S. clearance and real-time DeepSEE imaging are factual differences, but they do not guarantee a better result for every patient.
- “Minimal downtime” does not mean no pain, swelling, bruising, nerve symptoms, or need for follow-up.

## What Shurink Universe Actually Is

Classys identifies Shurink Universe domestically and Ultraformer MPT internationally among its focused-ultrasound products. The platform delivers focused ultrasound using replaceable cartridges selected for different depths and treatment areas. A clinician should decide whether the proposed plan matches your anatomy and degree of laxity.

The term HIFU is widely used in clinic marketing, while regulatory documents may use “focused ultrasound.” Neither term tells you the shot distribution, energy setting, cartridge depth, or operator experience. Ask for the complete plan rather than choosing from a one-line menu.

Calling Shurink an “off-brand knockoff” is inaccurate and unhelpful. It is a named product from a publicly listed Korean medical-device manufacturer. That fact, however, does not prove a particular clinic uses an authentic console, compatible cartridges, appropriate settings, or a protocol suited to you.

## The Regulatory Status: What Can Be Said Reliably

The FDA explains that device establishment registration, listing, or a certificate does **not** mean a device is approved, cleared, or authorized. Commercial 510(k) clearance is also different from an Investigational Device Exemption, which permits an investigational device to be used in a clinical study to collect safety and effectiveness data.

Koursea searched the FDA's releasable 510(k) records available through August 31, 2026 and did not find a clearance specifically named Shurink Universe or Ultraformer MPT. The database does contain a different Classys focused-ultrasound product, SCIZER, under K230100; that record cannot be used as proof that Shurink Universe itself is cleared.

Therefore, a clinic should not describe Shurink Universe as “FDA-approved” based only on company registration, an IDE, or another product's clearance. Regulatory databases can change, so verify the exact device and model in the current FDA database when U.S. status affects your decision.

Claims about a targeted future clearance date are corporate plans, not regulatory outcomes. Do not state that clearance “will” arrive in Q1 2027 unless the FDA has actually issued a decision.

For Korean use, ask the clinic to show the exact local authorization and device labeling applicable to its console and indication. Do not use “KFDA,” “MFDS,” or “CE” as a vague marketing badge without a model-specific document.

## Shurink Universe vs. Ultherapy Prime

### Imaging

Ultherapy Prime includes real-time DeepSEE ultrasound visualization. Shurink Universe does not use the same Ultherapy imaging workflow. Imaging can help a trained practitioner view tissue layers, but it does not eliminate operator judgment or guarantee a superior result.

### U.S. regulatory record

The Ulthera PRIME control unit has a specific FDA 510(k) clearance record. Koursea could not verify an equivalent U.S. clearance specifically for Shurink Universe/Ultraformer MPT as of the date above. This distinction matters when a clinic markets the devices using FDA language.

### Price

Shurink generally occupies the lower Korean promotional tier. Ultherapy Prime offers around 300 lines can range far higher. The price gap reflects different platforms, consumables, market positioning, clinical plans, and package structures—not a controlled head-to-head promise that one will always produce a particular percentage of improvement.

## Downtime and Side Effects

Koursea uses a general **zero-to-two-day** planning window for common redness, tenderness, or mild swelling. Clinic marketing often calls the procedure minimal downtime, but individual responses, cartridge depth, energy, shot density, and treatment area vary. Bruising, soreness, tingling, numbness, or tenderness may last longer.

Follow the treating clinician's instructions about exercise, heat, alcohol, massage, skincare, and pain medicine. Seek prompt medical assessment for severe or worsening pain, burns, marked weakness, persistent numbness, facial asymmetry, blistering, visual symptoms, or any reaction that worries you.

## Flying and Travel Planning
