# CORTIS Fan Guide: COER Membership, Official Music & Concert Prep

CORTIS is a five-member group under BIGHIT MUSIC. The group debuted on August 18, 2025, and its official profile lists MARTIN, JAMES, JUHOON, SEONGHYEON and KEONHO. Its official fandom name is **COER**. This guide keeps travel planning tied to official CORTIS, BIGHIT MUSIC and Weverse notices instead of rumored schedules or unofficial visitor locations.

## Key Takeaways for COER

- Use the official CORTIS profile, Weverse community and BIGHIT MUSIC pages as the source of truth for releases, membership and public schedules.
- The fandom name is **COER**, as announced through the official Weverse notice.
- CORTIS does not have a dedicated public visitor facility in Seoul.
- HYBE's Yongsan headquarters is a working office, and the former HYBE INSIGHT museum permanently closed on January 15, 2023. Do not plan a museum visit there.
- For concerts, verify membership, ticket-account identity and payment details before the sale opens.

## Who Is CORTIS?

BIGHIT MUSIC describes CORTIS as its newest boy band. The official group profile lists five members: MARTIN, JAMES, JUHOON, SEONGHYEON and KEONHO. Their debut material emphasizes the members' participation in music, choreography and visual production rather than presenting them only as performers.

The official debut release, *COLOR OUTSIDE THE LINES*, includes five tracks: “GO!”, “What You Want”, “FaSHioN”, “JoyRide” and “Lullaby”. BIGHIT MUSIC's album page credits all five members with participation in the album's production. Use the official release page for credits and track information; streaming milestones reported by fan accounts should not be treated as verified records without a primary source.

## What Does COER Mean for Fans?

The official Weverse announcement established **COER** as the CORTIS fandom name. Fans should use that spelling when searching for official membership, event and ticket notices. Similar-looking account names, resale listings or unofficial fan packages do not establish an official benefit.

When a membership benefit is offered, read the event-specific Weverse notice from beginning to end. Membership eligibility, application windows, identity checks and pickup rules can differ between tours and promotions. A membership purchase by itself does not guarantee a ticket, event entry or merchandise allocation.

## Visiting Seoul as a CORTIS Fan

CORTIS currently has no dedicated museum, permanent exhibition or public agency shop that travelers should build an itinerary around. HYBE's headquarters in Yongsan is an active workplace rather than a visitor attraction. HYBE INSIGHT permanently closed on January 15, 2023, and no reopening at the headquarters has been announced as of September 2026.

Avoid travel plans built around artist offices, dorm rumors, airport sightings or unannounced schedules. For an activity-based fan day, choose public record shops, officially announced pop-ups and ticketed venues. Confirm temporary events through Weverse, BIGHIT MUSIC or the named venue before crossing the city, because dates and entry systems can change quickly.

## CORTIS Concert and Event Preparation

Official CORTIS notices have included tour and fan-benefit information, but every event can use different rules. Work from the notice for the exact city and date you are attending.

Before ticketing:

1. Create and verify the required ticketing and Weverse accounts.
2. Match the account and membership name to the passport you will carry.
3. Check whether the sale requires membership presale registration before the ticket queue opens.
4. Test the accepted payment method and keep a backup card ready.
5. Save the organizer's venue, pickup and prohibited-items notice offline.

## Respectful Fan Travel Checklist

- Follow only official public schedules.
- Do not wait at agency entrances, hotels, airports or rumored private locations.
- Confirm temporary pop-ups on the organizer's own channel before traveling.
- Keep passport, membership and ticket-account names consistent.
- Treat venue and merchandise rules as event-specific, not permanent.
