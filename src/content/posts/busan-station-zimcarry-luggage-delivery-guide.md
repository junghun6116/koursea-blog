# Busan Station Luggage Delivery to Haeundae: ZimCarry Cutoff and Pricing

If you're arriving in Busan and heading straight to the beach, dragging suitcases through Haeundae before check-in is avoidable. Same-day luggage delivery services operating out of Busan Station handle the transfer for you.

## Where to find the counter

The delivery counter sits on the **1st floor of Busan Station**, near Exit 5 and the central information desk — easy to spot as soon as you come off the platform.

## The cutoff time matters more than the price

Same-day delivery to your hotel runs on a strict cutoff — drop your bags too late in the afternoon and delivery pushes to the next day instead. That exact cutoff time isn't fixed across every booking channel: it can differ slightly between paying directly at the counter and booking through a third-party platform like Klook, KKday, or Trazy. Confirm the current cutoff with staff the moment you arrive, rather than planning your whole day around a number from an old blog post.

## Pricing varies by size and zone

Delivery fees scale with luggage size, and outlying delivery zones outside central Haeundae — Gijang or Songjeong, for example — can carry an extra charge on top of the base rate. Ask for the total price for your exact bag count and destination before handing anything over, since quoted rates can shift between the counter and third-party booking apps.

## What happens after drop-off

You'll register your name, passport details, and destination hotel at the counter. Most services send a confirmation once your bags reach the hotel's storage area, and you collect them at check-in by showing your passport and claim tag.
