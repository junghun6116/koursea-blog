NCT WISH's second concert tour, **INTO THE WISH: Face Our WISH**, comes to **Gocheok Sky Dome** in Seoul on **January 15 and 16, 2027**. The sale is already underway, so the useful questions now are: what does the ticket page require, how does the mobile ticket work, and how do you get home from an evening dome show in winter?

Everything about dates, prices and rules below is from the official Melon Ticket listing, read on November 5, 2026. The organizers are SM Entertainment and Dream Maker Entertainment Limited. Rules can change, so reread the listing before you pay.

## Show Details From Melon Ticket

| | Day 1 | Day 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Date | Fri, Jan 15, 2027 | Sat, Jan 16, 2027 |
| Start (KST) | 18:00 | 17:00 |

- **Venue:** Gocheok Sky Dome (고척스카이돔), Guro-gu, Seoul.
- **Prices:** VIP KRW 220,000; general KRW 165,000.
- **Booking fee:** KRW 2,000 per ticket (KRW 3,000 per ticket by phone).
- **Age:** 7 and up. The listing does not state a running time.

## How the Sale Worked

Melon Ticket published this schedule:

| Step | Domestic page | Global page |
|---|---|---|
| Fan-club verification | Oct 19 14:00 – Nov 2 23:59 | Oct 19 14:00 – Nov 3 23:59 |
| Fan-club presale | Nov 2, 20:00–23:59 | Nov 3, 20:00–23:59 |
| General sale | Nov 4, 20:00 (KST) onward | Nov 4, 20:00 (KST) onward |

- **Presale** is for members of the official fan club, NCTzen WISH, at the GL membership level only, with 1 ticket per person per show.
- **General sale:** up to 2 tickets per person per show, counting any presale tickets.
- **Wheelchair seats** have their own presale and general sale times; see the listing.
- **Payment: credit card only.** The listing says KakaoPay, phone payment and bank transfer are restricted for this show. Foreign cards often work on Korean ticket sites but can fail, so test your card and keep a second one ready.

For general ticketing mechanics for visitors (accounts, queues, identity checks), see our [K-pop concert ticketing guide](/posts/kpop-concert-ticketing-seoul-2026/).

## Mobile Ticket Only: What That Means

This is the most useful part of the listing for a visitor.

- **No paper ticket, no delivery, no on-site pickup.** The ticket exists only as a mobile ticket in the **Melon Ticket app** (My Ticket → Booking history → Mobile ticket).
- **Install and log in before you travel.** You cannot show the mobile ticket from anywhere except the app.
- **Open it before you reach the dome.** Melon warns that data traffic around a packed venue can make the ticket slow to load, so open it at your hotel or a nearby café first.
- **Gifting works for two tickets.** If you book 2, the gift function sends 1 ticket to your companion's app, and each person must hold their own ticket to enter. Sort this out before the day, with a companion who also has the app.
- **View limits.** The listing says some seats, for example near railings, may have a blocked view from safety structures, stage production, cameras or nearby audience members. Those are not refundable or changeable on the day.

## Cancellation Rules

- **Same-day cancel:** cancelling before 23:59:59 on the booking day refunds everything including the booking fee.
- **After that:** the booking fee is not refunded.
- **Fees** (per Melon Ticket): none within 7 days of booking; up to KRW 4,000 per ticket (10% cap) from day 8 until 10 days before; then 10% (9 to 7 days before), 20% (6 to 3 days) and 30% (2 to 1 days).
- **Show-day booking:** cannot be cancelled, changed or refunded.

## Getting to Gocheok Sky Dome and Back

Winter evening, thousands of people, one station. Plan this before the ticket:

- **Arrival.** The operator-sourced route is **Guil Station on Line 1, Exit 2**. The dome is at **430 Gyeongin-ro, Guro-gu**. See the [concert-day venue and transport guide](/posts/kpop-concert-day-venue-transport-guide-2026/) for the full Gocheok notes.
- **Parking.** The operator says organizers can rent the whole parking facility for events, so do not plan around parking. Use the subway.
- **Line 1 return.** Line 1 can have wider late-night gaps than central Seoul lines. Identify your direction and your last transfer in advance, and note the last train you can still catch.
- **Meeting point.** Pick a specific named landmark before entering. If your group splits, wait there rather than moving against the exiting crowd.
- **January cold.** An evening dome show means waiting outside in the cold. Bring layers, hand warmers and a charged phone, since your ticket is on it.
