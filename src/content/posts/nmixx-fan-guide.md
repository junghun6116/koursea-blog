# NMIXX Seoul Guide: JYP Center, NSWER & Comeback Prep

NMIXX debuted on February 22, 2022 under JYP Entertainment with the single "O.O." The group is currently six members — Lily, Haewon, Sullyoon, Bae, Jiwoo and Kyujin — after Jinni departed the lineup on December 9, 2022. Their official fandom name is **NSWER**. This guide keeps travel planning tied to JYP's official channels rather than rumored schedules or unofficial visitor locations.

## Key Takeaways for NSWER

- The fandom name is **NSWER** (North, South, East, West, Route), announced through JYP's official channels shortly after debut.
- JYP Center, the agency's Seoul headquarters, is a working office — plan for a brief respectful exterior stop only, not a visitor facility.
- The most recently confirmed release as of this guide is the fifth mini album *Heavy Serenade* (May 11, 2026). Recheck official notices for anything more recent before you travel.
- Use JYP's official artist page and Weverse-style notices as the source of truth for schedules, not fan-account reposts.
- For concerts, verify membership, ticket-account identity and payment details before the sale opens.

## JYP Center: Current Location and Visit Rules

JYP Entertainment's headquarters — used by all of the label's artists, including NMIXX — is officially listed at 205 Gangdong-daero, Gangdong-gu, Seoul (성내동). Gangdong-gu Office Station on Subway Line 8 is the closest practical approach, roughly a 500-meter walk; Dunchon-dong Station on Line 5 is a longer alternative. Confirm the live walking route and station exit status on the day rather than relying on an old fan-blog map.

Treat a visit as a 10-to-20-minute exterior stop only. The building is a working office, not a museum or public NMIXX facility: photograph the exterior from public sidewalks, keep doors and driveways clear, and do not wait at entrances or film staff, artists or other visitors. A parked vehicle or a small crowd outside is not confirmation of an unannounced schedule.

## Current Activity Context for NSWER

NMIXX's most recently confirmed release is the fifth mini album *Heavy Serenade*, which came out May 11, 2026 with six tracks led by the title track, alongside songs including "Crescendo" and "Different Girl." Treat this as the latest confirmed activity at the time this guide was written — check NMIXX's official page and JYP's notices directly for anything announced since, since release schedules can move quickly.

For merchandise, compare the exact product name, official seller and benefit period against the current JYP or Weverse Shop listing before paying. A retailer carrying other licensed K-pop goods is not proof that every individual listing or photocard is official.

## NMIXX Concert and Fan-Event Ticketing

When a Seoul concert, fan meeting or fan-sign is officially announced, work from that event's own notice rather than a general schedule you saw elsewhere — presale eligibility, identity-matching rules and pickup instructions can differ from one event to the next.

Before ticketing:

1. Create and verify the required ticketing account (and membership account, if the event uses one).
2. Match the account and membership name to the passport you will carry.
3. Check whether membership presale registration is required before the general ticket queue opens.
4. Test your payment method in advance and keep a backup card ready.
5. Save the venue address, entry rules and transport plan offline for event day.

## Respectful Fan Travel Checklist

- Confirm the current JYP Center address and treat it as a brief exterior stop, not a destination in itself.
- Do not wait at agency entrances, hotels, airports or rumored private locations.
- Confirm any pop-up or fan event through JYP's own official channel before traveling to it.
- Keep passport, membership and ticket-account names consistent for any event with identity checks.
- Recheck official sources close to your trip — schedules, venues and benefit periods can change quickly.
