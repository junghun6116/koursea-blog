# A Suwon Day Trip: Hwaseong Fortress, Haenggung-dong & Lovely Runner's Filming Cafe

Suwon's Haenggung-dong neighborhood, built around the UNESCO World Heritage Hwaseong Fortress, is a genuine day trip from Seoul on its own — and it also happens to hold the real cafe used in "Lovely Runner" (선재 업고 튀어). The photo above shows the fortress itself as general area context; it is not a still from the drama. This guide focuses on what's actually sourced: the fortress's official hours and fees, how to get there, where to stay, and a reminder to respect private property along the way.

For other sourced filming locations around Seoul, see Koursea's [10 K-Drama & K-Pop Filming Locations Near Seoul](/posts/kdrama-kpop-filming-locations-seoul-2026/). This guide goes deep on a single day trip instead.

## The Lovely Runner Connection, and Its Limits

Korean entertainment outlet [TF News reported a wave of fans visiting and photographing Haenggung-dong filming spots](https://news.tf.co.kr/read/ogmeta/2099841.htm) after "Lovely Runner" aired, and [WikiTree's roundup of Suwon drama-filming hotspots](https://www.wikitree.co.kr/articles/1028829) names a cafe in the same neighborhood as a key stop. Fan-community posts, including one on [fanmaum.com](https://fanmaum.com/community/Lovely-Runner/45700353), place that cafe — commonly referred to as Monte-d — at an address on Hwaseomun-ro in Haenggung-dong. None of these is the cafe's own official page, so treat the exact address, floor, and opening hours as unconfirmed until you check a live map listing or the venue's own social account.

> **Respect private property.** Fan reporting on this neighborhood references at least one real, occupied private residence associated with an on-screen scene. Koursea does not publish its location. Do not seek out, approach, photograph, or linger at any private home connected to a filming location — here or anywhere else in Haenggung-dong.

Beyond this one cafe, Koursea could not independently confirm which specific scenes, if any, were filmed inside Hwaseong Fortress or Haenggung Palace themselves for this drama. Treat the fortress as the day trip's main destination in its own right, not as an extension of the filming-location stop.

## Getting There from Seoul

Koursea could not independently confirm which specific trains or subway lines currently connect Seoul to Suwon Station for this guide, so check Korail or a map app such as Naver Map or Kakao Map for the current rail and subway options before you travel. From Suwon Station, Haenggung-dong and Hwaseong Fortress require a further bus or taxi ride — the station is not within walking distance of the fortress area.

## Where to Stay

For a single day trip, most visitors base themselves in Seoul and travel to Suwon and back the same day by rail — check Korail or a map app for the current options. If you'd rather stay overnight, lodging clusters near Suwon Station itself (better onward transit) and within walking distance of Haenggung-dong (closer to the fortress and the cafe street, but fewer large hotel options). Koursea's [Suwon Haenggung-dong Cafe Street guide](https://www.koursea.com/suwon-haenggung-dong-cafe-street-hanok-courtyards-pastries.html) covers the immediate neighborhood if you're staying inside it.

## Hwaseong Fortress and Haenggung Palace: Sourced Hours and Fees

The Suwon Cultural Foundation's [official Haenggung Palace page](https://www.swcf.or.kr/?p=65) lists daytime visiting hours as **09:00–18:00 year-round**. The same page lists a seasonal **night-opening** period from **May 1 to November 1, 2026**, on **Fridays, weekends, and public holidays**, with entry from 18:00 and closing at 21:30. Admission fees apply for both the daytime and night visits; check the current fee schedule on the same official page rather than relying on a figure quoted elsewhere. These are the hours and dates as read from the Suwon Cultural Foundation's own page at this guide's verification date — confirm the live listing before you go, since schedules can still change season to season. Koursea's [Hwaseong Fortress Night Tour guide](https://www.koursea.com/hwaseong-fortress-night-tour-illuminated-ramparts-secret-night-gates.html) covers the illuminated night-walk experience itself in more detail.

If your interest is a different drama's Suwon filming spots, Koursea separately covers [Extraordinary Attorney Woo's filming locations](https://www.koursea.com/extraordinary-attorney-woo-filming-spots-suwon-ginkgo-tree-kimbap-shop.html) — a different part of the city, not inside Haenggung-dong.

## Food and Cafes Beyond Monte-d

Haengridan-gil — a nickname [Dailian's travel feature](https://www.dailian.co.kr/news/view/1476852/) traces to a blend of Seoul's "Gyeongridan-gil" and "Haenggung-dong" — has grown into a cafe-and-hanok-alley district on its own, independent of any one drama. Koursea's dedicated [cafe street guide](https://www.koursea.com/suwon-haenggung-dong-cafe-street-hanok-courtyards-pastries.html) lists hanok-courtyard cafes and pastry shops in the immediate area if Monte-d has a wait.
