# Rejuran Healer in Korea 2026: Cost & What It Does

Rejuran Healer is an injectable polynucleotide, or PN, treatment marketed for skin quality rather than structural volume replacement or muscle relaxation. That makes it different from hyaluronic-acid filler and botulinum toxin. Clinics commonly discuss texture, hydration, elasticity, and fine lines, but travelers should understand that the evidence base is still developing and that results are not guaranteed.

This guide explains how to compare Korean clinic quotes on the same 2cc basis, what recovery can look like, and which medical and travel questions to settle before paying.

## What Rejuran Healer Is—and Is Not

Rejuran Healer belongs to the broad category often called “skin boosters.” Its polynucleotide material is injected into the skin in many small points. It is not Botox: it does not work by reducing muscle activity. It is also not a conventional volumizing hyaluronic-acid filler intended to reshape an area.

A 2025 systematic review found promising findings for wrinkles, texture, and elasticity, with generally mild and transient reported side effects. However, it included only nine studies and 219 patients, rated the evidence as low to moderate quality, and noted variation in treatment areas and injection techniques. A newer review of randomized trials similarly found encouraging results but emphasized small samples, heterogeneous protocols, and the need for larger studies.

That means “regeneration,” “glass skin,” or guaranteed collagen claims should not be treated as settled outcomes for every patient. Ask the clinician what improvement is realistic for your skin and how it will be assessed.

The phrase “full face” does not define a dose. It may mean 2cc at one clinic and several syringes or a combination treatment at another. A ₩500,000 package containing more product cannot fairly be compared with a ₩180,000 single-dose promotion.

## Pain Control and Injection Method

Multiple superficial injections can be uncomfortable. Clinics may use topical numbing cream, cooling, a device injector, hand injection, or a combination. Pain varies by area, technique, and individual sensitivity.

Do not choose a clinic solely because it promises “painless” treatment. Ask how long numbing is applied, which areas will be treated, how the dose is distributed, and whether pain-management add-ons change the price. Tell the clinician about allergies, medicines, bleeding tendency, pregnancy or breastfeeding, active skin infection, and previous reactions before treatment.

## Downtime: Expect Visible Injection Points

Small raised bumps at injection sites commonly make the first day visually obvious. Redness, tenderness, swelling, or pinpoint bruising may also occur. Many bumps settle within 24–48 hours, but Koursea uses a broader one-to-three-day planning window because individual reactions and protocols differ.

Follow the clinic's written instructions on makeup, cleansing, exercise, alcohol, sauna, swimming, sun exposure, and active skincare. “Avoid sauna for a week” is a common clinic instruction, not a universal rule for every patient; the treating clinician's protocol takes priority.

Schedule professional photographs, weddings, performances, or other appearance-sensitive events after the visible marks have had time to settle.

## Flying After Rejuran

There is no universal aviation standard proving that every patient is medically safe to fly exactly 24 hours after PN injections. Koursea uses **one full day as a minimum logistics and appearance buffer**. It gives you time to monitor swelling, contact the treating clinic, and avoid going directly from the procedure chair to a long-haul departure.

Your clinician may advise a different interval based on your health, symptoms, treatment area, injection method, and flight duration. Read [Can You Fly After a Skin Procedure in Korea?](/posts/flying-after-skin-procedure-korea/) before placing the appointment near departure day.

## When to Contact a Clinician Urgently

Seek prompt medical assessment for severe or rapidly worsening pain, spreading redness, fever, pus, extensive swelling, breathing difficulty, widespread hives, visual symptoms, pale or dusky skin, or neurological symptoms. Do not assume every reaction is routine “downtime,” and do not wait for a broker or social-media account to answer an urgent question.

Before leaving the clinic, save its direct and after-hours contact details. Ask what symptoms require a photo message, same-day return, or emergency care.
