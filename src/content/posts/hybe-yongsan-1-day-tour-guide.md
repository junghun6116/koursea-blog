# HYBE Yongsan 1-Day Fan Tour: Headquarters, Cafes & Respectful Photo Stops

HYBE's Yongsan headquarters is a working office, not a walk-in attraction. The right fan itinerary treats it as a short exterior landmark, then uses Yongsan's excellent rail connections for public cultural stops, cafés and the Han River.

## Quick Route

1. Arrive at Sinyongsan Station on Line 4.
2. Visit the [HYBE Headquarters place page](https://www.koursea.com/place/hybe-headquarters-yongsan/) and open the verified Korean address in Naver Map.
3. Take exterior photos from public pavement without blocking doors or vehicles.
4. Continue to Yongsan Station for a meal or café break.
5. Spend the afternoon at the National Museum of Korea or Yongsan Family Park.
6. Finish at a public Han River viewpoint, then return before the late-night service interval grows.

## HYBE Headquarters: What a Visitor Can Actually Do

Use `서울특별시 용산구 한강대로 42` as the navigation query. Sinyongsan Station is usually the simplest subway approach; Yongsan Station is also walkable and useful if you arrive by Line 1, the Gyeongui–Jungang Line or KTX.

Stay on public paths. Do not enter employee areas, wait at vehicle exits, photograph staff at close range or treat a security-controlled lobby as a fan zone. Older posts about exhibitions inside the building may no longer reflect current operations. A temporary official exhibition should be confirmed from HYBE or the organizer before you build a day around it.

## Fan Cafes Without Chasing an Outdated List

Birthday and comeback cafés are temporary. Search the artist name plus `생일카페` or `생카` on the organizer's current social account, then verify the dates, queue rules and purchase minimum. A fan-run event is not an official artist schedule, and its gifts can sell out early.

Keep one flexible café slot rather than pinning three unverified venues. If the event moves or closes, Yongsan Station's connected commercial areas give you a reliable weather-proof alternative.

## A Balanced Afternoon in Yongsan

The National Museum of Korea is a strong public stop because it does not depend on celebrity rumors or private addresses. Check current gallery hours and reservation notices on the museum's official site. Nearby park paths make a calm transition after the headquarters area.

For sunset, choose a legal public riverside space and check the final walking route in Naver Map. Do not use a fan-made “member residence” map. Private homes, hotels and unannounced travel movements are outside a respectful itinerary.

## Concert Preparation Before the Tour

If the headquarters visit is paired with a concert, complete platform registration, membership matching and payment setup days in advance. Read the [K-Pop concert ticketing guide](/posts/kpop-concert-ticketing-seoul-2026/) for NOL World, foreign-card and identity-check issues. Large shows can overload mobile networks. Agree on a fixed meeting point, carry a physical transit card and keep a power bank. Do not assume the last train or venue shuttle is the same for every event.
