# NCT 127 Fan Guide to Seoul: NCTzen Stops & KWANGYA@SEOUL

NCT 127 is an SM Entertainment group whose official fandom name is **NCTzen**. For a fan visiting Seoul, the useful official public stop is [KWANGYA@SEOUL](https://www.koursea.com/place/sm-kwangya-seoul-forest/) in Seongsu—not an old Coex itinerary and not a private office entrance.

## Key Takeaways

- KWANGYA@SEOUL is at **B1, D Tower, Acro Seoul Forest, 83-21 Wangsimni-ro, Seongdong-gu**.
- SMTOWN Coex Artium closed completely in May–June 2020 and is no longer an SM visitor destination.
- Treat SM offices as workplaces and use only the public retail space announced for visitors.
- NCT 127's sixth full album *WALK* belongs to the group's established activity history; check current SM notices for any new event.
- Use the [Foreigner's Guide to K-Pop Concert Ticketing in Seoul](/posts/kpop-concert-ticketing-seoul-2026/) before a ticket sale.

## Visit KWANGYA@SEOUL in Seongsu

Enter Acro Seoul Forest's D Tower and follow the building's current signs to basement level 1. Store hours, purchase benefits, stock and event entry rules can change, so check the latest KWANGYA or SM notice on the day you travel. If a release benefit matters to you, confirm the branch, eligible album version and event period before paying.

Do not use old posts directing NCTzen to SMTOWN Coex Artium. That venue closed in 2020 and the former space is not a current SM attraction. A page, map pin or video that still calls it an operating SMTOWN venue is stale.

## Build a Practical Seongsu Fan Day

Keep the official stop as one part of a public Seongsu itinerary. Seoul Forest offers space to rest, and the surrounding district has cafés, shops and pop-ups. Temporary artist events require their own official notice; their dates should never be inferred from an old social post.

Avoid waiting near private office access points or following vehicles. Public retail and announced events are the appropriate fan-travel route.

## Concert Preparation

Start with the performance organizer's notice. Confirm the seller, NCTzen membership presale steps, account-name format, passport matching, pickup policy and accepted payment methods. Finish account verification before the queue opens and save the venue address and final train route offline.

## Quick Answers

- **Can I still visit SMTOWN Coex Artium?** No. It closed in 2020 and should not be used as an NCT 127 destination.
- **Where is the current official public stop?** KWANGYA@SEOUL at Acro Seoul Forest D Tower B1.
- **Can I enter SM's offices?** Do not assume office access. Use the designated public store and announced event spaces only.
