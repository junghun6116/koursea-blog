# TWS Fan Guide to Seoul: 42 Concert & PLEDIS Planning

PLEDIS Entertainment's [official fan-club announcement](https://weverse.io/tws/notice/18538) names the TWS fandom **42 (SAI)** and explains that the name describes fans who are with TWS 24/7. Current TWS notices are published through the group's official Weverse channel.

This guide covers announced public activities. PLEDIS and HYBE offices are workplaces, not visitor attractions, and no lobby access, tour, or artist encounter should be assumed.

## Key takeaways

- Use current PLEDIS and TWS notices for events, tickets, merchandise, and membership.
- Treat every concert, fan meeting, showcase, and booth as date-specific.
- Do not visit offices, studios, hotels, vehicles, or private schedules.
- Prepare the exact membership, identification, payment, and return route before ticket day.

## What can 42 verify before a Seoul visit?

Start with a current notice that identifies the city, venue, date, organizer, and sales channel. PLEDIS's [2026 Seoul fan-meeting announcement](https://weverse.io/tws/notice/33440) named ticketLINK LIVE ARENA, three March show dates, membership pre-verification, presale, and general sale. Those dates have passed, so the notice is a verified example—not a current invitation.

The [2026 tour booth notice](https://weverse.io/tws/notice/36837) likewise documented a 42:ZONE at KSPO DOME that required a same-day mobile concert ticket, physical identification, and a 42 GLOBAL MEMBERSHIP QR code. Those rules applied to the named June dates only. Recheck every requirement for a future event.

As of September 8, the official channel is also taking pre-orders for a TWS **VR Concert Box** through Weverse Shop (the listed window ends September 20). See the [official notice](https://weverse.io/tws/notice/38997) for the current product and dates; this is a home-release announcement, not a Seoul venue event.

## Is the PLEDIS or HYBE office a visitor stop?

No. A corporate address does not create public access. Do not wait at entrances, photograph employees, follow vehicles, or infer private schedules. If a public pop-up or exhibition is announced, visit only the stated venue during its published operating period.

A practical Seoul route pairs one confirmed public activity with nearby parks, cafes, or an established album retailer. The day remains useful if admission is capped or goods sell out.

## Concert preparation for overseas 42

Use only the ticket seller linked by PLEDIS or the organizer. Our [Foreigner's Guide to K-Pop Concert Ticketing in Seoul](/posts/kpop-concert-ticketing-seoul-2026/) covers passport-name matching, fan-club presales, overseas payment checks, ticket pickup, and post-show transport.

Before booking:

- confirm whether the event accepts 42 GLOBAL, Digital, or another membership type;
- match the ticket account name to the required identification;
- save the venue address and final train or bus route offline;
- read the current light-stick, recording, bag, and merchandise rules;
- keep the official notice available at entry rather than relying on a screenshot summary.

## Official merchandise and fan etiquette

HYBE's [official 2026 merchandise notice](https://weverse.io/tws/notice/34413) said that merchandise for the named fan meeting was sold only at its venue booth and on Weverse. Apply that verification method to future events: use the official sales channel, confirm the product and benefit period, and do not treat copied promotional images as authorization.

Attend announced concerts, broadcasts, fan meetings, and public pop-ups. Airports, hotels, offices, vehicles, and unannounced movements are not sightseeing stops.
