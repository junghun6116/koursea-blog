# BIGBANG & G-Dragon Fan Guide: BiiiG, XX : COSMOS & Seoul Prep

BIGBANG debuted in 2006, and its official fandom is **V.I.P**. The group is active in 2026 as a three-member lineup of G-Dragon, Taeyang and Daesung. T.O.P publicly confirmed his departure from the group in 2023 and is not part of the current comeback or tour lineup. This guide separates BIGBANG's group production relationship with YG Entertainment from each member's individual agency.

## Key Takeaways for V.I.P

- BIGBANG returned as a three-member group with the digital single “BiiiG” on August 19, 2026.
- The 20th-anniversary world tour *XX : COSMOS* opened at Goyang Stadium on August 21–23, 2026.
- YG is the group's concert and production partner for this project, but the members do not share one individual management contract.
- G-Dragon is represented by Galaxy Corporation, Taeyang by THEBLACKLABEL and Daesung by R&D Company.
- YG headquarters is a historic reference point, not a public BIGBANG museum or proof that the members currently work there day to day.

## The 20th-Anniversary Comeback

YG's official announcement describes “BiiiG” as BIGBANG's first new song in four years. It was released at 6 p.m. KST on August 19, 2026, the group's 20th debut anniversary. G-Dragon participated in its writing and composition.

The anniversary project continued with three sold-out Goyang Stadium concerts from August 21 to 23. YG's official tour site lists *BIGBANG 2026–2027 WORLD TOUR <XX : COSMOS>* dates extending into 2027. Its announced scale is 19 cities and 33 performances, but fans should use the live tour page for additions, cancellations and local ticket links rather than copying a fixed itinerary from this article.

## BIGBANG Is Currently a Three-Member Group

The active lineup is G-Dragon, Taeyang and Daesung. T.O.P stated in 2023 that he had left BIGBANG and is not included in the 2026 three-member comeback. This is a lineup fact, not an invitation to revisit controversies or speculate about private relationships.

Older promotional photos, merchandise listings and search snippets may still show previous lineups. For a 2026 concert, rely on the current organizer poster and official event notice.

## Understanding the Agency Structure

BIGBANG's 20th-anniversary group activities involve YG as a concert and production partner. That does not mean each member has returned to YG under an individual exclusive contract.

- **G-Dragon:** Galaxy Corporation.
- **Taeyang:** THEBLACKLABEL.
- **Daesung:** R&D Company.

This distinction matters when following solo releases, fan meetings and brand activity. Use the relevant member's current agency for individual schedules, then use YG's BIGBANG pages for the group tour and group releases.

## Why G-Dragon Can Keep His Stage Name

In 2024, YG transferred the trademarks for “G-Dragon” and “GD” to Galaxy Corporation without payment. Both Galaxy and YG confirmed the transfer to Yonhap. The transfer allows G-Dragon to continue using the established stage name in solo activity after his individual YG contract ended; it should not be misread as a new exclusive contract with YG.

## Is YG Headquarters a BIGBANG Attraction?

BIGBANG has a long history with YG, but [YG Entertainment headquarters in Hapjeong](https://www.koursea.com/place/yg-entertainment-hq-hapjeong/) remains a secure workplace. It has no general fan-tour access. Photograph only from public space and do not block entrances or wait for artists.

The visitor-facing alternative across the street is [the SameE Hapjeong](https://www.koursea.com/place/the-samee-hapjeong/), where YG has held official artist-anniversary café events and sold official merchandise. Current stock and event benefits are never guaranteed; check the latest official notice before making a dedicated trip.

## XX : COSMOS Ticket and Travel Checklist

Tour dates cross multiple ticketing companies and local rules. Prepare from the notice for your exact city:

1. Confirm the current date and venue on the official tour site.
2. Create the named ticketing account before presale day.
