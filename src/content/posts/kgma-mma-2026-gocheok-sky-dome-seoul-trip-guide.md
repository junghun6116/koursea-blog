# KGMA & MMA 2026 at Gocheok Sky Dome: Planning a Seoul Trip Around Both Award Shows

Two of Korea's biggest year-end music award shows are landing at the same Seoul venue one week apart this November. The Korea Grand Music Awards (KGMA) run November 7-8, and the Melon Music Awards (MMA) follow on November 14-15 — both at Gocheok Sky Dome. That's an unusual scheduling overlap, and it turns a single-show trip into a real choice: fly in for one weekend, or stay the week and catch both.

This post covers what's actually confirmed about each show, how to think about lodging and transit for Gocheok Sky Dome specifically, and where to go for ticketing. For the detailed station-by-station transport breakdown, Koursea's [K-pop concert day venue guide](/posts/kpop-concert-day-venue-transport-guide-2026/) already covers Gocheok Sky Dome in depth — this post focuses on the trip-planning decision these two shows create.

## The Schedule, As Confirmed So Far

**KGMA — November 7-8, 2026, Gocheok Sky Dome.** The event's [official ticket listing on NOL](https://nol.yanolja.com/ticket/products/26013920) confirms the Saturday-Sunday dates and venue. The domestic sale opened on NOL on October 2; if you're planning around this show now, check NOL World's international listing, or [Trazy's foreigner-only KGMA package](https://www.trazy.com/experience/detail/korea-grand-music-awards-kgma-ticket-package) (confirm the exact date, start time, and seating on the live page — some localized versions of that listing still show leftover text from the prior year's event), rather than an unofficial reseller, since the main domestic sale window has already passed.

**MMA — November 14-15, 2026, Gocheok Sky Dome.** This is the first time in the show's 18-year history that MMA runs two days instead of one. Kakao Entertainment, which operates Melon, announced the dates and venue; Korean entertainment press (including Dailian and Munhwa Ilbo) reported the announcement directly. As of this post's October 24, 2026 update, ticketing has moved past the announcement stage: Melon ran a first-round presale on October 19 for subscribers with a year or more at GOLD tier or above, then a general second-round presale on October 22 at KRW 65,000 per seat, capped at two tickets per Melon ID across both rounds. Those domestic rounds are now closed. No international or "Global" on-sale date has been confirmed yet — watch Melon Ticket Global and Kakao Entertainment's own channels rather than assuming one.

> **Practical rule:** Treat both schedules as subject to change until you see them on the artist's or promoter's own ticket page. A one-week gap between two shows at the same dome is a real opportunity, but neither date is a substitute for checking your own ticket notice closer to travel.

## One Venue, Two Shows: Two Ways to Plan the Trip

With roughly a week between the two shows, there are two reasonable trip shapes:

- **Single-show trip:** Fly in a day or two before your chosen show, stay through the day after, and use the rest of the time for a normal Seoul itinerary — Myeongdong, Hongdae, or a day trip, depending on your interests.
- **Stay-the-week trip:** Book lodging for the full span between November 7 and November 16, catch both shows, and treat the week in between as a low-pressure Seoul stay rather than a packed sightseeing schedule. This only makes sense if you can secure tickets to both shows — don't book the longer stay before tickets are confirmed.

Either way, build your calendar around the show dates first, then fill in everything else — not the reverse.

Koursea's [first-time guide to where to stay in Seoul](https://www.koursea.com/guide-where-to-stay-seoul-first-time.html) covers the general neighborhood tradeoffs. For Gocheok Sky Dome specifically, the existing venue guide's advice holds: pick a hotel by testing the **return** trip on Line 1 from Guil Station, not just the outbound distance shown on a hotel listing, since Line 1's late-night intervals can be wider than central Seoul lines. If you're doing the stay-the-week trip, a more central Line 1-adjacent area gives you an easier repeat trip to the dome for the second show.

## Getting to and from Gocheok Sky Dome

The short version: **Guil Station, Line 1, Exit 2**, and assume event parking will be restricted rather than available — both shows are exactly the kind of large cultural event where Seoul Facilities Corporation's parking-access rules commonly restrict general visitors. ## Booking Tickets as a Foreign Fan

Both shows run through Korea's major domestic ticketing platforms rather than a separate foreigner-only system. Koursea's [foreigner's guide to K-pop concert ticketing in Seoul](/posts/kpop-concert-ticketing-seoul-2026/) covers NOL World, Melon Ticket Global, identity-verification requirements, and — importantly — why a resold ticket outside these official channels carries real risk at the door. That guide's resale warning now applies to both shows: KGMA's main domestic sale and both of MMA's domestic presale rounds have already run, which is exactly the situation where unofficial "guaranteed ticket" sellers appear. If you don't have a domestic Melon or NOL account, a legitimate foreigner-facing package (NOL World for KGMA, or a verified reseller like Trazy) is the safer route — just confirm current availability and price directly on the platform's live page rather than trusting a cached screenshot.

## Summary Checklist

- [ ] Confirm the current show dates and your own ticket notice directly with NOL (KGMA) or Melon Ticket (MMA) before booking flights — both domestic sales have already run, so check what's actually left rather than assuming a window is still open.
- [ ] If you missed the domestic rounds, check NOL World or a verified foreigner package (e.g. Trazy) for KGMA, and watch for a Melon Ticket Global announcement for MMA, rather than buying from an unofficial reseller.
- [ ] Decide between a single-show trip and a stay-the-week trip — only commit to the longer stay once tickets for both shows are confirmed.
- [ ] Pick lodging by testing the Line 1 return route from Guil Station, not just the outbound distance.
- [ ] Buy only through NOL/NOL World, Melon Ticket/Melon Ticket Global, or the show's named official partner — never an unofficial reseller.
