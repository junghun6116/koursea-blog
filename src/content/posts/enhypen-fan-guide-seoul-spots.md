- HYBE Yongsan is a private workplace with no guaranteed public interior access.

## An ENGENE day in Seoul

### Yongsan exterior and transport hub

Reach the HYBE exterior from Sinyongsan or Yongsan Station. Stay on public pavement, do not wait near doors or vehicles, and leave if security requests it. The practical benefit is the district's transport: food, restrooms, lockers, and onward connections are close together.

### Temporary pop-ups and album events

Season's-greetings exhibitions, album pop-ups, and collaborations are time-limited. Old social posts remain searchable after closing, so confirm the exact date and location in the newest official notice. Check whether entry uses advance booking, a remote queue, or walk-in registration.

For lucky draws, compare the branch-specific benefit, minimum purchase, and pickup window. Official lightsticks can sell out near concerts; buy early and confirm compatibility with the current venue system. Carry a second payment method and your passport when tourist services require it.
