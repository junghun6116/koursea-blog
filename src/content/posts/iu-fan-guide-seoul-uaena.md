# IU Fan Guide to Seoul: UAENA Concert & Official Planning

IU's official artist channel identifies her fan club as **UAENA**, including its [official eighth-generation recruitment video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VJS3SB22FNQ). Kakao Entertainment's official newsroom also confirms that IU launched a global Berriz community for UAENA in May 2025.

This guide covers public schedules and official channels. EDAM Entertainment offices, production spaces, accommodation, and private movements are not fan attractions.

## Key takeaways

- Check IU and EDAM's current official channels before planning an event visit.
- Confirm which community or fan-club membership a specific presale accepts.
- Treat old concert, cafe, and membership notices as expired unless a new notice renews them.
- Prepare identification, payment, ticket pickup, and return transport before booking.

## Where should UAENA check current information?

IU's [official artist YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@dlwlrma) publishes official music and fan-club content. Kakao Entertainment's [official Berriz announcement](https://newsroom.kakaoent.com/news/iu-launches-1st-global-fan-community-on-berriz/) says IU opened her global community there and that official fan-cafe material would be transferred gradually.

Use a current EDAM, IU, Berriz, ticket-seller, or venue announcement for the exact activity you plan to attend. Do not assume that an old fan-club generation, cafe event, or concert benefit remains valid.

## Is an agency office a visitor stop?

No public office access should be assumed. Do not wait at entrances, photograph staff, follow vehicles, or infer IU's schedule from workplace activity. If EDAM announces a public pop-up, exhibition, or cafe event, use only the stated public venue and dates.

A safer Seoul itinerary combines one verified public event with nearby parks, cultural sites, cafes, or an established music retailer. It should still work if event admission is capped.

## Concert preparation for overseas UAENA

Use only the seller named by EDAM, IU's official account, or the event organizer. Our [Foreigner's Guide to K-Pop Concert Ticketing in Seoul](/posts/kpop-concert-ticketing-seoul-2026/) explains account-name matching, fan-club presales, overseas payments, ticket pickup, and late-night transport.

Before booking:

- confirm the event title, city, venue, show date, and sale time;
- verify the exact UAENA generation or membership platform accepted by the presale;
- match the ticket account name to the identification rules;
- save the venue address and final public-transport route offline;
- read the current bag, recording, light-stick, and merchandise rules.

## Official merchandise and respectful fan conduct

Use IU or EDAM's current official shop link, event notice, or named ticket seller. Verify the product edition, benefit conditions, collection deadline, and exchange policy. Do not treat copied images or an artist hashtag as proof that a seller is authorized.

Attend announced concerts, screenings, fan meetings, and public events. Airports, residences, hotels, offices, vehicles, and unannounced movements are outside a respectful fan itinerary.
