AND2BLE (앤더블) is holding its fan-con **2026 AND2BLE FAN-CON ⟨W!nter inside⟩ In Seoul** at **Korea University's Hwajeong Gymnasium** from **December 4 to 6, 2026**. This guide covers what a visitor needs from the official Melon Ticket listing, plus a plan for getting there and back on a December evening.

Show details, price and rules come from the Melon Ticket listing, read on November 6, 2026. The event is organized by YH Entertainment and hosted by COM Z Inc. Rules can change, so reread the listing before you pay or cancel.

## Show Details From Melon Ticket

| | Day 1 | Day 2 | Day 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Date | Fri, Dec 4 | Sat, Dec 5 | Sun, Dec 6 |
| Start (KST) | 20:00 | 17:00 | 16:00 |

- **Venue:** Korea University Hwajeong Gymnasium (고려대학교 화정체육관).
- **Price:** all seats KRW 143,000.
- **Running time:** 100 minutes.
- **Age:** preschool-age children are not admitted.
- **Support:** Melon Ticket, 1899-0042.

The Friday show starts at 20:00, so it ends close to 22:00. Plan the return before you plan dinner.

## The Added Show and the Booking Rules

- **Added show:** the Friday December 4 show at 20:00 was added later. It went on **general sale only, with no fan-club presale**, on October 30 at 20:00 KST. Wheelchair seats went on sale at 10:00 the same day.
- **Booking pause:** to open that show, booking for the December 5 and 6 shows was paused on October 30 from 16:00 to 20:00.
- **Payment:** **bank transfer is not available** for this fan-con. Plan on a card, and test it before the queue. Foreign cards often work on Korean ticket sites but can fail, so keep a second method ready.
- **Availability:** this guide cannot see live inventory. Check the listing for released seats, and treat any seat you can book as inventory that may change.

For the general routine of Korean ticket sites for visitors, see our [K-pop concert ticketing guide](/posts/kpop-concert-ticketing-seoul-2026/).

## Cancellation Rules and Fees

These apply per Melon Ticket:

- **Booking fee:** refundable only if you cancel before midnight on the booking day.
- **Cancellation fees:** none within 7 days of booking; up to KRW 4,000 per ticket (10% cap) from day 8 until 10 days before the show; then 10% (9 to 7 days before), 20% (6 to 3 days) and 30% (2 to 1 days).
- **Show-day booking:** cannot be cancelled, changed or refunded, nor can anything booked after the cancellation deadline.
- **Card refunds:** timing depends on the card company. Bank downtime late at night can block cancellations, so do not leave it to the last minute.

**Delivery and pickup are not stated.** The listing text we read does not say whether tickets are mailed, collected on site or issued as mobile tickets. Open your booking confirmation and the full show notice, and ask support at 1899-0042 if it is unclear.

## Getting to Hwajeong Gymnasium and Back

- **Location.** Melon Ticket lists the hall at **145 Anam-ro, Seongbuk-gu** (Korea University's Anam campus, science side). It is inside a university campus, not on a street front.
- **Subway.** The campus is served by **Seoul Subway Line 6**, and **Korea University Station** (the cover photo shows its Exit 2) is the station name most visitors associate with it. We could not confirm an official walking route, so open the venue name in a map app and check the exit and the campus gate.
- **Return.** The Friday show ends late and everyone leaves at once. Look up the last Line 6 train for your direction on the day, note the time you must leave by, and keep a taxi app ready as a backup.
- **Driving.** Campus parking rules for events were not confirmed. Do not plan on parking.
- **December cold.** Queues and walks across a campus are outdoors. Bring layers and keep your phone charged.
