# ITZY Fan Guide to Seoul: MIDZY Concert & JYP Planning

ITZY's [official JYP profile](https://itzy.jype.com/profile) lists Yeji, Lia, Ryujin, Chaeryeong, and Yuna. Current official notices use MIDZY as the fandom name, including JYP's [2026 fan-club recruitment notice](https://itzy.jype.com/Mobile/NoticeView?AnSeq=6877&NoticeNumber=1513).

This is a public-event planning guide. JYP Center is an office, not an artist attraction, and the company does not promise lobby access, tours, or artist encounters.

## Key takeaways

- Use ITZY's official JYP site for profiles, releases, schedules, and ticket notices.
- JYP's current official address is in Seongnae-dong, Gangdong-gu, not Cheongdam.
- Do not build a route around access to an entertainment-company workplace.
- Recheck the named ticket seller, identity rules, and venue transport for every event.

## Where is JYP Center now?

JYP's [official recruitment site](https://recruit-apply.jype.com/) lists JYP Center at 205 Gangdong-daero, Gangdong-gu, Seoul. That confirms the company location; it does not make the building open to visitors. Stay in public areas and do not wait at entrances, photograph employees, follow vehicles, or infer private schedules.

Older fan itineraries that point to Cheongdam describe a former location. Do not combine the current Seongnae office with a Cheongdam walking route as though they were the same neighborhood.

## How should MIDZY track current activities?

Use the official notice board rather than an undated roundup. JYP's [official 2026 Kaohsiung tour notice](https://itzy.jype.com/Mobile/NoticeView?AnSeq=6781&NoticeNumber=1478) demonstrates the details a valid concert notice should provide: tour name, city, venue, show date, and ticket-sale schedule. It is not a Seoul event, so do not use it as a Seoul venue plan.

The official site also published the [2026 “Motto” release notice](https://itzy.jype.com/Mobile/NoticeView?AnSeq=6987&NoticeNumber=1544). Use current release and schedule notices to confirm public promotions instead of relying on reposted calendars.

## Concert preparation for overseas MIDZYs

Our [Foreigner's Guide to K-Pop Concert Ticketing in Seoul](/posts/kpop-concert-ticketing-seoul-2026/) explains membership presales, account-name matching, foreign-card fallback, ticket pickup, and venue-day checks.

Before booking:

- open only the seller linked by JYP or the official event organizer;
- verify whether fan-club membership and ticket accounts must match;
- enter your name in the exact format required by the seller;
- read identification and pickup requirements before paying;
- save the venue address, seating notice, and return route offline.

## Official merchandise and respectful fan conduct

Buy through the channel named in the current JYP notice or a clearly authorized retailer. Check the product edition, benefit conditions, pickup deadline, and exchange rules. A past booth or pop-up should not be assumed to remain open.

Use announced concerts, broadcasts, fan meetings, and public pop-ups as the boundary for fan travel. Airports, offices, hotels, vehicles, and unannounced movements are not appropriate sightseeing stops.
