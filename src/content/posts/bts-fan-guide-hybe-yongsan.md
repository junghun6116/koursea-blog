# BTS Fan Guide to Seoul: HYBE Yongsan, ARMY Photo Stops & Concert Prep

BTS debuted in June 2013 under BIGHIT MUSIC. The seven-member group—RM, Jin, SUGA, j-hope, Jimin, V and Jung Kook—calls its official fandom **ARMY**. For visitors, the most practical BTS-related stop in Seoul is [HYBE's headquarters in Yongsan](https://www.koursea.com/place/hybe-headquarters-yongsan/), but it should be treated as a working office rather than a public attraction.

## Key Takeaways for ARMY Visiting Seoul

- HYBE's official address is **42 Hangang-daero, Yongsan-gu, Seoul** (`서울특별시 용산구 한강대로 42`).
- The headquarters interior is not a general walk-in fan attraction. Plan for respectful exterior photos from public areas only.
- Sinyongsan Station and Yongsan Station make the visit easy to combine with the National Museum of Korea, Yongsan electronics district or the Han River.
- Do not rely on an old blog post, fan map or unofficial schedule for access. Check only HYBE, BIGHIT MUSIC, Weverse and the event organizer for current notices.
- Complete concert account verification and payment setup before the ticket sale opens.

## Visiting HYBE Headquarters in Yongsan

### Address and transport

Use the Korean address `서울특별시 용산구 한강대로 42` in Naver Map or KakaoMap. The building is near **Sinyongsan Station on Line 4** and within walking distance of **Yongsan Station**, which serves Line 1, the Gyeongui–Jungang Line and KTX trains. Station construction and exit access can change, so let your Korean map app choose the final pedestrian route on the day.

The cleanest visit is usually a short daytime stop. Arrive from Sinyongsan, photograph the building from the public sidewalk without blocking entrances, and continue toward Yongsan Station or the museum district. Leave extra time if you are carrying luggage: Yongsan is a major transport hub, and the most direct-looking exit is not always the easiest one with a suitcase.

### What fans can—and cannot—do

HYBE is an active corporate office. A headquarters exterior is not permission to enter the lobby, wait near employee access points or photograph staff and visitors at close range. Stay on public walkways, obey security instructions, keep doors clear and avoid large props that obstruct pedestrians. Never follow vehicles or people leaving the building.

> **HYBE INSIGHT closure notice:** HYBE INSIGHT permanently closed on January 15, 2023. As of September 2026, HYBE has not announced a reopening at the Yongsan headquarters. Do not plan a museum visit; treat the site only as a working-office exterior photo stop.

> **2026 comeback context:** BTS confirmed a full-group comeback for March 20, 2026 — their first group activity since the military-service era began — along with a world tour of roughly 65 dates across North America and other regions. A BTS COMEBACK POP-UP with ARIRANG-themed merchandise ran at HYBE Yongsan from March 20 to April 12, 2026, though that pop-up has since ended. Check HYBE, BIGHIT MUSIC and Weverse for any current or upcoming activity at the Yongsan site before visiting.

### A practical Yongsan fan route

Allow roughly half a day for a relaxed route:

1. Start at Sinyongsan Station and walk to the HYBE exterior.
2. Continue to Yongsan Station for cafés, shopping and an easy meal break.
3. Take the subway or a short taxi to the National Museum of Korea and Yongsan Family Park.
4. Finish near the Han River around sunset, choosing a public riverside park rather than an unverified “member spot.”

This route gives ARMY a meaningful headquarters photo without turning private workplaces or rumored locations into tourist stops. Fan-run birthday cafés may appear around Seoul during member birthdays or comeback periods, but their locations and dates change quickly. Verify the organizer's current social post before making a special journey, and buy only what fits your own budget.

## Respectful Fan Travel Checklist

- Use only official schedules and public venues.
- Never visit private homes, hotels, airports or rumored filming sites to encounter members.
- Photograph headquarters buildings only from legal public space.
- Keep your passport name consistent across membership and ticket accounts.
- Save venue, hotel and emergency addresses in Korean before leaving Wi-Fi.
