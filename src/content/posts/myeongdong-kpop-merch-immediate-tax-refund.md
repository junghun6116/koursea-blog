# Immediate Tax Refund on K-Pop Merch in Myeongdong: What the Rules Actually Say

If you're buying albums, photocards, or other merch in Myeongdong, several stores offer an immediate VAT deduction right at the register instead of making you claim it back at the airport. Here's what the actual rule covers — and what it doesn't.

## The real transaction limits

The current governing rule is a special provision under Presidential Decree No. 35947 (effective January 2, 2026), Article 10-2. It sets two limits for the instant, at-register refund:

- A **single transaction must be under 1,000,000 KRW** to qualify for the immediate deduction.
- Your **total refunded amount across a visit is capped at 5,000,000 KRW**.

Older claims floating around about a specific minimum purchase amount for this scheme aren't reflected in the current article — don't plan your shopping around a number you haven't confirmed with the store directly.

## It's not K-pop-specific

This is the standard tax-free retail scheme, not something unique to K-pop stores. It applies broadly to eligible tax-free retailers, and most major record shops and merch stores selling to tourists participate — but confirm the specific store is registered before you assume the discount applies.

## What to expect at checkout

Passport verification is part of the process, but exactly how a given store handles it — a scan, a manual check, other ID details — varies by retailer. Ask before you're mid-transaction if you want to know what to have ready.
