# BABYMONSTER Fan Guide to Seoul: MONSTIEZ Concert & YG Planning

BABYMONSTER is a seven-member YG Entertainment group. The group's [official YG profile](https://ygfamily.com/en/artists/babymonster/profile) lists Ruka, Pharita, Asa, Ahyeon, Rami, Rora, and Chiquita, with an official debut date of April 1, 2024. YG's [official fandom announcement](https://ygfamily.com/en/news/report/6314) confirms the fan name MONSTIEZ.

This guide covers public, officially announced activities. An agency headquarters is a workplace, not an open fan attraction, and an old concert notice is not evidence that a venue event is still operating.

## Key takeaways

- Check the official BABYMONSTER artist page and YG notices before building a route.
- Do not expect access to YG offices, staff areas, rehearsal spaces, or private schedules.
- Treat every concert, pop-up, and merchandise booth as date-specific.
- Match ticket-account details to the identification required by the current seller notice.

## What can MONSTIEZ verify before visiting Seoul?

Start with a current YG announcement. It should identify the event, city, venue, date, and official sales route. A previous event can explain the usual planning sequence, but its entry and merchandise rules must not be copied onto a future event.

YG's [2026 Seoul tour report](https://ygfamily.com/en/news/report/7507) confirms that BABYMONSTER performed three shows at Jamsil Indoor Stadium from June 26 to 28, 2026, for the opening of the 2026–27 **CHOOM** world tour. Those Seoul dates have passed. They are useful as verified history, not as a current invitation to visit the venue.

## Is the YG building a visitor attraction?

No public office access should be assumed. YG's [2025 sustainability report](https://www.ygfamily.com/contents/attachments/2025/08/YG%2BEntertainment%2B2025%2BSustainability%2BReport%2B%28ENG%29.pdf) identifies the company headquarters in Mapo-gu, but a corporate address does not create a public tour, lobby visit, or artist-viewing opportunity.

If an official fan space, pop-up, or retail event is announced near Hapjeong, follow only the published venue entrance and operating dates. Stay on public streets, avoid staff entrances, and do not wait for vehicles or inferred schedules.

## Concert preparation for overseas fans

Open the ticket seller named in the official announcement rather than a search-ad link or resale message. Our [Foreigner's Guide to K-Pop Concert Ticketing in Seoul](/posts/kpop-concert-ticketing-seoul-2026/) covers passport-name matching, fan-club presales, overseas payments, ticket pickup, and return transport.

Before the sale or show:

- compare the account holder name with the passport you will carry;
- confirm whether membership verification is a separate step;
- save the venue address and final public-transport route offline;
- read the current light-stick, recording, bag, and merchandise rules;
- keep a second payment method without assuming it will bypass seller restrictions.

## Official merchandise and fan etiquette

Use the sales channel linked by YG or the event organizer. Confirm the item version, order period, pickup location, and exchange policy before paying. Do not treat a social-media seller's use of official photos as proof of authorization.

Attend only announced public schedules. Do not follow artists or vehicles, enter company property, visit accommodation, or share live location information from an unannounced movement.
