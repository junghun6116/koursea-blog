# IVE Fan Guide to Seoul: DIVE Concert & Starship Planning

IVE's [official Starship profile](https://www.starship-ent.com/musician/ive) lists An Yujin, Gaeul, Rei, Jang Wonyoung, Liz, and Leeseo, and records the group's December 1, 2021 debut with **ELEVEN**. IVE's official Japan site identifies its fan club as DIVE.

This guide is for public events and official sales channels. Starship Entertainment's office is a workplace, not a fan attraction, and no lobby access, tour, or artist encounter should be assumed.

## Key takeaways

- Use Starship's artist page and IVE's official channels for releases and schedules.
- Treat company offices, studios, vehicles, and private movements as off-limits.
- Confirm every concert, pop-up, and merchandise booth through a current dated notice.
- Prepare ticket identity, payment, and return transport before a sale opens.

## What can DIVE verify before a Seoul visit?

Start with a current official notice that names the event, city, venue, date, and sales route. The [official Starship discography](https://www.starship-ent.com/musician/ive) records IVE's second album **REVIVE+**, released on February 23, 2026. That page is useful for checking official releases, but an album listing alone does not confirm a public pop-up or Seoul appearance.

IVE's [official Japan fan-club site](https://ive-official.jp/) publishes DIVE ticket and merchandise notices for Japan. Those notices show why city and venue details matter: a Japan notice is not a Seoul itinerary. Use the regional official channel named for the event you plan to attend.

## Is the Starship office a visitor stop?

No. A company address does not create public access. Do not wait at entrances, photograph employees, follow vehicles, or infer artist schedules from office activity. If Starship announces a public pop-up, exhibition, or merchandise booth, visit only the stated public venue during its published operating period.

A safer Seoul plan pairs one confirmed public event with nearby parks, cafes, or an established music retailer. That route still works if an event reaches capacity or merchandise sells out.

## Concert preparation for overseas DIVE

Use only the ticket seller linked by Starship or the event organizer. Our [Foreigner's Guide to K-Pop Concert Ticketing in Seoul](/posts/kpop-concert-ticketing-seoul-2026/) covers fan-club presales, passport-name matching, overseas payments, ticket pickup, and return transport.

Before booking:

- verify the city, venue, show date, and sales time in the official notice;
- match the ticket account name to the identification rules;
- confirm whether DIVE membership verification is a separate step;
- save the venue address and final public-transport route offline;
- recheck the current bag, recording, light-stick, and merchandise rules.

## Official merchandise and fan etiquette

Buy through the channel named in the current official notice or a clearly authorized retailer. Check the product edition, benefit conditions, pickup deadline, and exchange rules. An old booth announcement is not evidence that a store or event remains open.

Attend only announced public schedules. Airports, hotels, offices, vehicles, and unannounced movements are not sightseeing stops.
