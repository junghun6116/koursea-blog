## Best Things to Do in Pohang: Skywalks, Ocean Heritage & Natural Wonders

Pohang is the industrial and maritime heartbeat of South Korea's southeastern coast. Set along Yeongil Bay where heavy industry meets the East Sea, the city has developed an impressive collection of open-air ocean architectural landmarks, restored historical districts, and rugged scenic coastal trails.

Here are 14 verified attractions across Pohang, organized by experiential theme.

## Ocean Architecture & Modern Landmarks (스카이워크 & 도심 명소)

### 1. [Hwanho Park Space Walk](https://www.koursea.com/place/2474/)

**Ratings:** 4.5 / 5.0 (2,093 reviews)

**The Space:** A massive steel artistic roller-coaster structure created by German artists Heike Mutter and Ulrich Genth. It extends 333 meters in track length and rises up to 25 meters above Hwanho Park.

**The Experience:** Visitors walk up open-grated metal staircases that flex gently with coastal winds. The high points provide uninterrupted, panoramic views over Yeongil Bay and the industrial skyline of POSCO.

**Visiting Note:** Free entry. Strictly closed during rain, snow, or high wind conditions exceeding 8 m/s.

### 2. [Yeongildae Beach](https://www.koursea.com/place/2502/)

**Ratings:** 4.3 / 5.0 (7,781 reviews)

**The Space:** Pohang's primary urban beach, featuring a 1.7-kilometer sandy coastline along the northern city waterfront.

**The Experience:** The shoreline centers around the Yeongildae Pavilion, Korea's first traditional two-story maritime pavilion set 80 meters out over the water via a pedestrian bridge. At night, the pavilion lights up against the glittering backdrop of the POSCO steelworks across the bay.

### 3. [Pohang Sea Skywalk](https://www.koursea.com/place/2470/)

**Ratings:** 4.2 / 5.0 (444 reviews)

**The Space:** A curved maritime walkway looping 463 meters along the shoreline of Yeohang-dong, suspended over the open sea.

**The Experience:** The path includes transparent reinforced glass floor sections that let travelers view crashing ocean currents below while looking across the waters toward Hwanho Park and Yeongildae.

### 4. [Park1538](https://www.koursea.com/place/2500/)

**Ratings:** 4.4 / 5.0 (66 reviews)

**The Space:** POSCO's brand and cultural complex, named after 1,538°C—the thermodynamic melting point of pure iron.

**The Experience:** Situated across landscaped terraced gardens, it combines an architectural steel exhibition hall, an interactive media technology pavilion, and outdoor sculpture parks.

**Visiting Note:** Indoor museum and hall tours require advance reservation through the official Park1538 website.

### 5. [Fire Garden (불의 정원)](https://www.koursea.com/place/2459/)

**Ratings:** 4.4 / 5.0 (865 reviews)

**The Space:** A specialized urban park located along the Pohang Green Way railroad park corridor, featuring a natural underground methane fire that has burned continuously since 2017.

**The Experience:** After accidental ignition during a groundwater drilling project, the city built a protected stone hearth enclosure allowing visitors to safely observe the perpetual flame burning from the raw soil.

### 6. [Pohang Canal](https://www.koursea.com/place/2452/)

**Ratings:** 4.2 / 5.0 (511 reviews)

**The Space:** An eco-restoration project that reopened a 1.3-kilometer waterway reconnecting the Hyeongsan River to Dongbin Inner Harbor.

**The Experience:** Landscaped pedestrian paths line both banks of the canal with public artwork. Regular canal cruise boats depart from the Pohang Canal Pavilion, taking passengers through the inner harbor and onto Yeongil Bay.

### 7. [Songdo Beach](https://www.koursea.com/place/2552/)

**Ratings:** 4.5 / 5.0 (58 reviews)

**The Space:** A historical urban beachfront in southern Pohang bordered by dense pine forests and the Songdo Pine Grove trail.

**The Experience:** A quieter alternative to Yeongildae with coastal cafes, seaside walking promenades, and direct night vistas overlooking the maritime cargo harbor.

## Coastal Heritage & Filming Locations (남부 해안 & 역사 문화)

### 8. [Guryongpo Japanese House Street](https://www.koursea.com/place/2439/)

**Ratings:** 4.0 / 5.0 (6,994 reviews)

**The Space:** A hillside heritage enclave preserving authentic two-story wooden Japanese residences built during the regional fishing port boom of the early 1900s.

**The Experience:** Gained major global recognition as the primary filming backdrop for the K-drama When the Camellia Blooms. Visitors climb the central staircase to recreate the iconic promotional poster shot, visit the Guryongpo Modern History Museum, and explore preserved vintage teahouses.

### 9. [Homigot Sunrise Square](https://www.koursea.com/place/2436/)

**Ratings:** 4.4 / 5.0 (5,047 reviews)

**The Space:** Located on the easternmost protruding tip of the Korean peninsula, this public park serves as the nation's premier annual New Year sunrise gathering destination.

**The Experience:** Famous for the Hands of Harmony (Sangsaeng's Hand), a pair of large bronze sculptures consisting of one hand rising from the sea and the matching opposite hand anchored on the square. The grounds also host the historic 1903 Homigot Lighthouse and the National Lighthouse Museum.

### 10. [Haseondae (하선대)](https://www.koursea.com/place/2493/)

**Ratings:** 4.3 / 5.0 (569 reviews)

**The Space:** A small rocky island reef along Section 2 of the Homiban-do Coastal Trail, tied to regional folklore of heavenly maidens descending to bathe in the sea.

**The Experience:** Elevated wooden coastal boardwalks trace directly along the crashing shoreline, enabling flat, scenic walks past eroded sea rocks and sunrise viewing points without climbing cliffs.

### 11. [Ilchulam Rock (일출암)](https://www.koursea.com/place/2462/)

**Ratings:** 4.3 / 5.0 (122 reviews)

**The Space:** An offshore sea stack rising right off the coastline of Sinchang-ri in southern Pohang, topped by a hardy cluster of twisted evergreen pine trees.

**The Experience:** A celebrated coastal sunrise point historically praised by modern scholar Choe Nam-seon as one of the most magnificent sunrise landscapes in Korea. Photographers gather at dawn to capture the first light breaking through the rocky cleft and the silhouettes of the pine trees.

## Northern Coasts & Mountain Valleys (북부 해안 & 계곡)

### 12. [Igari Anchor Observatory](https://www.koursea.com/place/2479/)

**Ratings:** 4.3 / 5.0 (2,441 reviews)

**The Space:** A seaside observation jetty shaped like a ship's anchor, projecting 102 meters straight out from pine-covered bluffs over the East Sea.

**The Experience:** Standing 10 meters above the tide, the boardwalk leads to a red-roofed miniature tower oriented toward Dokdo Island, featuring grated floor sections that look down into clear coastal waters.

### 13. [Sangsaeng Falls (상생폭포)](https://www.koursea.com/place/2496/)

**Ratings:** 4.4 / 5.0 (281 reviews)

**The Space:** The first waterfall among the famous 12 cascades inside the scenic Naeyeonsan Mountain valley.

**The Experience:** Characterized by twin parallel streams falling over a broad rock ledge into an emerald basin pool, easily reached via an easy, paved 20-minute walking route from Bogyeongsa Temple.

### 14. [Haok Valley (하옥계곡)](https://www.koursea.com/place/2446/)

**Ratings:** 4.4 / 5.0 (247 reviews)

**The Space:** A secluded 12-kilometer freshwater mountain gorge cutting through dense forestry in northwestern Pohang.

**The Experience:** Features exceptionally clear natural swimming pools, wide river gravel beds, and surrounding limestone bluffs, serving as a peaceful summer retreat for freshwater wading and nature camping.
