# JISOO Fan Guide to Seoul: BLISSOO, BLINK & Official Planning

JISOO's [official BLISSOO site](https://www.blissoo.co.kr/) identifies BLISSOO as her personal label and describes her as a singer and actor who debuted as a BLACKPINK member in 2016. The site links BLISSOO's official social channels and publishes current news.

This guide is for public events and authorized sales. A label or agency office is a workplace, not a fan attraction, and no public access should be assumed.

## Key takeaways

- Use BLISSOO's official site for JISOO's solo news and verified social links.
- Separate JISOO's solo-label notices from BLACKPINK group announcements.
- Treat every concert, pop-up, screening, and merchandise booth as date-specific.
- Do not visit offices, hotels, homes, vehicles, or private schedules.

## What does BLISSOO officially confirm?

The [BLISSOO homepage](https://www.blissoo.co.kr/) says the label was created for JISOO's individual activities and maintains her official profile, filmography, and news. It is the clearest starting point for checking whether a solo release, acting project, campaign, or public appearance is current.

BLISSOO's official news currently includes JISOO's September 2026 single **CLICK**. A release listing does not automatically confirm a Seoul fan event, so look for a separate notice naming the venue and dates before traveling.

## Is a BLISSOO or YG office a visitor stop?

No. Corporate offices and production facilities should not be treated as sightseeing locations. Stay on public streets, do not wait at entrances, photograph staff, follow vehicles, or infer JISOO's movements.

For group activity, check BLACKPINK and YG's official channels. For solo activity, begin with BLISSOO. Do not combine old office guides, expired pop-ups, and current announcements into a route unless each stop is independently confirmed.

## Concert and event preparation for overseas fans

Use only the ticket seller linked by BLISSOO, BLACKPINK, YG, or the official organizer for the event concerned. Our [Foreigner's Guide to K-Pop Concert Ticketing in Seoul](/posts/kpop-concert-ticketing-seoul-2026/) covers passport-name matching, fan-club presales, overseas payment checks, ticket pickup, and post-show transport.

Before booking:

- confirm whether the event is a JISOO solo activity or a BLACKPINK group activity;
- verify the city, venue, date, sale time, and organizer;
- match the ticket account name to the identification requirements;
- save the venue address and final public-transport route offline;
- recheck current bag, recording, light-stick, and merchandise rules.

## Official merchandise and fan etiquette

Use the store or seller linked from the current official announcement. Confirm the product edition, order period, benefit conditions, pickup location, and exchange policy. An account using official photos is not automatically an authorized seller.

Attend announced concerts, screenings, showcases, and public pop-ups. Airports, hotels, offices, residences, vehicles, and unannounced movements are outside a respectful itinerary.
