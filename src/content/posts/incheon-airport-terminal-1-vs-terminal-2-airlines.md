# Incheon Airport Terminal 1 vs Terminal 2: Airline Check Guide

Do not choose an Incheon Airport terminal from the airline name alone. The airport warns that codeshares and other operating circumstances can change the actual arrival or departure terminal. Your e-ticket and the airport's live flight search are the final checks.

## Key takeaways

- Terminal 2 currently handles Korean Air, Asiana Airlines, Jin Air, Air Seoul, Air Busan, Delta Air Lines, and other listed carriers.
- Terminal 1 currently handles Jeju Air, T'way Air, Eastar Jet, Air Premia, PARATA AIR, and other listed foreign carriers.
- Codeshares can use a different terminal from the one you expect.
- Concourse gates are reached from Terminal 1 by shuttle train, and the airport says return is not allowed after boarding it.

## Which terminal does my airline use?

| Check | What it tells you | When to use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E-ticket | Booked operating details and terminal, when assigned | Before leaving for the airport |
| [Incheon Airport flight search](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1407/subview.do) | Current flight and terminal information | The day before and again on departure day |
| Airport departure display | Check-in counter, terminal status, and gate | Immediately after arrival |
| Airline channel | Operational changes and check-in requirements | When the sources disagree |

The airport's [departure procedure page](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1413/subview.do) publishes the current high-level airline split but explicitly tells passengers to confirm the terminal through the e-ticket or flight search.

## Current high-level airline split

### Terminal 2

The official airport guide currently lists Korean Air, Asiana Airlines, Jin Air, Air Seoul, Air Busan, Delta Air Lines, and other airlines at Terminal 2. This is a planning overview, not a substitute for checking your flight number.

### Terminal 1

The same guide currently lists Jeju Air, T'way Air, Eastar Jet, Air Premia, PARATA AIR, and other foreign airlines at Terminal 1. Some Terminal 1 passengers board through the separate Concourse.

The airport's [airline information directory](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1408/subview.do) can be used to check an airline listing, but the operating flight remains decisive for a codeshare.

## Why codeshares cause terminal mistakes

A ticket can display one airline's marketing code while another airline operates the aircraft. Terminal assignments can also change for operational reasons. Search the exact flight number shown for the operating carrier, then compare it with your e-ticket and the airport display.

If two sources disagree, contact the airline rather than guessing from a previous trip or a general airline list.

## What does the Concourse mean?

The official departure guide maps gates 1–50 to Terminal 1, gates 101–132 to the Concourse, and gates 208–290 to Terminal 2. It instructs Concourse passengers to use the 24-hour shuttle train and notes that return is not allowed after boarding.

Follow the gate and transfer signs after completing the required departure steps. Do not ride the shuttle merely to look around.

## Before leaving Seoul

1. Check the e-ticket for terminal and operating flight number.
2. Search the exact flight on the official airport site.
3. Save the terminal, check-in counter, and airline contact offline.
4. Tell your taxi or bus driver whether you need Terminal 1 or Terminal 2.
5. Recheck the departure display as soon as you enter the terminal.

Terminal and counter information can change. Verify with the airport and airline on the day of travel.
