# T.O.P and Squid Game Guide: Thanos, Official Sources & Seoul Planning

Netflix's official Tudum cast guide identifies Choi Seung-hyun, also known as T.O.P, as **Thanos (Player 230)** in *Squid Game* Season 2. This page separates that verified screen credit from fan-made filming-location claims and unannounced private schedules.

There is no public production set or artist office attached to this guide. Plan around official exhibitions, screenings, and announced appearances only.

## Key takeaways

- Netflix confirms Choi Seung-hyun as Thanos in *Squid Game* Season 2.
- A scene screenshot or social post does not verify a public filming location.
- Do not treat production facilities, accommodation, vehicles, or private schedules as visitor stops.
- Check the organizer's current page before traveling to an exhibition or public event.

## What does Netflix officially confirm about Thanos?

The [Netflix Tudum cast guide](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/squid-game-season-2-cast) describes Thanos as Player 230, a rapper who enters the game after a failed cryptocurrency investment, and credits Choi Seung-hyun in the role. Netflix's [Season 2 guide](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/squid-game-season-2-confirmed?inapp=true) repeats the same cast pairing.

Those sources verify the role. They do not establish that a fan-identified building is an accessible set, that a private production location remains in use, or that an actor will appear at a promotional venue.

## How should fans verify a Seoul event?

Use an announcement from Netflix, the venue, the distributor, or the event organizer. Confirm the event name, public address, operating dates, admission method, photography policy, and final-entry time. A past premiere or temporary installation should not be treated as permanent.

Netflix's [official report on the Seoul Season 2 premiere](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/squid-game-season-2-events) documents the December 2024 promotional event as history. It is not a current invitation or a standing attraction.

## Respectful fan boundaries

Public screenings, authorized exhibitions, and announced appearances are appropriate fan activities. Production offices, hotels, homes, vehicles, and inferred movements are not. Do not follow cast members, block entrances, or publish real-time locations from an unannounced encounter.

If a future music performance is officially announced, use the seller named by its organizer. Our [Foreigner's Guide to K-Pop Concert Ticketing in Seoul](/posts/kpop-concert-ticketing-seoul-2026/) explains account-name matching, overseas payments, ticket pickup, and venue-day checks.
