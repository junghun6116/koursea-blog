# ROSÉ Fan Guide: APT., Official Music & Seoul Planning

ROSÉ is a BLACKPINK member and solo artist. Her official store identifies **APT.** as a ROSÉ and Bruno Mars single released through Atlantic Records and THEBLACKLABEL. This guide keeps that confirmed release separate from changing fan rumors, unofficial merchandise, and private schedules.

## Key takeaways

- Use ROSÉ's official store and label channels to confirm releases and merchandise.
- A music release does not create a permanent public attraction in Seoul.
- Treat agency offices, residences, hotels, and inferred schedules as private.
- Use the ticket seller named in an official concert announcement.

## What is officially confirmed about APT.?

The [official ROSÉ store](https://shop.rosesarerosie.com/products/apt-7-single-black) lists the seven-inch single as **ROSÉ & Bruno Mars — APT.** and identifies Atlantic Records and THEBLACKLABEL as the official release partners. That is the cleanest primary source for checking the title, collaboration, and physical edition without relying on reposted social claims.

Availability can change by country and date. Check the official product page before ordering, and compare the seller, format, track listing, and shipping territory before payment.

## Planning a ROSÉ-themed Seoul day

There is no permanent APT. attraction that should be assumed open. Build the day around public, source-checked stops instead: an established official-merchandise retailer, a confirmed pop-up if one has a current organizer notice, and a neighborhood itinerary that remains useful if stock or admission changes.

For albums and lightsticks, verify the retailer and product before paying. Koursea's [guide to spotting fake K-pop merchandise in Seoul](/posts/how-to-spot-fake-k-pop-merch-seoul-guide/) explains what can be checked without treating packaging alone as proof.

If an official pop-up is announced, save the organizer's date, address, admission method, purchase limits, and final-entry time. Do not substitute a fan repost for the original notice.

## Concert and event preparation

Start with the artist or promoter announcement and follow only the linked ticket seller. Our [K-Pop Concert Ticketing in Seoul guide](/posts/kpop-concert-ticketing-seoul-2026/) covers passport-name matching, overseas payment, presales, pickup rules, and return transport.

Save the venue address in Korean, download the ticket conditions, and keep a payment backup. Never assume that a collaboration announcement, release page, or merchandise listing confirms a concert date.

## Fan etiquette

Official public appearances, ticketed events, and authorized pop-ups are appropriate fan destinations. Offices, residences, hotels, vehicles, airport movements, and unannounced schedules are not. Stay in public areas and follow staff instructions on photography and queues.
