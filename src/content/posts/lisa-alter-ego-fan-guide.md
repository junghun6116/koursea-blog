# LISA Fan Guide: Alter Ego, Official Music & Seoul Planning

LISA is a BLACKPINK member and solo artist. Her official store identifies **Alter Ego** as a solo album released through LLOUD Co. and RCA Records. This guide stays with primary-source release information and avoids treating private offices, rumored sightings, or unannounced schedules as visitor attractions.

## Key takeaways

- Verify releases and merchandise through LISA's official site and stores.
- An album campaign does not create a permanent public attraction in Seoul.
- Do not visit private offices, residences, hotels, or inferred schedules.
- Use only the ticket seller named in an official event announcement.

## What is officially confirmed about Alter Ego?

The [official LISA store](https://ukshop.lalisaofficial.com/collections/all/products/alter-ego-vinyl) describes **Alter Ego** as her debut solo album through LLOUD Co. and RCA Records. The [official music collection](https://asiashop.lalisaofficial.com/collections/music) lists its current physical and digital editions.

Stock, prices, editions, and shipping territories can change. Verify the seller, format, track list, inclusions, and delivery region on the official product page before paying. An old screenshot or resale listing is not proof of current availability.

## Planning a LISA-themed Seoul day

There is no permanent Alter Ego attraction that travelers should assume is open. Build a flexible route around public, source-checked stops: an established album retailer, an organizer-confirmed pop-up if one is active, and nearby neighborhoods that remain useful if stock or admission changes.

Koursea's [guide to spotting fake K-pop merchandise in Seoul](/posts/how-to-spot-fake-k-pop-merch-seoul-guide/) explains how to evaluate a seller and product without treating a single packaging detail as definitive proof.

For a temporary activation, use the original organizer notice to confirm its address, dates, entry method, purchase limits, and photography rules. A past campaign page does not confirm current operation.

## Concert and event preparation

Start with an official artist or promoter announcement and follow its ticket link. Our [K-Pop Concert Ticketing in Seoul guide](/posts/kpop-concert-ticketing-seoul-2026/) covers overseas payment, passport-name matching, presales, venue pickup, and return transport.

Save the Korean venue address and entry conditions offline. Keep a payment backup, and do not interpret a music or merchandise release as confirmation of a concert.

## Fan etiquette

Official public schedules, ticketed events, and authorized retail activations are appropriate. Offices, residences, hotels, vehicles, airport movements, and unannounced schedules are not. Stay in public areas and follow staff instructions on photography and queues.
