Gangneung is Korea's coffee capital and a coastal city rich with royal history — home to the birthplace of two figures on Korean banknotes, a UNESCO-adjacent folk heritage estate, and one of the country's most photographed coastal boardwalks. It's an easy KTX trip from Seoul (about 2 hours) that pairs beach culture with genuine history.

Here are 10 verified attractions across Gangneung, organized by experience type.

## Beaches & Korea's Coffee Street

### [Anmok Beach (Gangneung Coffee Street)](https://www.koursea.com/place/1699/)

4.3/5.0 (10,216 reviews). Home to Korea's best-known coffee street, where more than 30 independent cafes line the sand facing the East Sea. The area has earned Gangneung its reputation as the country's coffee capital, anchored by the Coffee Cupper Museum and the annual Gangneung Coffee Festival.

### [Gyeongpo Beach](https://www.koursea.com/place/1694/)

4.4/5.0 (8,206 reviews). One of Korea's most popular East Coast beaches, backed by pine forests and set beside Gyeongpo Lake. Gangneung served as an ice-sports host city for the 2018 PyeongChang Winter Olympics, and this stretch of coast remains one of the region's most visited summer destinations.

### [Hourglass Park (모래시계공원)](https://www.koursea.com/place/1710/)

4.0/5.0 (6,169 reviews). Built in 1999 by Gangneung city and Samsung Electronics to mark the millennium, the park's giant sand-filled hourglass measures 8.06 meters in diameter and holds 8 tons of sand. It takes a full year for the sand to trickle through, and the mechanism is flipped to restart at midnight every New Year's Day.

## Art, Cliffs & Coastal Trails

### [Haslla Art World](https://www.koursea.com/place/2334/)

4.3/5.0 (4,399 reviews). A 33,000-pyeong hillside sculpture park overlooking the sea, mixing outdoor installations like the "Sundial Square" and Ok Young-choi's red "Into The Head" brain sculpture with an indoor Pinocchio Museum. Its Museum Hotel lets guests actually sleep inside the art environment, just a short taxi ride from Jeongdongjin Station.

### [Jeongdong-Simgok Sea Fan Road (정동심곡바다부채길)](https://www.koursea.com/place/1709/)

4.2/5.0 (3,920 reviews). A 2.86-kilometer coastal boardwalk trail hugging dramatic cliffs between Jeongdongjin and Simgok. Gangneung-born novelist Lee Soon-won coined the trail's name after the coastline's shape, which fans out toward the sea like an open folding fan.

### [Jeongdongjin Station](https://www.koursea.com/place/1708/)

4.5/5.0 (165 reviews). Widely cited as the closest train station to the ocean anywhere in the world (a claim tied to a Guinness World Records listing, though not independently verified), the platform sits close enough to the sand that the beach is essentially a few steps away. It's also a popular sunrise-viewing spot, especially on New Year's Day.

## Royal Heritage, Lake Legends & Highland Skies

### [Seongyojang House](https://www.koursea.com/place/2336/)

4.3/5.0 (2,765 reviews). Built during the Joseon Dynasty as the residence of Lee Nae-beon (1703–1781), this sprawling upper-class estate was designated a National Folklore Cultural Heritage site in 1967. Its ornamental pond, pavilion, and preserved living quarters offer a rare, intact look at aristocratic domestic life under the Joseon Dynasty.

### [Ojukheon House & Museum](https://www.koursea.com/place/2347/)

4.6/5.0 (766 reviews). The birthplace of Confucian scholar Yi I (Yulgok), born here in 1536 in a room still known as Mongnyongsil, and of his mother, the artist and calligrapher Shin Saimdang. The mother-son pair appear together on Korean currency — Shin Saimdang on the 50,000-won note and Yi I on the 5,000-won note, whose reverse side also depicts the Ojukheon house itself.

### [Gyeongpo Lake](https://www.koursea.com/place/1695/)

4.4/5.0 (580 reviews). A scenic lagoon beside Gyeongpo Beach, long tied to a local legend that on a clear night the moon appears five times over Gyeongpo — once in the sky, once on the lake, once on the sea, once in a drinking cup, and once in the eyes of a loved one. A lakeside path makes it an easy, romantic add-on to a beach visit.

### [Anbandegi (안반데기)](https://www.koursea.com/place/2345/)

4.3/5.0 (374 reviews). A highland cabbage-farming plateau sitting 1,100 meters above sea level, reclaimed from bare, rocky slopes by settlers using oxen starting in 1965. Today it's one of Korea's best-known sunrise and stargazing spots, with a stone viewing platform (built from rocks cleared off the fields) overlooking terraced vegetable farms that roll into the clouds.
