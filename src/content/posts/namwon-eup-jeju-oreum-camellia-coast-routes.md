Most Jeju guides cover the north coast or the west. This one stays inside a single area on the south-east side, Namwon-eup in Seogwipo, using a 2024 Jeju village-tourism resource file that lists 61 places there. Each entry gives a name, a category, an address, coordinates, opening hours and, for many, whether entry is free. Where the file gives no hours, fee or transit, this guide says so.

## What the 61 Namwon-eup listings are

| Category in the file | Listings |
| --- | --- |
| Stays (숙소) | 23 |
| Things to do (즐길곳): farms, parks, workshops | 9 |
| Oreums (volcanic cones) | 8 |
| Food and cafes (F&B) | 6 |
| Places to visit (가볼곳): ports, camellia sites | 5 |
| Forest trails | 3 |
| Heritage sites (cultural property, monument, natural monument) | 4 |
| Coast, port and temple | 3 |

The file's descriptions mention Jeju Olle routes 4 and 5 around Sinheung-ri, Namwon Port and Wimi Port. It does not give bus routes or timetables, so the distances below are straight-line from the file's coordinates and a car or taxi is the realistic way to link the clusters. Check bus options in a map app.

## Route 1: Wimi and Keunneong coast (camellia and sea)

- **큰엉해안경승지 (Keunneong coastal viewpoint):** listed with no closing day, hours 11:00 to 20:00 on all days, free entry. The file calls it a sea cliff coast with untouched rock walls.
- **위미동백나무군락 (Wimi camellia grove):** a designated monument, free, no hours given. About 2.7 km from Keunneong.
- **제주동백수목원 (Jeju Camellia Arboretum):** about 450 m from the Wimi grove. The file says it is private land and that it now charges an admission fee for photography. The amount is not stated.
- **위미항 / 좌배머들코지 (Wimi Port):** about 1.2 km from the Wimi grove. The file notes it as the place in Jeju where cherry blossoms bloom earliest.

For camellia timing, the file only gives one date: 동백포레스트 (Camellia Forest) is described as at its peak from late December to January. That place is listed as closed Mondays and Tuesdays, open 10:00 to 17:30, and as "temporarily closed from 2024-03-04". We could not confirm whether it has reopened, so call before you plan around it.

## Route 2: Forest trails, oreums and a tangerine park

- **머체왓숲길 (Mecheowat forest trail):** free, open all year. Summer hours 09:00 to 20:00 and winter hours 09:00 to 19:00.
- **사려니오름 (Saryeoni Oreum):** about 2.8 km from Mecheowat. The file lists it as closed Mondays, Tuesdays and during a wildfire-prevention period from 1 November to 15 May each year, and open 09:00 to 17:00 otherwise. Based on the file, it is closed now.
- **사라오름 (Sarioreum):** free entry; hours 05:00 to 13:00 in summer, 05:00 to 12:30 in spring and autumn and 05:00 to 12:00 in winter. Parking is 500 won for a motorbike, 1,000 won for a light car, 1,800 won for a car and 3,000 won for a van. It is about 10 km from the Camellia Forest, so treat it as a separate morning.
- **휴애리자연생활공원 (Hue-ae-ri Nature Life Park):** open 09:00 to 18:00 every day, with last entry at 17:30 from March to September and 16:30 from October to February. The file describes a tangerine-picking experience from September to January. Entry fees are not stated in the part of the file we used.
- **고살리숲길 (Gosalli forest trail):** free. Hours are 10:00 to 16:00 on weekdays and 14:00 to 16:00 at weekends.

## Route 3: Uigwi-ri village and wetland oreum

- **물영아리오름 (Mulyeongari Oreum):** free, no closing day listed. The file describes a crater that holds a wetland.
- **걸서악오름 (Geolseoak Oreum):** free. The file says it shows the Hareri village and Beomseom and Seopseom islands at once.
- **의귀 송원가든 (Uigwi Songwon Garden):** 10:00 to 22:00, no closing day. The file lists native-chicken shabu at 60,000 won, native chicken at 50,000 won, braised chicken at 50,000 won and smoked duck at 55,000 won.
- **해바라기식당 (Haebaragi Sikdang):** closed Thursdays, 11:00 to 21:00 with a break from 14:00 to 17:00 and last order at 20:00. About 770 m from Songwon Garden. The file describes a Jeju-style duruchigi (stir-fried pork or seafood) with a half-and-half option.
- **랑이식당 (Rangi Sikdang):** the file describes a dumpling hotpot; hours 10:30 to 20:00 with a break from 15:00 to 17:30.

## Route 4: Ports and the fish auction

- **남원항 (Namwon Port):** the file describes it as the start of Olle route 5 and keeps its stone-built look.
- **옥돔마을 (Okdom Village, Taeheung 2-ri):** the file says a whistle announces the tilefish auction at 1 pm on weekdays, that you can buy tilefish after the auction and that a restaurant in front cooks fish bought there. It is about 3 km from Namwon Port.

## Where to stay

The file lists 23 stays as small private lodgings. It gives names, addresses and, for some, check-in and check-out times (for example 사계절펜션 lists 15:00 and 11:00), but not rooms or prices we can compare, so treat these as places to search, not recommendations.

- **Near Olle route 4, Sinheung-ri:** 사계절펜션, 밤편지, 녹음실 제주, 루다스테이.
- **Hansin-ro and camellia village:** 정원집, 청재설헌, 제주하늘바람 (listed as adjoining Olle route 4).
- **Uigwi-ri, inland:** 은빌레 펜션, 바띠에, 귤림동화, 의귀소담, 정원책방.
- **Wimi and Taeheung coast:** 룸엣155 펜션 and 비레이지, which the file describes as near a bus stop and convenient without a car, and 제주소요 (B&B).

## A one-day plan, east to west

1. Morning: Keunneong, the Wimi camellia grove and the arboretum (all within about 3 km).
2. Lunch: Haebaragi Sikdang or Songwon Garden in Uigwi-ri, if you drive inland. Check their closing days first.
3. Afternoon: Hue-ae-ri (last entry 16:30 in winter) or Mecheowat trail until closing.

## Before you go
