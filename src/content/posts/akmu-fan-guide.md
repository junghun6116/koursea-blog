AKMU (Lee Chan-hyuk and Lee Su-hyun) are a sibling duo under YG Entertainment. For an overseas fan, the useful plan is simple: follow official announcements first, then build a public Seoul route around verified, open locations.

## Official updates and tickets

Check [YG Entertainment](https://www.ygfamily.com/) and AKMU's official social channels for releases, appearances and ticket notices. If no current concert is listed there, treat third-party event pages as unconfirmed rather than planning travel around them. Ticket links should lead to the official seller named in the announcement.

## A YG-area day, not a private-visit plan

The YG headquarters area and public fan retail spaces can be viewed only from publicly accessible areas. Do not wait at offices, follow artists or treat a workplace as a visitor attraction. For BLACKPINK- and YG-area context, see our [BLACKPINK Hapjeong guide](/posts/blackpink-fan-guide-yg-hapjeong/) and the related TREASURE guide when planning a wider route.

## Fan etiquette

Use official schedules, keep entrances clear and never share private sightings. Before booking flights, re-check the agency notice, venue rules and ID requirements because schedules can change.

## Verify the exact event, not only the artist name

YG's official site identifies AKMU among its artists and provides the agency's current public notices. For a concert or appearance, open the dated notice and check the venue, performance date, named ticket seller and eligibility rules together. A third-party calendar can be a discovery tool, but it should not replace the organizer's page when you book a flight or buy a ticket.

Membership presales, general sales and venue pickup can use different instructions. Keep the ticket account name consistent with the ID or passport requested by the seller, and save the original confirmation. If the official notice changes, the revised organizer notice controls.

## Make the neighborhood stop worthwhile

If you visit Hapjeong, combine public retail or café stops with another nearby attraction instead of treating the office entrance as the destination. Photograph only from public areas, do not block the pavement, and do not wait for artists or vehicles. Merchandise inventory should be checked with the public shop itself.
