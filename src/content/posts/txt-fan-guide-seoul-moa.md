# TOMORROW X TOGETHER Fan Guide to Seoul: MOA Concert & Official Merch Planning

TOMORROW X TOGETHER is a five-member BIGHIT MUSIC group made up of Soobin, Yeonjun, Beomgyu, Taehyun, and Hueningkai, according to the group's [official profile](https://txt.ibighit.com/eng/profile/). Its official fan club is MOA, as confirmed by the [official MOA Membership notice](https://weverse.io/txt/notice/880).

This guide focuses on public, announced fan activities. It does not treat an entertainment-company office as a tourist attraction or promise access to workplaces, rehearsal rooms, or private schedules.

## Key takeaways

- Use the official TXT site and Weverse notices as the source for current schedules.
- A Seoul concert or pop-up is visitable only for its published dates and entry conditions.
- Prepare the exact account name, membership status, passport, payment method, and return route before ticket sales.
- Buy official merchandise through the sales channel named in the current notice.

## What can a MOA safely plan in Seoul?

Start with a dated official event rather than an agency building. TXT's official notices publish the venue, event organizer, ticketing window, merchandise method, and identity rules when they apply. A past notice is useful for understanding the process, but it is not evidence that the same booth or event is still operating.

For example, the [2026 TXT MOA CON announcement](https://weverse.io/txt/notice/32951) named KSPO DOME as the venue and separated membership pre-verification, membership presale, and general reservation. The event took place from February 27 to March 1, 2026, so do not travel there expecting it to be current. Use the format as a checklist for the next official announcement.

## How did official merchandise pickup work?

The [MOA CON merchandise notice](https://weverse.io/txt/notice/33920) offered two official methods for that event: Weverse Shop pre-purchase with on-site pickup, or purchase at the venue on the concert day. It also required the buyer to be within the announced pickup radius for mobile ordering and imposed product-specific purchase limits.

That arrangement was event-specific. For a future show, verify these items again:

- the exact sales dates and booth operating hours;
- whether advance purchase or walk-up sales are offered;
- the pickup location and any distance restriction in the app;
- purchase limits, exchange rules, and required order details;
- the current official light stick model and service-booth rules.

## Concert ticket preparation for overseas MOAs

Read the ticket seller named by the artist notice, then compare the purchaser name with the passport you will carry. Do not assume a screenshot, copied ID, or another person's membership will pass admission checks. Our [Foreigner's Guide to K-Pop Concert Ticketing in Seoul](/posts/kpop-concert-ticketing-seoul-2026/) explains account setup, membership presales, identity checks, payment fallback, and ticket pickup.

Before the sale opens, save the venue address in Korean, confirm the final subway departure, and choose a meeting point outside the busiest station exit. Mobile service can slow around large events, so keep the ticket notice and accommodation address available offline.

## Fan etiquette

Stay within public event areas and follow staff instructions on queues, photography, recording, and gifts. Do not wait at airports, follow vehicles, enter offices, or visit an unannounced schedule. An official notice is the boundary between a public fan activity and a private movement.
