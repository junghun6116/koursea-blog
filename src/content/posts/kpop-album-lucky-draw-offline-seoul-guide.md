# K-Pop Lucky Draw Kiosks in Seoul: Payment Fixes When Your Card Won't Work

You've picked your album, you're ready for the lucky draw pull, and the kiosk rejects your card. This is a common snag at stores like Soundwave and Music Plant, and there's usually a simple workaround.

## Why it happens

Regular in-store checkout at these shops generally accepts both domestic and foreign cards, plus cash. The standalone lucky draw kiosk machines are a separate system, though, and their card readers vary by model and by event — some simply aren't set up to process foreign-issued cards.

## The fix: pay at the counter first

If the kiosk won't take your card, ask a staff member about paying for the album directly at the register instead. Many stores will then hand you a physical token or coin to insert into the lucky draw machine, which sidesteps the kiosk's own payment step entirely. This is common enough that staff are usually already familiar with the request.

## It varies by store and event

Soundwave and Music Plant don't run identical setups across every branch, and a special release event can bring in a temporary kiosk that behaves differently from the store's usual one. If you're planning around a specific release, it's worth asking the store in advance rather than assuming the same payment flow that worked last time will work again.
