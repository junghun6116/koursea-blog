Gyeongju served as the ancient capital of the Silla Kingdom for nearly a millennium, leaving behind a remarkable concentration of historic sites and scenic landmarks. Whether you arrive by KTX for a day trip or stay for a weekend, planning around verified landmarks ensures a smooth itinerary. Below are 20 verified attractions across Gyeongju, organized by focus area to help you navigate efficiently.

## Historic Sites & Ancient Heritage

### [Cheomseongdae Observatory](https://www.koursea.com/place/2304/) (Google Rating: 4.5 / 20,541 reviews)

Cheomseongdae is a seventh-century stone astronomical observatory dating to the Silla period, recognized as one of the oldest surviving observatories in East Asia. Admission is free, and the site is open daily from 09:00 to 22:00.

### [Donggung Palace & Wolji Pond](https://www.koursea.com/place/2303/) (Google Rating: 4.5 / 20,673 reviews)

This historic site preserves the grounds and artificial pond of an ancient Silla secondary palace compound. It is widely visited in the evening for the illuminated views across the water.

### [Gyeongju National Museum](https://www.koursea.com/place/2344/) (Google Rating: 4.6 / 13,299 reviews)

This national institution houses extensive collections of Silla Kingdom cultural heritage, including gold crowns, historic pottery, and Buddhist artifacts. General admission to the permanent exhibition halls is free.

### [Woljeonggyo Bridge](https://www.koursea.com/place/2322/) (Google Rating: 4.6 / 8,775 reviews)

Woljeonggyo is a reconstructed Silla-era covered wooden bridge crossing the Namcheon stream. It offers free public access and connects directly toward historic walking paths.

### [Seokguram](https://www.koursea.com/place/2310/) (Google Rating: 4.4 / 7,923 reviews)

Located on the slopes of Mount Toham, Seokguram is an eighth-century Buddhist grotto hermitage housing a seated Buddha statue, listed as a UNESCO World Heritage site.

### [Daereungwon Tomb Complex](https://www.koursea.com/place/2294/) (Google Rating: 4.5 / 3,708 reviews)

This central park holds numerous large grassy burial mounds from the ancient Silla royal dynasty. The outdoor park grounds are free to walk through, while access inside the Cheonmachong tomb interior requires a separate paid ticket.

### [Gyeongju Gyochon Traditional Village](https://www.koursea.com/place/2320/) (Google Rating: 4.1 / 5,451 reviews)

A preserved heritage village area featuring traditional Korean hanok buildings located near Woljeonggyo Bridge. Visitors can walk through public alleys lined with heritage homes and traditional shops.

### [Wolseong Fortress](https://www.koursea.com/place/2417/) (Google Rating: 4.3 / 158 reviews)

The historic earthen ramparts of the primary royal palace fortress of the Silla Kingdom, situated on a low crescent-shaped hill in the central historic zone.

## Scenic Nature & Coastal Spots

### [Bomunho Lake Tourism Complex](https://www.koursea.com/place/2350/) (Google Rating: 4.3 / 2,671 reviews)

A developed resort zone centered around the large Bomun Reservoir, featuring wide walking pathways and seasonal lakeside scenery.

### [Gyeongju Yangnam Columnar Jointing (경주 양남 주상절리)](https://www.koursea.com/place/2340/) (Google Rating: 4.3 / 2,724 reviews)

A coastal geological formation located in Yangnam-myeon featuring distinct cooling fractures in volcanic rock, including a rare fan-shaped columnar formation along the shoreline.

### [Songdaemal Lighthouse](https://www.koursea.com/place/2309/) (Google Rating: 4.4 / 997 reviews)

A coastal lighthouse situated on the rocky headland of Gampo Port, known for panoramic views over the East Sea and surrounding pine groves.

### [Bomunjeong Pavilion](https://www.koursea.com/place/2349/) (Google Rating: 4.3 / 787 reviews)

A traditional wooden pavilion and small scenic pond located within the Bomun complex, commonly visited for photography and quiet resting along the trail.

### [Geumjangdae (금장대)](https://www.koursea.com/place/2324/) (Google Rating: 4.3 / 232 reviews)

A scenic cliffside pavilion overlooking a bend in the Hyeongsan River, offering an open vantage point over the water and surrounding hillsides.

### [Gyeongju Lotus Complex (경주연꽃단지)](https://www.koursea.com/place/2388/) (Google Rating: 4.5 / 86 reviews)

A public outdoor wetland field near the Eastern Historic Site zone featuring large lotus flower ponds along open walking paths.

## Modern Landmarks, Streets & Leisure

### [Hwangnidan-gil Street](https://www.koursea.com/place/2305/) (Google Rating: 4.2 / 7,779 reviews)

A vibrant pedestrian-friendly street lined with restored low-rise hanok buildings, modern cafes, boutique retail shops, and casual dining options.

### [Gyeongju World](https://www.koursea.com/place/2301/) (Google Rating: 4.5 / 11,527 reviews)

The primary amusement theme park in the Gyeongju resort area, offering roller coasters, family rides, and seasonal recreational facilities.

### [Memories of the Past Moon Village (추억의달동네)](https://www.koursea.com/place/2317/) (Google Rating: 4.0 / 3,064 reviews)

A retro-themed experiential park near Mount Toham recreating typical Korean urban alleyways, classrooms, and domestic life from the 1970s and 1980s.

### [Gyeongju Tower](https://www.koursea.com/place/2315/) (Google Rating: 4.3 / 2,704 reviews)

An 82-meter architectural landmark inside Gyeongju Expo Grand Park, designed with a hollow silhouette cutout of the historic nine-story Hwangnyongsa wooden pagoda.

### [Gyeongju Bird Park](https://www.koursea.com/place/2321/) (Google Rating: 4.3 / 2,620 reviews)

A bird-themed park with an indoor ecological exhibition hall and outdoor aviary areas, located in the Bomun tourism district.

### [RaWon](https://www.koursea.com/place/2432/) (Google Rating: 4.9 / 100 reviews)

A local establishment in Gyeongju holding a 4.9 rating across 100 verified visitor reviews.
