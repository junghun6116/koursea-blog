# Botox in Korea 2026: Cost, Districts & What to Expect

Botulinum toxin injections are among the most standardized aesthetic procedures sold by Korean clinics, but “Botox” is often used casually to describe several domestic and imported products. A low headline price therefore does not tell you the product, number of units, treatment area, injector, tax-inclusive total, or follow-up plan.

This guide gives foreign visitors a practical framework for comparing forehead and full-face quotes across Seoul and Busan without treating an advertisement as medical advice.

> **Medical disclaimer:** This article is for general planning and does not constitute medical advice. Botulinum toxin is a prescription treatment that should be administered by a licensed, appropriately trained healthcare professional after an individual consultation.

## Key Takeaways

- Compare the **same product, treatment area, and unit count**, not two differently labeled packages.
- Koursea's planning ranges run from approximately **₩15,000 to ₩120,000 for a forehead area**, depending on district, product tier, units, and promotion.
- “No mandatory downtime” does not mean no side effects, no aftercare, or automatic medical clearance to fly.
- Korea's tourist VAT refund for cosmetic procedures ended on **January 1, 2026**. Ask for the tax-inclusive total before booking.
- Contact the clinic through its official channel and confirm the product, units, injector, cancellation policy, and complication contact route in writing.

## Why Botox Can Be Easier to Compare Than Other Procedures

Unlike a vague “full-face lifting package,” a botulinum toxin quote can be broken into concrete variables: product name, units, injection area, and clinician fee. Forehead treatment is also a narrower comparison than a multi-zone full-face plan.

That does not make every injection equivalent. Facial anatomy, muscle strength, desired movement, product formulation, dilution, placement, and injector judgment all matter. Full-face contouring and jaw or masseter treatment should not be priced as though they were the same service as a small forehead zone.

## The Six Questions to Send Before Paying

Copy these questions into the clinic's official chat or email:

1. What is the exact botulinum toxin product and manufacturer?
2. How many units are included for my treatment area?
3. Will a licensed doctor assess me and perform the injections?
4. Does the quoted total include consultation, numbing if used, and 10% VAT?
5. Is a follow-up adjustment included, and within what time window?
6. Who should I contact after hours if I develop concerning symptoms?

For the tax question, show:

> **이 가격에 부가세 포함인가요?**  
> Is VAT included in this price?

If a clinic will not identify the product or units, a low package price is not meaningfully comparable.

## What the Appointment Usually Involves

A responsible appointment begins with a medical history and facial-movement assessment. Tell the clinician about pregnancy or breastfeeding, neuromuscular conditions, swallowing or breathing problems, allergies, current medicines, recent injections, and prior adverse reactions. Do not conceal medication use simply to keep an appointment.

The clinician marks the treatment points, cleans the skin, and administers small injections. The procedure itself can be brief, but consultation and waiting time vary. Request the product name and treatment record before leaving.

Clinical-study results for BOTOX Cosmetic forehead treatment were assessed at multiple time points, including days 7 and 14, rather than promising an immediate final result. Onset and duration vary by product, dose, area, and patient; effects are temporary and commonly last for months, not permanently.

## Downtime, Aftercare and Flying

Many patients return to ordinary low-intensity plans after treatment, but temporary injection-site pain, redness, swelling, bruising, headache, facial weakness, eyelid droop, or brow droop can occur. Follow the treating clinic's written instructions about rubbing or pressing the area, exercise, alcohol, makeup, lying down, and tight headwear rather than relying on a universal social-media rule.

## Red Flags That Need Prompt Medical Attention

The FDA notes that approved botulinum toxin products carry serious warnings and that unapproved or counterfeit products increase risk. Seek urgent medical care for trouble swallowing or breathing, generalized muscle weakness, double vision, speech difficulty, or other rapidly worsening symptoms. Do not board a flight or wait for a broker to answer if these symptoms occur.

Minor bruising is not the same as a systemic reaction, but only a healthcare professional can evaluate your specific symptoms. Save the clinic's direct number and know how to reach emergency care before treatment.

## VAT and Payment Reality in 2026

The temporary tourist refund for eligible Korean cosmetic medical services ended on January 1, 2026. Budget the full tax-inclusive total instead of assuming you can reclaim VAT at the airport. Retail tax-refund rules for eligible goods are separate and do not restore the clinic refund.

Read the [2026 cosmetic procedure tax-refund and budget guide](/posts/korea-cosmetic-procedure-tax-refund-ended-2026-budget-guide/) for the policy distinction and itemized-quote checklist.

Paying by card creates a clearer transaction record. If a clinic promotes a cash price, ask whether the same invoice, treatment record, and aftercare rights apply. Avoid third-party price brokers when the clinic cannot independently confirm the quoted product, units, and total.

## Which District Fits Your Trip?

### Gangnam

Gangnam offers the densest mix of high-volume and private consultation models. It works well when you want many quotes, but headline promotions create more comparison noise. Decide whether speed and price or doctor continuity matters more before booking.

### Myeongdong

Myeongdong is convenient for short-stay visitors and commonly offers English coordination. Confirm that the advertised price applies to the product and unit count you want rather than a first-visit teaser.

### Seomyeon and Centum, Busan

Both districts have established clinic clusters and can price routine forehead treatment competitively. English support is less consistently documented than in Seoul's main tourist clinic districts, so confirm that the consultation—not only the reservation chat—can be conducted in a language you understand.
