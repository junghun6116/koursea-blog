## Neighborhoods, Not a Single Address

### Hongdae
**Hongdae** is the natural fit for a NewJeans-adjacent day out: it's Seoul's long-running youth fashion and street-culture district, with the secondhand shops, indie fashion boutiques, and casual street-style energy that overlaps heavily with the group's visual identity. Treat it as a neighborhood to wander — vintage and streetwear shops, casual cafes, street performance areas — rather than hunting for one specific confirmed filming spot.

### Hannam-dong
**Hannam-dong** is the other natural stop, on the fashion-forward end of the spectrum — boutiques, concept stores, and the kind of minimalist cafe aesthetic that shows up across a lot of K-pop styling and lookbook content generally, NewJeans included. Like Hongdae, approach it as a district-level recommendation rather than a single confirmed address.
