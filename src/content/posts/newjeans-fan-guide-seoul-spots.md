## Confirmed 2022–2024 Activity

NewJeans debuted in 2022 with an EP led by “Attention” and “Hype Boy”; both songs became defining early releases, and “Attention” earned the group its first music-show win. The following single album paired “Ditto” with “OMG”. In 2023, the *Get Up* EP reached No. 1 on the Billboard 200. Confirmed 2024 activity includes “How Sweet”, the Japanese debut single “Supernatural”, and the sold-out Bunnies Camp fan meeting at Tokyo Dome.

Those releases are the boundary of this page's activity timeline. Travelers should not infer a current lineup, agency position or future tour from this historical summary.

## Neighborhoods, Not a Single Address

### Hongdae
**Hongdae** is the natural fit for a NewJeans-adjacent day out: it's Seoul's long-running youth fashion and street-culture district, with the secondhand shops, indie fashion boutiques, and casual street-style energy that overlaps heavily with the group's visual identity. Treat it as a neighborhood to wander — vintage and streetwear shops, casual cafes, street performance areas — rather than hunting for one specific confirmed filming spot.

### Hannam-dong
**Hannam-dong** is the other natural stop, on the fashion-forward end of the spectrum — boutiques, concept stores, and the kind of minimalist cafe aesthetic that shows up across a lot of K-pop styling and lookbook content generally, NewJeans included. Like Hongdae, approach it as a district-level recommendation rather than a single confirmed address.

### The Reality of Filming Spots and Pop-ups
Individual MV filming locations and brand pop-up stores are time-limited and change constantly — a location that hosted a pop-up last year may be an unrelated business today. Rather than list specific addresses that risk going stale (or being wrong), this guide points you to the right neighborhoods and recommends checking current fan-community posts and official brand-partner announcements close to your travel dates for anything time-sensitive.
