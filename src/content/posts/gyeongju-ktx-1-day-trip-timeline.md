# Gyeongju in One Day From Seoul: A Realistic KTX Timeline

Gyeongju is a genuine one-day trip from Seoul if you build your schedule around the actual transfer times instead of guessing. Here's a timeline that works.

## Getting there

KTX service between Seoul Station and Gyeongju Station runs frequently through the day — roughly every 30-40 minutes — with a one-way trip taking a little over two hours. Book ahead for weekend travel; popular departures sell out.

## The bus that actually matters

From Gyeongju Station, **Bus 50** runs every 15-20 minutes directly toward the Hwangridan-gil area and is the practical default. **Bus 700** exists but only runs a handful of scheduled departures a day — don't build your itinerary around waiting for it.

## A sample day

- **Morning departure from Seoul Station**, arriving in Gyeongju by mid-morning.
- **Bus 50 to Hwangridan-gil** — roughly a 20-25 minute ride.
- **Daereungwon and the historic tomb complex** — the park grounds are free to walk; the interior tomb exhibit (Cheonmachong) has a separate entrance fee.
- **Cheomseongdae and Gyochon Village** — a flat, walkable loop; bike rentals are available near Daereungwon if you'd rather cover ground faster.
- **Gyeongju National Museum** in the afternoon — free entry to the permanent Silla collection.
- **Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond** in the evening — the night illumination is worth timing your visit around sunset.
- **Return to Gyeongju Station** by taxi or bus, with a KTX back to Seoul that gets you home the same night.

## The one thing to build in slack for

Whatever your exact schedule, leave more buffer than feels necessary between your last Gyeongju activity and your return train — a delayed bus or a longer-than-expected walk back from Donggung Palace is the easiest way to miss a departure you can't easily rebook same-day.
