# ZEROBASEONE Fan Guide to Seoul: ZEROSE Concert & WAKEONE Planning

WAKEONE's [official ZEROBASEONE profile](https://wake-one.com/en/artists/zerobaseone/) identifies the group as a BOYS PLANET project under WAKEONE and lists its official discography. ZEROBASEONE's official Japan site identifies its fan club as ZEROSE JAPAN, reflecting the ZEROSE fandom name.

This page is a public-event planning guide. WAKEONE offices and production spaces are workplaces, not visitor attractions, and no office tour or artist access should be assumed.

## Key takeaways

- Use WAKEONE's artist page and ZEROBASEONE's current official channels.
- Verify the exact city and venue before using a regional fan-club or ticket notice.
- Treat past concerts and booths as history, not current access instructions.
- Prepare identification, membership, payment, and return transport early.

## What should ZEROSE verify before a Seoul visit?

Start with WAKEONE's [official artist page](https://wake-one.com/en/artists/zerobaseone/) for the group profile, releases, and official video links. Its current discography includes **Ascend**, **RE-FLOW**, and **NEVER SAY NEVER**. A release listing does not by itself confirm a public Seoul appearance, so look for a separate dated event notice before making a route.

The [ZEROBASEONE Japan official site](https://zerobaseone.jp/) publishes regional fan-club, ticket, schedule, and merchandise notices. Those pages are authoritative for the events they name, but a Japan notice should not be reused as a Seoul venue guide.

## Is the WAKEONE office a visitor stop?

No. A corporate address does not create public access. Do not wait at entrances, photograph employees, follow vehicles, or infer private schedules. If WAKEONE announces a public pop-up or exhibition, visit only the stated venue during the published dates.

A reliable Seoul route pairs one confirmed public activity with nearby parks, cafes, or an established music retailer. This keeps the day useful if admission is capped or merchandise sells out.

## Concert preparation for overseas ZEROSE

Use the seller linked by WAKEONE or the event organizer. Our [Foreigner's Guide to K-Pop Concert Ticketing in Seoul](/posts/kpop-concert-ticketing-seoul-2026/) covers passport-name matching, fan-club presales, overseas payments, pickup, and late-night transport.

Before booking:

- confirm the event title, city, venue, show date, and ticket-sale time;
- verify which regional ZEROSE membership, if any, the presale accepts;
- match the account name to the identification required at pickup or entry;
- save the venue address and final public-transport route offline;
- check the current light-stick, recording, bag, and merchandise rules.

## Official merchandise and fan etiquette

Buy through an official site or the retailer named in a current notice. Confirm the product version, preorder benefit, pickup location, and deadline. Do not assume that a past collaboration shop or event booth is permanent.

Use announced concerts, broadcasts, fan meetings, and public pop-ups as the boundary for fan travel. Offices, hotels, airports, vehicles, and unannounced movements are not appropriate sightseeing stops.
