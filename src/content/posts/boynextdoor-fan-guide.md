# BOYNEXTDOOR Fan Guide: ONEDOOR Activity & Seoul Concert Prep

BOYNEXTDOOR is managed by KOZ Entertainment, a HYBE-affiliated label, and the official fandom name is **ONEDOOR**. The most important travel fact is also the simplest: there is no verified public KOZ headquarters shop or dedicated BOYNEXTDOOR visitor facility in Seoul.

## Key Takeaways

- Do not build an itinerary around entering KOZ offices or HYBE headquarters.
- HYBE's Yongsan building is a workplace with no general fan access.
- BOYNEXTDOOR already held a Seoul concert run in 2026: three sold-out "KNOCK ON Vol.2" shows at [KSPO Dome](/posts/kspo-dome-concert-survival-guide/), July 17-19 — treat this as history, not a current on-sale.
- The mini album *19.99* and the *ONEDOORful Day* fan-meeting name are useful confirmed activity references, not current event listings.
- Buy through official online channels or a clearly announced temporary retailer event.
- Use the [Foreigner's Guide to K-Pop Concert Ticketing in Seoul](/posts/kpop-concert-ticketing-seoul-2026/) for future official shows.

## Why This Guide Has No Headquarters Route

An agency relationship does not turn an office into a tourism venue. KOZ does not operate a separately verified public shop or exhibition for walk-in visitors, and HYBE headquarters does not provide general interior access. Koursea therefore does not invent a place card or direct ONEDOOR to private entrances.

If you want a fan-focused stop, wait for a dated official pop-up, album benefit, fan meeting or concert announcement. Confirm its venue and operating period through KOZ, BOYNEXTDOOR, Weverse, the venue or the named ticket seller. Old event pages often remain searchable after closing.

## KNOCK ON Vol.2: A Real Tour, Already in the Past

BOYNEXTDOOR's first headlining world tour, **KNOCK ON Vol.2**, opened its domestic leg with three shows at **KSPO Dome in Seoul on July 17-19, 2026**. According to [allkpop's report on the Seoul run](https://www.allkpop.com/article/2026/06/boynextdoors-first-world-tour-knock-on-vol2-in-seoul-sells-out-all-three-shows), all three dates sold out during presale, and [NOL World's own ticketing page for the shows](https://world.nol.com/en/articles/503732d0-1cf7-4ed7-830f-3d9ff5ef1638) listed the general on-sale date as June 23, 2026.

From Seoul, the tour continued to a Japan leg (August-September 2026) and a North America leg, with BOYNEXTDOOR's own Weverse notice for the North American dates ([weverse.io/boynextdoor/notice/36556](https://weverse.io/boynextdoor/notice/36556)) opening ticket presale there starting June 17, with general on-sale from June 26. Reported North American tour-stop lists are not fully consistent across secondary outlets, so treat any specific non-Seoul city/date as unconfirmed until you check BOYNEXTDOOR's own Weverse page directly.

**Treat this as concert history, not a current sale.** If you're planning a trip around a *new* Seoul-area BOYNEXTDOOR date, see the next section — do not assume KNOCK ON Vol.2's July 2026 Seoul dates or pricing still apply.

If a new Seoul date is announced, our [KSPO Dome concert survival guide](/posts/kspo-dome-concert-survival-guide/) covers venue transit, bag rules and nearby food options for that exact arena, since KOZ/HYBE-affiliated acts have used it before.

The *19.99* mini album and *ONEDOORful Day* fan meeting are established parts of BOYNEXTDOOR's activity history. They can help travelers understand merchandise names and older fan content, but they should not be presented as a current Seoul schedule without a new official notice.
