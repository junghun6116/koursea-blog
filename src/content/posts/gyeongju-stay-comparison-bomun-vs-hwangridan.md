# Where to Stay in Gyeongju: Hwangridan-gil vs Bomun Complex

Gyeongju's two main lodging areas suit different trips. The right call comes down to whether you have a car and how much you want to walk versus drive between sights.

## Hwangridan-gil: the walkable choice

Hwangridan-gil sits close to Gyeongju KTX Station — a direct bus ride away — and puts you within walking distance of Cheomseongdae, Daereungwon, and Woljeonggyo. It also has a genuinely lively evening scene: bakeries, cafes, bars, and restaurants that stay open well into the night. This is the better base if you're relying on public transit and want to end the day somewhere with options, not just a hotel restaurant.

## Bomun Tourism Complex: the resort choice

Bomun is built around lakeside resort hotels — think larger international chains and family-oriented condos — but it sits further from Gyeongju Station, generally needing a bus transfer or taxi to get there, and a car or local bus to move between its own spread-out attractions. Evening dining options are mostly in-house at the hotels, with fewer independent restaurants nearby.

## The deciding factor

If you're traveling by KTX and public transit without a rental car, Hwangridan-gil is the more practical base almost every time — less transit friction, more to do within walking distance after dark. If you have a car and want a quieter, resort-style stay with lake views, Bomun is a reasonable trade-off, especially traveling with kids.
