# LE SSERAFIM Fan Guide to Seoul: FEARNOT Events, Pop-Ups & Concert Planning

LE SSERAFIM is a five-member SOURCE MUSIC group comprising Sakura, Kim Chaewon, Huh Yunjin, Kazuha, and Hong Eunchae, as listed by the group's [official profile](https://www.le-sserafim.jp/profile). The official fandom name is FEARNOT, confirmed in the [fandom-name announcement](https://weverse.io/lesserafim/notice/3267).

There is no permanent LE SSERAFIM visitor facility presented in these official sources. Build a fan itinerary around dated concerts, pop-ups, and authorized retail notices instead of assuming access to a company workplace.

## Key takeaways

- Check Weverse before traveling because pop-ups and fan sessions have fixed operating dates.
- A past Yongsan pop-up does not mean a permanent shop is open at that address.
- Membership presales require a separate application and may use region-specific ticket systems.
- Private schedules, airports, offices, and artist travel routes are not visitor attractions.

## How should FEARNOTs check a Seoul pop-up?

The [LE SSERAFIM 2026 S/S pop-up visit guide](https://weverse.io/lesserafim/notice/35772) named a temporary event at 42 Hangang-daero in Yongsan from May 22 to June 2, 2026. It used online reservations, entry QR verification, limited sessions, and event-specific purchase rules. Those dates have passed. The notice verifies how that event operated, not that a permanent public shop remains there.

For any new pop-up, confirm all of the following in the newest notice:

- operating dates, daily hours, and final entry;
- exact floor or event zone;
- reservation opening time and whether walk-in entry is allowed;
- the accepted QR screen and identity document;
- purchase limits, benefit eligibility, and sold-out updates.

The separate [FEARNOT TIME notice](https://weverse.io/lesserafim/notice/35824) shows why membership details matter: its first daily session was restricted to selected FEARNOT Membership holders and required both the winning screen and valid identification. Never assume a membership-only session permits general entry.

## Current-tour information needs a fresh notice

SOURCE MUSIC's [PUREFLOW Incheon announcement](https://weverse.io/lesserafim/notice/35577) confirmed two July 2026 shows at INSPIRE ARENA and stated that ticket details would follow separately. The group's [official Japan ticket notice](https://weverse.io/lesserafim/notice/35347) also demonstrates that dates, time zones, regional membership products, raffles, and ticket vendors can differ by market.

Always open the notice for the exact city you plan to attend. Our [Foreigner's Guide to K-Pop Concert Ticketing in Seoul](/posts/kpop-concert-ticketing-seoul-2026/) covers passport-name matching, membership presales, foreign payment fallback, pickup, and venue-day preparation.

## Fan etiquette is part of the itinerary

SOURCE MUSIC's [official fan etiquette notice](https://weverse.io/lesserafim/notice/2585) says not to visit locations or activities that are not listed on the official schedule or announced through Weverse. The [August 2026 additional guidelines](https://weverse.io/lesserafim/notice/38306) also prohibit forged or transferred identity documents, unauthorized recording in restricted areas, visits to unofficial schedules or private spaces, and dangerous approaches along artist routes.

Keep the trip to announced public events. Follow staff instructions, respect photography restrictions, and leave immediately when an event closes or a queue is capped.
