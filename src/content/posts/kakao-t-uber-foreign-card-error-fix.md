# Kakao T or Uber Payment Declined in Korea? Current Card Fixes

Your taxi request fails at the payment step, but the message does not explain whether the problem is the card, the app or the ride option. The safest fix is to check the payment method **before** requesting another car. Kakao T and Uber Taxi do not use identical payment screens, and paying twice is a bigger problem than a single failed authorization.

## Why it happens

Kakao Mobility's current customer-support page says overseas-issued **Visa, Mastercard, JCB and American Express** credit or debit cards can be registered for taxi, bike and pet services. That means a foreign card is not automatically ineligible.

A valid card can still fail if the issuer blocks overseas or online transactions, the saved card details are stale, the card is prepaid, or the authorization is temporarily rejected. Start with the exact network printed on the card and the decline notice in the app. If the network is supported, check the issuer's app or call the number on the card rather than repeatedly submitting the same request.

## Check the payment choice before calling

Before confirming a Kakao T taxi request, tap the payment-method area shown on the request screen and review the choices available for that vehicle type. If the screen offers direct or on-site payment, select it **before** calling the taxi. If it does not, do not assume the driver can override the app's payment rule.

Kakao Mobility also warns about double payment: when automatic payment was selected, paying again in the vehicle can create two charges. Keep the paper receipt or card approval record if that happens, then use the trip record in Kakao T to file a double-payment inquiry.

## Uber Taxi: choose in-person payment in the app

Uber's current Seoul taxi page says riders can select an **in-person payment** option and then pay by cash or card at the end of the trip. It also supports adding a card to the Uber account. Availability can depend on the ride option shown in the app, so make the selection before confirming the request.

Do not hand over cash merely because a driver asks when the trip is set to automatic card payment. Uber's rider help says cash should only be paid when that payment option was selected in the app.

## A five-minute recovery sequence

1. Check whether the card is Visa, Mastercard, JCB or American Express for Kakao T.
2. Confirm overseas and online transactions are enabled with the issuer.
3. Remove an expired or duplicated saved card and add the intended card again.
4. Return to the request screen and inspect the payment choice before booking.
5. If available, choose on-site payment and keep a physical card plus some cash as backup.
6. Take a screenshot of the decline and retain any vehicle receipt if a second charge appears.

## If you don't have the language to explain the problem

Keep this ready to show the driver on your phone screen if the vehicle reader rejects a card: **"카드 결제가 안 됩니다. 다른 카드나 현금으로 결제해도 될까요?"** ("The card payment is not working. May I pay with another card or cash?"). This is for an on-site-payment trip; it is not permission to pay again when the app already charged you.

## Do not transfer one app's rule to the other

The two apps can both connect you with licensed taxis, but their payment choices must be read from the specific booking screen. Do not treat a workaround shown for one app, city or vehicle category as a universal rule.
