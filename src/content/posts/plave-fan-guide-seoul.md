# PLAVE Fan Guide to Seoul: PLLI, VLAST & the KEEP IT MANIC World Tour

**VLAST** is the agency behind PLAVE, a five-member **virtual** idol group, and **PLLI** is the group's official fandom name. Don't expect a meet-and-greet spot or a physical storefront here — in the Seoul area, PLAVE's "in-person" presence is basically its concerts, staged through real-time motion-capture avatars instead of the members walking out on stage themselves. That's why this guide takes a different shape from Koursea's other K-pop fan guides: it's built around attending a concert, not finding a meetup spot.

## Key Takeaways

- PLAVE's official fandom name is **PLLI**; the group is signed to **VLAST**, a Korean studio built around real-time virtual-avatar technology.
- PLAVE concerts are large-scale arena and stadium shows — not small AR pop-ups — performed with real-time avatar technology rather than in-person stage appearances, and often simulcast to fans who can't attend.
- PLAVE's most recent Seoul-area dates were **September 12–13, 2026**, opening its **"KEEP IT MANIC" World Tour** at **Incheon Munhak Stadium** (about 40–50 minutes from central Seoul by public transit).
- Treat that tour opener as recent history, not a standing or recurring event — confirm any future Seoul-area date only from VLAST's or PLAVE's own current notice.
- Use the [Foreigner's Guide to K-Pop Concert Ticketing in Seoul](/posts/kpop-concert-ticketing-seoul-2026/) before any future presale.

## A Different Kind of "Fan Visit"

Since what audiences see on stage is an animated avatar driven by a performer working off-stage, there's no dressing-room door or agency storefront serving as a fan landmark the way KWANGYA@SEOUL does for SM's artists. VLAST is first and foremost a technology and production company, and its offices aren't set up to receive visiting fans. Whatever PLAVE experience is available to you as a traveler will come down to a concert, a livestream-viewing event, or pop-up merchandise tied to one specific tour stop — not a fixed place to hang around.

## KEEP IT MANIC: Recent Context, Not a Current Event

PLAVE's first *world* tour, **"KEEP IT MANIC,"** opened in 2026 with two shows at **Incheon Munhak Stadium** on **September 12 and 13**, confirmed on [PLAVE's official Weverse tour notice](https://weverse.io/plave/notice/37847). That notice lists later stops in Kanagawa, Kaohsiung, Bangkok, Singapore, Taipei, Macau and Pasadena, so check the current notice rather than treating the Incheon dates as an active sale.

The Incheon dates sold through [NOL Ticket's official listing for the show](https://nol.yanolja.com/ticket/products/26008189). The listing confirms the venue, dates and PLLI membership presale requirements, and now marks the event as closed. Those rules applied only to the Incheon show and should not be assumed for a future date.

Whenever you're reading this, September 2026 is already in the past — worth knowing for how PLAVE structures a tour and prices its tickets, but not evidence that a new Seoul-area date is on sale right now. Any older tour poster or ticket page deserves the same treatment this site gives other groups' past eras: background, not a live schedule.

## Concert Preparation

Should a new PLAVE date near Seoul get announced, treat it like any other in-demand K-pop show: find out which platform is running sales (VLAST's 2026 Incheon dates went through NOL Ticket), check whether the presale needs an official fan-club or membership account, and make sure your ticketing account name matches your passport wherever identity verification applies. A foreign-issued card can still get rejected mid-checkout on Korean ticket platforms, so line up a backup payment method beforehand.
