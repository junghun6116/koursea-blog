Located just south of Seoul in Gyeonggi Province, Yongin is a prime destination for expansive suburban cafes. Blessed with surrounding mountains, hidden mountain valleys in Goki-ri, and peaceful reservoirs, the city's coffee scene specializes in destination cafes featuring architectural grandeur, expansive lawns, and in-house artisanal bakeries.

Here are 11 verified destination cafes across Yongin, organized by location and atmosphere.

## Architectural Flagships & Sprawling Garden Bakeries

### 1. [A Loaf Slice Piece (어 로프 슬라이스 피스)](https://www.koursea.com/place/3122/)

Yurim-dong, Cheoin-gu. 4.0/5.0 (3,346 reviews). A sprawling culinary village of red-brick buildings, landscaped flower gardens, a baking laboratory, and a cooking-class pavilion. Best known for its "Ttwiwmtteul-ppang" — a tall, gymnastics-vaulting-horse-shaped brioche pastry filled with custard cream — alongside a "Batjul-ppang" (rope bread) and artisanal sourdough loaves.

### 2. [Calliope (칼리오페)](https://www.koursea.com/place/3110/)

Yeokbuk-dong, Cheoin-gu. 4.1/5.0 (1,741 reviews). An immense hillside cultural complex spanning roughly 12,000 pyeong (about 39,600 square meters), built entirely around Greek mythology to match its namesake muse. A central plaza recalls Rome's Colosseum, flanked by statues of Calliope and Apollo, floor-to-ceiling glass walls, and an underground "Gaia Garden" with a painted sky backdrop that's become a popular photo spot.

### 3. [Terreno cité (떼레노시떼)](https://www.koursea.com/place/3148/)

Gomae-dong, Giheung-gu, near Giheung IC. 4.4/5.0 (1,248 reviews). Split between an original café hall and a modern specialty roastery annex, with tall gabled glass walls opening onto surrounding forest greenery and multi-level outdoor terrace seating. Known for single-origin pour-over coffee roasted in-house and signature Einspänner cream coffee.

### 4. [Monpere Bakery (몽뻬르베이커리카페)](https://www.koursea.com/place/3182/)

Mohyeon-eup, Cheoin-gu. 4.3/5.0 (539 reviews). A European-lodge-style bakery tucked into a quiet woodland valley, with stone fireplaces, wood-paneled interiors, and outdoor patio seating under mountain trees. Known for natural yeast-fermented baguettes, butter croissants, and seasonal fruit tarts baked fresh daily.

## Lake Vistas & Nature Hideaways

### 5. [Lee & Hoy's Coffee (리엔호이스)](https://www.koursea.com/place/3158/)

Idong-eup, Cheoin-gu. 4.0/5.0 (910 reviews). A three-story landmark directly on the banks of Idong Reservoir, Yongin's largest freshwater lake, with floor-to-ceiling windows and an open rooftop terrace overlooking the water.

### 6. [Lamir (라미르)](https://www.koursea.com/place/3167/)

Wonsam-myeon, Cheoin-gu. 4.4/5.0 (415 reviews). Set along the walking trail of Yongdam Reservoir, with wide lake views from outdoor lawn seating and upper-floor terraces. Known for gourmet salt bread and fruit-topped pastries paired with cold brew.

### 7. [코페아 COFFEA](https://www.koursea.com/place/3185/)

Namsa-eup, Cheoin-gu. 4.4/5.0 (537 reviews). A quiet countryside roastery set among farming pastures, aimed at coffee purists seeking single-origin filter coffee and daily house-baked goods away from crowds.

## Valley Waters & Minimalist Retreats

### 8. [Mukri 459 (묵리459)](https://www.koursea.com/place/3184/)

Idong-eup, Cheoin-gu. 4.0/5.0 (709 reviews). A minimalist café built around a distinctive dark, curved facade, with an inward-facing gallery framing views of the surrounding forest. Known for a signature black sesame cream latte and seasonal brunch plates.

### 9. [Gomae Coffee (고매커피)](https://www.koursea.com/place/3176/)

Gomae-dong, Giheung-gu. 4.1/5.0 (700 reviews). A restored traditional hanok home centered on a gravel courtyard with an old tree at its center, where guests can sit along the wooden veranda (maru). Known for a black-sesame cream coffee and traditional Korean rice cake desserts.

### 10. [Terrace 478 (테라스478)](https://www.koursea.com/place/3166/)

Gogi-dong, Suji-gu, in the Goki-ri Valley. 4.1/5.0 (380 reviews). Built down a steep embankment into the valley, with outdoor daybeds and parasol tables set right beside the flowing mountain stream. Known for hearty brunch plates, eggs Benedict, and citrus sparkling coolers.

### 11. [Mosobamboo Cafe (모소밤부 카페)](https://www.koursea.com/place/3163/)

Gogi-dong, Suji-gu, in the Goki-ri Valley. 4.1/5.0 (362 reviews). A bamboo-garden café beside the same mountain stream, with large glass doors opening onto a grove of bamboo along the riverbed. Known for a signature bamboo Einspänner and savory brunch pasta sets.
