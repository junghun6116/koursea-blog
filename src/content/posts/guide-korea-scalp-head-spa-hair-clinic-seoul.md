## Why Everyone's ASMR Feed Is Suddenly a Korean Scalp Treatment
If your social feeds have shown you someone lying face-up beneath a gentle waterfall raining from a circular halo ring over their scalp, you've encountered Korea's viral head spa. While the aesthetics make for viral ASMR clips, the treatment itself is rooted in a decades-old Korean scalp-care routine (*dupi scaling*, 두피 스케일링) designed to treat the scalp with the same multi-step rigor as Korean facial skincare.

## The Viral Phenomenon vs. Medical Scalp Care
Before booking, understand which service fits your condition:

- **Aesthetic Head Spas (두피 스케일링 / 헤드스파):** Relaxation and deep-cleansing services offered at premium hair salons and scalp wellness studios. They feature high-magnification camera analysis, exfoliating scrubs, lymphatic massage, and the signature waterfall ring. These are non-medical, preventive treatments open to anyone without a doctor's referral.
- **Medical Dermatology Scalp Clinics:** Clinical practices overseen by board-certified dermatologists. They treat diagnosed pathologies—such as severe seborrheic dermatitis, alopecia areata, or acute scalp psoriasis—using prescription-grade topicals, mesotherapy injections, and medical devices. If you experience burning pain, sudden patch hair loss, or open lesions, visit a dermatology clinic rather than a head spa.

**Session Duration:** A comprehensive program typically runs **60 to 90 minutes** (with ultra-luxury programs extending up to 120 minutes). Services advertised under 45 minutes are typically express wash-and-massage add-ons.

## Step-by-Step: Inside the "15 Steps"
While exact protocols vary by salon, a standard 15-step scalp ritual generally proceeds through this sequence:

1. **Initial Consultation:** Discussing scalp sensitivity, oiliness, and recent color/chemical history.
2. **Micro-Camera Scalp Diagnosis ("Before"):** High-magnification lens inspection projecting clogged follicular sebum, erythema (redness), or dead skin buildup onto a monitor.
3. **Aromatherapy Breathing Ritual:** Deep inhalation of essential oils to ease autonomic nervous tension.
4. **Pre-Cleansing Scalp Scaling:** Applying botanical scaling oils or BHA solutions to soften stubborn sebum plugs.
5. **Steam Pod / Therapeutic Mist:** Warm ultrasonic mist softens keratin and opens pores for deeper penetration.
6. **Customized Exfoliating Scrub:** Sea-salt or AHA/BHA-based scalp scrub worked through parted hair sections.
7. **Galvanic / Micro-Current Brush Therapy:** Stimulating blood circulation and cellular turnover across the scalp.
8. **Meridian Acupressure Massage:** Targeted pressure-point release along the temples, occipital ridge, and neck.
9. **Deep Cleansing Shampoo:** Double wash using pH-balanced, sulfate-free scalp shampoos.
10. **The Signature Halo Waterfall Ring:** A warm, multi-stream circular water fountain continuous-flow rinse over the hairline and crown, creating sensory decompression.
11. **Scalp Tonic & Ampoule Infusion:** Targeted serum application (biotin, peptide, or cica complexes) directly onto parted roots.
12. **Absorption Tool (Y-Stick / Iontophoresis):** Handheld metal conductor optimizing nutrient absorption into follicular roots.
13. **LED Light Therapy Dome:** Red light (collagen and circulation support) or blue light (antimicrobial calming) treatment.
14. **Micro-Camera Re-examination ("After"):** Clear visual confirmation of cleared follicular funnels and calmed scalp tissue.
15. **Leave-in Nourishing Mist & Rough Dry:** Application of heat protectant and basic drying.

## Booking Friction & Critical Travel Tips

### 1. Book 2–4 Weeks in Advance
Due to high international demand, weekend and peak afternoon slots sell out weeks ahead. Foreign travelers can book via **Naver Booking** (if using a card supported without domestic phone verification), direct salon **Instagram DMs**, or booking concierge platforms.

### 2. Rough-Dry vs. Full Salon Blowout
Almost all standard head spa packages include a **rough dry** (finger-drying until hair is damp-dry or fully dry). A professional round-brush blowout, curling, or flat-iron styling usually incurs an optional add-on fee of ₩20,000–₩40,000. Confirm styling options upon arrival if you have dinner plans immediately following your appointment.

### 3. Do NOT Book Directly After Fresh Hair Dye
Intense scalp scaling, clarifying shampoos, and prolonged warm-water rinsing will strip newly deposited pigments. If you plan to dye your hair in Korea, complete your scalp spa **before** coloring, or allow a buffer of at least 7 to 10 days post-color.
