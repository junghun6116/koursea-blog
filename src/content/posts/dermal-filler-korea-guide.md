# Dermal Filler in Korea 2026: Domestic vs Imported Cost

The first question that explains most dermal filler price differences in Korea is not “Gangnam or Busan?” It is **which exact product, how many milliliters, and which treatment area?** Domestic Korean fillers and imported products such as Juvéderm or Restylane can sit in different price tiers, but brand alone does not predict the best outcome for an individual face.

This guide helps foreign visitors compare itemized quotes, plan realistic recovery time, and recognize the safety information that matters more than a headline discount.

> **Medical disclaimer:** This article is for informational and itinerary-planning purposes only. Dermal filler injection is a medical procedure with rare but potentially serious complications. Consult a licensed, appropriately trained healthcare professional for an individual assessment and follow that clinician's aftercare and travel advice.

## Key Takeaways

- Compare the same **brand, product line, treatment area, and 1 ml syringe count**.
- Domestic-brand planning ranges vary by district, approximately **₩130,000–₩350,000 per 1 ml** in Koursea's current records.
- Imported-brand planning ranges commonly sit around **₩350,000–₩500,000+ per 1 ml**.
- Bruising and swelling may take several days to about two weeks to settle.
- Koursea uses one full day as a minimum travel-planning buffer—not as universal medical clearance to fly.
- Korea's tourist VAT refund for cosmetic procedures ended on January 1, 2026, so confirm the tax-inclusive total before paying.

## What Actually Determines Filler Price?

“Filler” is not a single interchangeable product. A useful quote identifies:

1. The exact manufacturer and product line.
2. Whether it is a domestic Korean or imported product.
3. The volume in milliliters and number of sealed syringes.
4. The injection area and intended result.
5. The clinician performing the procedure.
6. Whether cannula, numbing, consultation, follow-up, and VAT are included.

An imported brand is not automatically better for every patient. Product characteristics, approved indication, anatomy, injector experience, and treatment goal all matter. Avoid claims that one country of manufacture guarantees a longer duration or superior result without product-specific evidence and a medical assessment.

## The Dose-Basis Trap

A “1 ml syringe” quote and a “full-face package” are not comparable. The package may contain two or three syringes, multiple treatment areas, a different product tier, or additional procedures. Ask the clinic to break the offer into product name, milliliters, areas, and included services.

Use this message:

> **필러 제품명과 총 용량이 몇 cc인지, 부가세 포함 총액이 얼마인지 알려주세요.**  
> Please tell me the filler product name, total volume in cc, and the final VAT-inclusive price.

One cubic centimeter and one milliliter are equivalent units of volume, but confirm that the clinic is describing the contents of a sealed syringe rather than a loosely named package.

## Authenticity and Provider Checks

Choose a licensed medical provider trained in facial anatomy and filler complications. Ask to see the unopened, labeled product before injection and keep a record of the product name and lot or batch information when available. Larger premises are not automatically safer, and a polished foreign-language booking page is not proof of clinician qualifications.

The U.S. FDA advises patients to discuss the product, injection site, risks, and patient labeling with a licensed healthcare provider. Approval status varies by country and indication, so a product's U.S. status should not be presented as a substitute for checking Korean authorization and the intended use with the treating clinician.

## Downtime: What “One to Two Weeks” Means

Common temporary reactions include swelling, bruising, redness, pain, tenderness, itching, and localized bumps. Some people look socially presentable sooner, while lips or highly mobile areas may remain visibly swollen longer. Plan important photographs and events after the likely settling period rather than assuming a next-day final result.

Do not schedule dental work, another facial procedure, or intense pressure on the treated area without asking the injector. Follow the clinic's specific instructions about exercise, alcohol, makeup, massage, heat, and skincare.

## Flying After Filler

There is no single aviation rule establishing that every filler patient becomes safe to fly after exactly 24 hours. Koursea uses **one full day as a minimum logistics buffer** because it gives you time to monitor early swelling and remain close to the treating clinic. Your clinician may recommend a longer interval based on the injection site, product, symptoms, medical history, and flight duration.

Rare vascular complications can be time-sensitive. For that reason, heading directly from the treatment chair to an airport is a poor contingency plan even if cabin pressure itself is not prohibited. Save the clinic's urgent number, understand its after-hours process, and know where to seek emergency care.

## Red Flags: Seek Immediate Medical Attention

The FDA identifies unintended injection into a blood vessel as the most concerning filler risk. Although uncommon, it can interrupt blood supply and lead to tissue death, visual impairment, blindness, or stroke.

Seek urgent medical attention for:

- Unusual, severe, or rapidly escalating pain.
- White, gray, blue, mottled, or rapidly darkening skin near the treated area.
- Blurred or lost vision, double vision, or eye pain.
- Sudden weakness, numbness, facial droop, confusion, speech difficulty, or severe headache.
- Breathing difficulty, widespread hives, or signs of a severe allergic reaction.
- Fever, pus, spreading redness, or rapidly worsening swelling.

Do not wait for a booking broker or social-media account to answer if these symptoms occur.

## VAT and Payment in 2026

Ask:

> **이 가격에 부가세 포함인가요?**  
> Is VAT included in this price?

Korea's temporary cosmetic-procedure VAT refund for eligible foreign visitors ended on January 1, 2026. Retail tax-refund programs for eligible goods are separate and do not make clinic services refundable. Budget the full tax-inclusive amount and read the [2026 cosmetic procedure tax-refund guide](/posts/korea-cosmetic-procedure-tax-refund-ended-2026-budget-guide/) before comparing prices.

Use the clinic's official channel, not a price-quoting broker, and obtain the full total in writing. Card payment provides a clearer transaction record; make sure you receive an itemized receipt and treatment information regardless of payment method.

## Choosing Gangnam, Myeongdong, Seomyeon, or Centum

### Gangnam

Gangnam offers the widest mix of high-volume and private consultation models. It is useful for comparing product tiers, but aggressive promotions can make unlike-for-like quotes look deceptively similar.

### Myeongdong

Myeongdong is convenient for short-stay visitors and commonly offers English coordination. Confirm that the consultation and complication instructions—not only the booking chat—are available in a language you understand.

### Seomyeon and Centum, Busan

Both areas have established clinics and competitive domestic-tier prices. English support is less uniformly documented, so confirm it directly. A slightly cheaper injection may not be better value if follow-up communication is difficult.
