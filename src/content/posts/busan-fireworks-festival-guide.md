# Busan Fireworks Festival 2026: A Foreigner's Guide

The **21st Busan Fireworks Festival** is scheduled for **Saturday, November 7, 2026**, centered on Gwangalli Beach with viewing areas extending toward Haeundae and Igidae. It is a single-night city festival built around Gwangan Bridge—not the smaller weekly Gwangalli M Drone Light Show.

The date and paid-seat sale are official. The detailed 2026 running order, final show time, road closures and station crowd-control plan can still change, so recheck the festival website and Busan City notices during the week of the event.

## Where to Watch: Free vs. Paid

### Gwangalli Beach

Gwangalli provides the central, front-facing view of Gwangan Bridge and the festival's coordinated display. It also carries the heaviest pedestrian demand. If you want to be near the main production, arrive in the afternoon, follow marked viewing zones and expect long periods without an easy exit.

Do not treat “arrive at 2–3 PM” as a guarantee of a particular spot. Safety barriers, paid-seat zones and crowd capacity determine where visitors can sit. Keep emergency lanes and marked passages clear.

### Dongbaekseom and Haeundae-side viewpoints

The official festival organization lists the Haeundae area as part of the wider viewing footprint. The display is farther away than at central Gwangalli, but the angle can include the bridge and bay. Paths, coastal edges and access points may be controlled when capacity rises, so follow the current event map rather than an old photo guide.

### Igidae

Igidae offers a more distant view across the water. It is not a simple substitute for the beach: access involves bus connections or additional walking, terrain is less forgiving after dark, and authorities may restrict crowded or unsafe sections. Wear stable shoes, bring a light and confirm the designated 2026 viewing and exit route before committing to this area.

### Paid seats

Busan City says 2026 R seats cost **₩100,000** and S seats cost **₩70,000**. Official regular sales opened at 2:00 PM on September 4 through NOL Ticket and the Busan Bank mobile app; S seats also have a Busan Bank branch option. Use only the organizer's linked seller, confirm the purchaser-name and pickup rules, and avoid unprotected resale transfers.

Yacht and hotel packages are private products, not official substitutes for a festival ticket. Confirm the actual view, cancellation policy, boarding point and weather terms directly with the operator.

## Getting There Without Losing the Evening

Use **Busan Metro Line 2** and expect a walk. Gwangan Station and Geumnyeonsan Station are the usual approaches to Gwangalli Beach. Official Busan directions identify Geumnyeonsan exits 3 or 5 as roughly a five-minute walk and Gwangan exits 3 or 5 as roughly a ten-minute walk under ordinary conditions; festival barriers can change the final path.

Do not drive toward the beach. Previous festivals used phased road closures around Gwangalli, Millak, Marine City, Igidae and Gwangan Bridge. The 2026 control map should be treated as authoritative only after Busan City publishes it.

After the show, avoid committing to a single station entrance. Staff may meter access or direct people toward another route. If your schedule allows, wait in an open café or other legitimate indoor business until the first surge eases—but verify its hours and make a purchase rather than occupying space without permission.

A standard T-money card used in Seoul also works on Busan's metro and city buses. ## Cold, Connectivity and Crowd Safety

- **Dress for a windy waterfront.** November air beside the sea can feel much colder than the forecast. Pack an insulating layer, wind-resistant outerwear and hand warmers.
- **Bring a charged power bank.** Dense crowds can slow mobile data and drain batteries as phones repeatedly search for signal.
- **Choose a physical meeting point in advance.** Save a numbered exit, public landmark and backup time. “Meet near the beach” is not specific enough.
- **Leave large luggage elsewhere.** Lockers near the event can fill early, and carrying a suitcase through dense pedestrian controls is unsafe. Use your hotel or a staffed storage option before approaching Gwangalli.
- **Save information offline.** Screenshot your hotel address in Korean, transit route, ticket and official event map.
- **Keep the exit route open.** Do not sit in emergency lanes, stairways or marked pedestrian corridors, even if others do.

## Turn It Into a Full Busan Day

Avoid crossing the entire city immediately before the event. A coastal day around Haeundae and Gwangalli reduces transport risk; use our [Busan Haeundae and Gwangalli itinerary](/itinerary/busan-haeundae-gwangalli-coastal-itinerary/) as the planning base.

If you want Gamcheon Culture Village or Jagalchi Market, visit early and leave a large buffer before moving east. The [Busan subway, food and Gamcheon guide](/posts/guide-busan-subway-qr-pass-food-gamcheon-haeundae/) explains the practical route tradeoffs. Do not schedule a tight timed attraction immediately before reaching the beach.
